# Character Creation

The standard 5.2 character creation process is modified in the following ways to make it easier for new players to follow, as well as allowed experienced players more dynamic choices.

## Heroic Ability Generation

New PCs generate their ability scores by rolling what is known as an "array" from dice. The procedure to generate an array is as follows:

1. Roll 8d6, keep the highest 3. Record the sum on a sticky note.
2. Then roll 6d6, 5d6, 4d6, 3d6, and 3d6. Keep the highest 3 each time. Record each sum on the stick note. You should have 6 random ability scores between 3 and 18 now.
3. If you do not have at least one score that is a 14 or higher, you can re-roll your entire array.
4. Given the 6 scores you generated, assign them to the 6 abilities in any way you desire.

## Backgrounds

Your background can be anything you want. Think "former pirate", "former priest", or something along those lines. You can be as vague or specific as you desire! The background does not have strict mechanics. Instead, it may provide you with knowledge of things within the campaign, or grant you advantage in certain situations that the GM decides it applies.
