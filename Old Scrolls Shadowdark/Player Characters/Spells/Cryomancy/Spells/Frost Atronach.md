# Frost Atronach

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Cryomancy

You conjure an elemental being made out of pure frost to fight with you. The creature acts on its own and has the following statistics. The attacks it makes are frost based magic attacks with a range of 5 feet. When they deal damage, the target's speed is halved for the next round.

When you lose focus, the creature explodes in a ball of frost dealing 1d6 cold damage to anything within 5 feet of it.
