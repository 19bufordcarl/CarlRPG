# Ice Disk

**Duration:** 5 rounds per mana point

**Range:** 30 feet

**School:** Cryomancy

You create a floating, circular disk of magical ice with a concave center. It can carry up to 100 pounds per mana point spent. It hovers at waist level and automatically stays within 30 feet of you. It can't cross over drops or pits taller than 5 feet.
