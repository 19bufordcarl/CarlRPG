# Blizzard

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** Self

**School:** Cryomancy

You create a storm of freezing ice within range. The area of the storm can be up to a 30 foot diameter sphere. Each creature other than you in the area takes 1d6 cold damage per mana point and has their speed halved for 3 rounds. Whenever you succeed on your focus check, you may deal that damage again to any creatures still in the area. Any creatures less than your level are frozen solid until the spell ends.
