# Frost Rune

**Duration:** Until rest

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Cryomancy

You create an invisible magical symbol on a surface, container, or door. Touching the rune will trigger it and freeze the victim. They take 1d6 cold damage each round for 1 round per mana point. The rune cannot be disabled easily once created, it requires separate magic to dispel it.
