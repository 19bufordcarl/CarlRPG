# Resilient Ice Sphere

**Duration:** 3 rounds per mana point

**Range:** Self

**School:** Cryomancy

You create a lightweight but strong sphere of translucent magical ice around you with a 5 foot radius.

For the spell's duration, if the sphere is attacked, treat it as having 50 HP. Afterwards, it becomes normal, brittle, mundane ice; treat it as having 5 HP.
