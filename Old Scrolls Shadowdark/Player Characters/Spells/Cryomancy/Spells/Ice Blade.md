# Ice Blade

**Duration:** 4 rounds per mana point

**Range:** Self

**School:** Cryomancy

You conjure a weapon of your choice formed from magical ice in your hand. It strikes with a +1 bonus and deals 1d8 damage. At 4 mana points, raise this to a +2 bonus and 2d8 damage. At 7 mana points, raise this to a +3 bonus and 3d8 damage.
