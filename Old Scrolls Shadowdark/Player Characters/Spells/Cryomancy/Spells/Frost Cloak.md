# Frost Cloak

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** Self

**School:** Cryomancy

You become cloaked in a magical freezing mist affecting anyone who gets within 5 feet of you. Whenever a creature starts their turn, ends their turn, or enters the affected area, they take cold damage equal to the mana points spent to cast this spell and has their speed halved for the next round. A creature can only take this damage once per turn.

While protected by this spell, you take half damage from fire and cold. If this would reduce the damage taken to less than 5, you take no damage.
