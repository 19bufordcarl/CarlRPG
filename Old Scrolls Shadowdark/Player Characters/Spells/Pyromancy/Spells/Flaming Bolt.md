# Flaming Bolt

**Duration:** Instant

**Range:** 120 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You hurl a flaming magical bolt towards a single target. It must have a clear path of travel with no obstructions. It deals 1d6 fire damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 7d6 damage.
