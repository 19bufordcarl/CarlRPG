# Wall of Flames

**Duration:** 3 rounds

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You create a magical wall of fire that extends up to 30 feet in length and 5 feet thick. It deals 1d6 fire damage to anyone or anything trying to pass through it.

Unintelligent animals will not attempt to pass through the wall.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage and lasts 5 rounds.

At **Max** power, this spell does 5d6 damage and lasts 10 rounds.
