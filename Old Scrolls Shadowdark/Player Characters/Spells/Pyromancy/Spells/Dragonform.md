# Dragonform

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** Self

**School:** Pyromancy

You become wreathed in flames, growing flaming wings that grant you the ability to fly up to 60 feet per turn. You also can breath fire as an action, dealing 1d6 fire damage to all creatures in a 30 foot cone.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 6d6 damage.
