# Flames

**Duration:** Instant

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You conjure a cloud of scorching ash and flame from your fingertips onto a single target. They take 1d6 fire damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 4d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 8d6 damage.
