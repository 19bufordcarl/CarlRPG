# Flaming Sphere

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** 30 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You create a floating ball of fire with a 5 foot diameter within range. When you cast this spell and whenever you succeed on a focus check for this spell, you may move the ball up to 30 feet. If it enters the space of a creature while moving, that creature takes 1d6 fire damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 2d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 4d6 damage.
