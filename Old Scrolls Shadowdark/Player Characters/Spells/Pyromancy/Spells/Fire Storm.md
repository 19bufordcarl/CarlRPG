# Fire Storm

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** 120 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You create a storm of fire within range. The area of the storm can be up to a 30 foot diameter sphere. Each creature in the area takes 1d6 fire damage. Whenever you succeed on your focus check, you may move the fire storm to anywhere you can see within range and deal that damage again to any creatures in the new area of the storm.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 5d6 damage.
