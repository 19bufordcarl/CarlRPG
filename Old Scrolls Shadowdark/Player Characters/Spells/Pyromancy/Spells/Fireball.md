# Fireball

**Duration:** Instant

**Range:** 120 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You hurl a small flame that erupts into a fiery blast. All creatures in a 15 foot radius around where the flame lands take 1d6 damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 5d6 damage.
