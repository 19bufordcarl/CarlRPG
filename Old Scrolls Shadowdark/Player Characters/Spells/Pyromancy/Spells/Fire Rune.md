# Fire Rune

**Duration:** Until rest

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You create an invisible magical symbol on a surface, container, or door. Touching the rune will trigger it and douse the victim in fire. They take 1d6 fire damage each round for 3 rounds. The rune cannot be disabled easily once created, it requires separate magic to dispel it.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 2d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 4d6 damage.
