# Flame Cloak

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** Self

**School:** Pyromancy

You become cloaked in raging flames that hurt anyone who gets within 5 feet of you. Whenever a creature starts their turn, ends their turn, or enters the affected area, they take 1 fire damage. A creature can only take this damage once per turn.

While protected by this spell, you take half damage from fire and cold. If this would reduce the damage taken to less than 2, you take no damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 1d6 damage and you negate up to 5 damage instead of 2.

At **Max** power, this spell does 2d6 damage and you negate up to 10 damage instead of 2.
