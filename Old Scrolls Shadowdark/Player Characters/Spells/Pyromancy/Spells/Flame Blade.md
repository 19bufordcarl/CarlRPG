# Flame Blade

**Duration:** 10 rounds

**Range:** Self

**School:** Pyromancy

You conjure a flaming weapon of your choice in your hand. It strikes with a +1 bonus and deals 1d8 damage.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, the weapon has a +2 bonus and does 2d8 damage.

At **Max** power, the weapon has a +3 bonus and does 3d8 damage.
