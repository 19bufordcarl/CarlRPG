# Flame Atronach

**Duration:** Focus

**Range:** 5 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You conjure an elemental being made out of pure flames to fight with you. The creature acts on its own and has the following statistics. The attacks it makes are fire based magic attacks with a range of 60 feet.

When you lose focus, the creature explodes in a ball of flames dealing 1d6 fire damage to anything within 5 feet of it.
