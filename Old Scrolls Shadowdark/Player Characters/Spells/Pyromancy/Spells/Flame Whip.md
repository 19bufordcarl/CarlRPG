# Flame Whip

**Duration:** Instant

**Range:** 30 feet

**School:** Pyromancy

You create a flame-cloaked whip that lashes out at your command toward a creature in range. They take 1d6 fire damage and if the creature is not too big you can move it up to 10 feet in any direction.

***Higher Power Casting***

At **High** power, this spell does 3d6 damage.

At **Max** power, this spell does 5d6 damage.
