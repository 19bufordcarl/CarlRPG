# Spellcasting

When you cast a spell, you invoke magic to cause an effect. Casting a spell takes your action. Characters with the spellcasting talent can cast spells.

To cast a spell you know, make a spellcasting check by rolling 1d20 + your spellcasting ability modifier. Choose a spell power level. Unless you have a class talent specifying otherwise, you can only cast spells at normal power or lower.

| Spell Power | Casting DC |                Effect                 |
| :---------: | :--------: | :-----------------------------------: |
|     Low     |     9      | Must spend 2 rounds casting the spell |
|   Normal    |     12     |                 None                  |
|    High     |     15     |        The spell gains a bonus        |
|     Max     |     18     |        The spell gains a bonus        |

If you succeed on your spellcasting check, the spell takes effect.

If you fail your spellcasting check, the spell does not take effect. The mana point is still spent.

If you cast a spell that has a range greater than 5 feet while within 5 feet of another creature you have disadvantage on the spellcasting check.

## Critical Success

If you roll a natural 20 on your spellcasting check, you may double one of the spell's numerical effects. This remains in effect on a focus spell until your next focus check.

## Critical Failure

If you roll a natural 1 on your spellcasting check, the spell does not take effect. If it was a focus spell, the spell immediately ends.

## Cantrips

Cantrips are simple spells that can always be cast without a spellcasting check.
