# Necromancer

Manipulators of unlife and undeath.

**HP:** Starts at 4 + Constitution. Then 1d4 + Constitution per level.

**Spellcasting.** You can cast Necromancy spells you know. Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability when you become an Necromancer. Intelligence gives +1 spells known. Wisdom gives +1 to spellcasting checks. Charisma gives +2 mana points.

**Mana Points.** You have mana points as listed in the Necromancy spell table. Once per day, you can regain half your maximum mana points (round down) after resting for 1 round. You regain all mana points when you complete a long rest.
