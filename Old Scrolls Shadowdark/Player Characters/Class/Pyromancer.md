# Pyromancer

Experts of wielding magical flames.

**HP:** Starts at 4 + Constitution. Then 1d4 + Constitution per level.

**Spellcasting.** You can cast Pyromancy spells you know. Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability when you become a Pyromancer. Intelligence gives +1 spells known. Wisdom gives +1 to spellcasting checks. Charisma gives +1 mana points. Whenever you fail to cast one of your known Pyromancy spells, you lose 1 mana point.

**Mana Points.** You have mana points as listed in the Pyromancy spell table. Once per day, you can regain 1 mana point after resting for 1 round. You regain all mana points when you complete a long rest.

**Burning.** When you cast a Pyromancy spell against a target capable of being on fire, it becomes smoldering. If on a following turn the smoldering target touches a flame, either from a spell or otherwise, they are set on fire and take 1d4 fire damage at the end of their turn until they douse the flames.

### Cantrips

The list of cantrips to choose from is [Firecraft](../Spells/Pyromancy/Cantrips/Firecraft.md), [Dancing Flames](../Spells/Pyromancy/Cantrips/Dancing%20Flames.md), and [Tiny Flames](../Spells/Pyromancy/Cantrips/Tiny%20Flames.md).

## Blaster

You gain the following abilities:

- When you cast [Flaming Bolt](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flaming%20Bolt.md) you may instead summon multiple bolts equal to the number of d6s that you would roll. If you do, each bolt only does 1d6 damage to its target. You may select the same target multiple times.
- When you cast [Flames](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flames.md), you critically succeed if your check is 20 or greater after your bonuses.

## Slasher

You gain the following abilities:
 
- When you cast [Flame Blade](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flame%20Blade.md), you can attack with it immediately after you summon it on the turn you summon it.
- While you have [Flame Blade](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flame%20Blade.md) or [Flame Cloak](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flame%20Cloak.md) active, you can choose to automatically succeed your spellcasting check when you cast the other one. You must still succeed on focus checks for [Flame Cloak](../Spells/Pyromancy/Spells/Flame%20Cloak.md).
