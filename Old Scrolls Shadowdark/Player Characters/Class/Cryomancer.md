# Cryomancer

Conjurers of ice and frost.

**HP:** Starts at 4 + Constitution. Then 1d4 + Constitution per level.

**Spellcasting.** You can cast Cryomancy spells you know. Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability when you become a Cryomancer. Intelligence gives +1 spells known. Wisdom gives +1 to spellcasting checks. Charisma gives +2 mana points.

**Mana Points.** You have mana points as listed in the Cryomancy spell table. Once per day, you can regain half your maximum mana points (round down) after resting for 1 round. You regain all mana points when you complete a long rest.

### Cantrips

The list of cantrips to choose from is [Tiny Frost](../Spells/Cryomancy/Cantrips/Tiny%20Frost.md) and [Shape Water](../Spells/Cryomancy/Cantrips/Shape%20Water.md).

## Bulwark

You gain the following abilities:

- You can cast [Frost Armor](../Spells/Cryomancy/Spells/Frost%20Armor.md) on other creatures too.
- When you cast [Ice Blade](../Spells/Cryomancy/Spells/Ice%20Blade.md), it also creates a shield that you can wield granting you +2 AC.

## Freezer

You gain the following abilities:

- Whenever you deal cold damage, the target becomes chilled.
- Whenever you deal cold damage to a chilled target, it must make a DC 15 Constitution check or become frozen.
