# Weapons

**Grip.** Determines whether 1 or 2 hands are required to wield this weapon.

**Strength.** Indicates how much Strength you need to wield this weapon effectively. If you do not have this much strength, all attacks are made at disadvantage. If the weapon is one-handed, you can wield with two hands to halve the Strength requirement.

**Range.** Indicates the maximum range in feet at which the weapon can be used effectively.

**Damage.** Indicates how much damage the weapon inflicts on a hit.

**Finesse (F).** You may use your STR or DEX when attacking with this weapon.

**Loading (L).** You must forgo moving to reload this weapon.

**Thrown (Th).** You may throw this weapon to make a ranged attack with it using STR or DEX.

**Versatile (V).** You can use this weapon with one or two hands. Use the higher damage die if you're wielding it with two.

**Blunt (B).** This weapon is primarily used to bludgeon.

**Slash (S).** This weapon is primarily used to slash.

**Pierce (P).** This weapon is primarily used to pierce.

**Weapon types.** Melee weapons (M) strike at arm's reach, and ranged weapons (R) strike at a distance.
