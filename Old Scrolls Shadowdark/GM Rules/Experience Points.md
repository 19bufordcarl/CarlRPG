# Experience Points

I use milestone XP. At the end of each session, the GM awards each PC a number of XP points between 1-10 depending on the events that occurred that session. When PCs reach 10 XP, they can level up reset their XP to 0 the next time they have a period of a week or more in a safe place with a tutor or other way to gain more power.

As the party reaches higher levels, gaining XP will require more heroic tasks, and potentially even the acquiring of magical items or tutoring from ancient wizards to unlock the highest levels of power. The exact nature will vary in any campaign, but expect gaining levels to require a justification in the fiction of the world. Until that requirement is met, as determined by the GM, a PC will wait at 10 XP being "ready" to level up when the moment presents itself.
