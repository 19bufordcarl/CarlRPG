# Economy

The silver coin is the benchmark for the economy of the Old Scrolls. One silver piece is the rough equivalent of one day of standard "peasant" labor. This fantast world is one with lots of economic and power inequality. The most expensive items will often be quite expensive compared to this figure.

## Encumbrance

As a related note, encumbrance need not be tracked super strictly at the slot granularity. Instead, expect to work reasonably with the GM to determine if a PC is carrying too much for the narrative at hand. On average, somewhere between 50 and 100 coins weigh 1 pound. For important magical items, a physical handout, like an index card, might be used.
