# Cunning Action

_Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature, Level 3+)_

Your impressive agility in combat allows you to move and act quickly. On your turn, you can take one of the following actions as a Bonus Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

Also as a Bonus Action, you can do one of the following:

**Sleight of Hand.** Make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools or to pick a pocket.

**Use an Object.** Take the Utilize action, or take the Magic action to use a magic item that requires that action.
