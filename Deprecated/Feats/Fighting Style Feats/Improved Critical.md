# Improved Critical

_Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature, Level 3+)_

Your attack rolls with weapons and Unarmed Strikes can score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

At level 11, this expands to a roll of 18–20 on the d20.

At level 20, this expands to a roll of 17–20 on the d20.
