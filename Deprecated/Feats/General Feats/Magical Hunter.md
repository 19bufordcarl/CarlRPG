# Magical Hunter

_General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)_

**Ability Score Increase**. Increase one ability score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**One with the Hunt.** You always have the _Hunter's Mark_ spell prepared. You can cast it a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a Long Rest.

Once you reach level 13, you cannot lose concentration on your _Hunter's Mark_ spells.

Once you reach level 17, you have on Advantage on attack rolls against the creature currently marked by your _Hunter's Mark_ spell.

Once you reach level 20, the damage die of your _Hunter's Mark_ is a d12.
