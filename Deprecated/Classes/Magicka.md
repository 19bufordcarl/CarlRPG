## Becoming a Magicka …

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in the Core Magicka Traits table.
- Gain the Magicka's level 1 features, which are listed in the Magicka Features table.
- There are no Magicka subclasses. You get an expanded spell list instead.

### As a Multiclass Character

- Gain the Hit Point Die from the Core Magicka Traits table.
- Gain the Magicka's level 1 features, which are listed in the Magicka Features table.
- You only gain spell slot multiclass benefits if you multiclass this class with a Hybrid. Treat each level in this class as two levels towards your Hybrid spell slot progression chart.

### Level 1: Spellcasting

Through your exceptional Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma, you have learned how to harness the power of Magicka to shape and cast spells. See "Spells" for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Magicka spells, which appear in the Magicka spell list later in the class's description.

**Cantrips.** You know three cantrips of your choice from the Magicka spell list.

Whenever you gain a Magicka level, you can replace one of your cantrips from this feature with another cantrip of your choice from the Magicka spell list.

When you reach Magicka levels 4, 8, and 12, you learn another cantrip of your choice from the Magicka spell list, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Magicka Features table.

**Spell Slots.** The Magicka Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain any expended slots that correspond to the value of a Mana Potion you consume when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+.** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose four level 1 spells from the Magicka spell list.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Magicka levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Magicka Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional spells from the Magicka spell list until the number of spells on your list matches the number on the table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 3 Magicka, your list of prepared spells can include six spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.

If another Magicka feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Magicka spells for you.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you finish a Long Rest with sufficient downtime in a safe location, you can replace any spells on your list with other Magicka spells for which you have spell slots if the GM agrees that you have sufficient resources to learn the new spells.

**Spellcasting Ability.** When you first gain a level in this class, you must choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma to be your spellcasting ability for your Magicka spells.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use any Focus as a Spellcasting Focus for your Magicka spells.

### Level 1: Ritual Caster

You can cast any spell as a Ritual if that spell has the Ritual tag and you have the spell prepared.

### Level 3: Magicka Subclass

You gain a Magicka subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Magicka levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Magicka level or lower.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Magicka levels 8, 12, and 16.

### Level 18: Spell Mastery

You have achieved such mastery over certain spells that you can cast them at will. Choose a level 1 and a level 2 spell that you know that have a casting time of an action. You always have those spells prepared, and you can cast them at their lowest level without expending a spell slot. To cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot.

### Level 19: Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

### Level 20: Signature Spells

Choose two level 3 spells that you know to become your signature spells. You always have these spells prepared, and you can cast each of them at will at level 3 without expending a spell slot. To cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot.
