## Becoming a Hybrid …

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in the Core Hybrid Traits table.
- Gain the Hybrid's level 1 features, which are listed in the Hybrid Features table.
- There are no Hybrid subclasses. You get an expanded spell list instead.

### As a Multiclass Character

- Gain the Hit Point Die from the Core Hybrid Traits table.
- Gain the Hybrid's level 1 features, which are listed in the Hybrid Features table.
- You only gain spell slot multiclass benefits if you multiclass this class with a Magicka. Treat each level in this class as two levels towards your Hybrid spell slot progression chart.

### Level 1: Spellcasting

Via a method of your choosing Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma, you have learned how to harness the power of Hybrid to shape and cast spells. See "Spells" for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Hybrid spells, which appear in the Hybrid spell list later in the class's description.

**Cantrips.** You know one Hybrid cantrip of your choice. Whenever you gain a Hybrid level, you can replace one of your cantrips from this feature with another Hybrid cantrip of your choice.

When you reach Hybrid levels 4 and 12 you learn another Hybrid cantrip of your choice, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Hybrid Features table.

**Spell Slots.** The Hybrid Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a Long Rest. You regain all expended slots when you finish a Short or Long Rest according to the Mana Potion consumed.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+.** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose four level 1 Hybrid spells.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Hybrid levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Hybrid Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Hybrid spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Hybrid Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 3 Hybrid, your list of prepared spells can include six Hybrid spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.

If another Hybrid feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Hybrid spells for you.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you finish a Long Rest with sufficient downtime in a safe location, you can replace any spells on your list with other Hybrid spells for which you have spell slots if the GM agrees that you have sufficient resources to learn the new spells.

**Spellcasting Ability.** When you first gain a level in this class, you must choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma to be your spellcasting ability for your Hybrid spells.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use an Arcane Focus as a Spellcasting Focus for your Hybrid spells.

### Level 1: Ritual Caster

You can cast any spell as a Ritual if that spell has the Ritual tag and you have the spell prepared.

### Level 1: Weapon Mastery

Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Longbows and Shortswords.

Whenever you gain a Hybrid level, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Scimitars and Longswords.

### Level 1: Hybrid Armor Training

You have trained your ability to fight proficiently in armor without in hindering you. You gain the following armor trainings once you reach the appropriate Strength score:

| Strength Score | Armor Training |
| -------------- | -------------- |
| 9              | Light Armor    |
| 11             | Shields        |
| 13             | Medium Armor   |
| 15             | Heavy Armor    |

### Level 2: Fighting Style

You have honed your martial prowess and gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice (see "Feats").

Whenever you gain a Hybrid level, you can replace the feat you chose with a different Fighting Style feat.

Alternatively, you can choose another Origin feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Hybrid levels 6 and 14.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Hybrid levels 6, 8, 12, 16, and 18.

### Level 5: Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn using weapon(s) for which you have Weapon Mastery.

### Level 19: Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

### Level 20: Master Jack

Choose any single class ability from any popularly published 5e compatible system.
