## Becoming a Hero …

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in the Core Hero Traits table.
- Gain the Hero's level 1 features, which are listed in the Hero Features table.
- There are no Hero subclasses. You get additional Feats instead.

### As a Multiclass Character

- Gain the following traits from the Core Hero Traits table: Hit Point Die, Weapon Proficiencies, and Hero Armor Training.
- Gain the Hero's level 1 features, which are listed in the Hero Features table. Treat this class as if it were a "Fighter" for multiclassing.

### Level 1: Fighting Style

You have honed your martial prowess and gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice (see "Feats").

Whenever you gain a Hero level, you can replace the feat you chose with a different Fighting Style feat.

Alternatively, you can choose another Origin feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Hero levels 3, 7, 11, and 15.

### Level 1: Weapon Mastery

Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of three kinds of Simple or Martial weapons of your choice. Whenever you gain a Hero level, you can practice weapon drills and change one of those weapon choices.

When you reach certain Hero levels, you gain the ability to use the mastery properties of more kinds of weapons, as shown in the Weapon Mastery column of the Hero Features table.

### Level 1: Hero Armor Training

You have trained your ability to fight proficiently in armor without in hindering you. You gain the following armor trainings once you reach the appropriate Strength score:

| Strength Score | Armor Training |
| -------------- | -------------- |
| 9              | Light Armor    |
| 11             | Shields        |
| 13             | Medium Armor   |
| 15             | Heavy Armor    |

### Level 2: Action Surge

You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action, except the Magic action.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Starting at level 17, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Hero levels 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, and 20.

### Level 5: Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn using weapon(s) for which you have Weapon Mastery. This feature increases to three total attacks at level 11 and four total attacks at level 20.

### Level 19: Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.
