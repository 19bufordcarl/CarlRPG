# Sorcerer

As a sorcerer, you are a living mote of arcane power. Your physical form is a direct tap into the ebb and flow of magic, known as the Arcanum. With each beat of your heart, the Arcanum resonates and shudders. As you gain power, you adapt your pitiable, fragile mortal form to channel the power that flows through it.

As a sorcerer, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Sorcerer Features table.

## Level 1: Sorcery

You have developed a spark of magic that allows you to channel raw, magical energy to suit your needs.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Constitution is the spellcasting ability for sorcerer features. If you make an attack roll with a sorcerer spell or feature, your bonus is your Constitution modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Spell Power.** When you use a sorcerer feature, its effects might be determined by your power. Many features have effects that scale, such as damage, based on your power.

**Sorcery Points.** Your magic is flexible and improvisational. Where a wizard learns rote formulas for their spells, you channel magic into whatever effect you need. You gain sorcery points as indicated in the Sorcerer Features table. You can spend a number of points per turn up to your power.

You regain up to a number of sorcery points equal to your power when you finish a short rest, and all expended sorcery points when you finish a long rest.

**Arcane Bolt.** As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against a target within 10 areas. On a hit, this attack does 1d10 + your Constitution modifier force damage. The damage increases to 2d10 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d10 if your power is 5 or greater.

Before rolling your attack you can expend a sorcery point to increase the damage by 1d10 per point spent, and the target takes half damage on a miss.

# Sorcerous Origins

Here are four sorcerous origins to choose from. Each origin gives you a set of core benefits, along with expanded benefits as your level increases.

## Pyromancy

You are attuned to the power of raw fire, a destructive force that burns away your enemies. Fire is impossible to control. When you use your magic, you merely direct the power you tap into and are sometimes as surprised at the effects as others.

**Unstable Flame.** When rolling fire damage, if you roll the maximum possible result on a die you may roll an additional die of that type as additional fire damage. This benefit applies to the additional die you roll.

**Flickering Flame.** As a magic action, you can cause fire to erupt from one of your hands. This fire sheds bright light in your area and dim light up to 1 area away. As a utilize action, you can touch a flammable object in melee range and cause it to ignite. You can choose to douse your hand on your turn without using an action.

**Gout of Flame.** As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against up to three targets in your area. On a hit, this attack does 1d6 + your Constitution modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 2d6 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d6 if your power is 5 or greater.

You can expend sorcery points to increase the attacks' damage by 2d6 per point spent, and a target takes half damage on a miss. Spend these points before rolling your attacks.

**Power 2: Cinder Cloud.** If your power is 2 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more sorcery points to cause a great gout of smoke and cinders to erupt from your hands. Pick an area within 10 areas. It fills with smoke that heavily obscures the area for one minute per sorcery point spent or until you use this feature again. At the end of each of your turns while this effect lasts, creatures in the smoke-filled area take 1d6 fire damage per sorcery point spent on this effect.

**Unfettered Arcana – Raging Flame.** When you gain the Unfettered Arcana class feature, you gain the following benefit.

On your turn, you can choose to channel a raging flame. For the next minute, when you roll fire damage you gain the benefits of your unstable flame feature when you roll the maximum value or one less than the maximum value on a die.

Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Power 3: Fiery Eruption.** If your power is 3 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more sorcery points to hurl a burning ember that detonates in an area. Pick an area within 10 areas. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage per sorcery point spent on this effect or half that much on a successful save.

Until the end of your next turn, creatures that end their turn in the target area take 2 fire damage per sorcery point spent on this effect.

**Power 4: Conjure Fire Fiend.** If your power is 4 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 4 sorcery points to conjure a vicious fire elemental, a fire fiend, to serve you. It lasts for up to 10 minutes, disappearing when it is reduced to 0 hit points.

The fire fiend is size Medium and appears in your area. It has AC 16 and hit points equal to your power times 5. If the fire fiend needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

The fire fiend uses your initiative and takes its turn after yours. It can use a melee attack with an attack bonus equal to 2 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier that does 2d6 + your Constitution bonus fire damage.

**Power 5: Fiery Form.** If your power is 5 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 5 sorcery points to transform into a fire elemental for 1 minute and gain the following benefits. You are immune to fire damage. You can move through spaces as narrow as one inch. Once per turn when a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 5d6 fire damage. Your Unstable Flame class feature now adds two additional dice when it triggers.

# Sorcery Feats

Here are the core Sorcery feats you can choose from.

## Arcane Aegis

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

Once per turn when you take damage, you can spend 2 sorcery points and gain resistance to one damage type of your choice until the end of that turn. That resistance applies to the damage that triggered the use of this feat, if it is the chosen type.

## Arcane Eyes

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

Magical auras appear as shimmering lights to you, allowing you to detect magical objects or effects by sight. However, you cannot perceive the aura of an object that is hidden or invisible.

As a magic action, you can expend a sorcery point and pick an area within 10 areas. For the next minute, while your eyes are closed you can see as if you were standing in that area.

## Chromatic Soul

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

You are infused with the many colors of illusion magic, allowing you to sculpt images as you wish. You gain the minor illusion and light wizard cantrips. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for them.

## Divine Sorcery

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

Your arcane origin is intertwined with the power of the gods. You gain a cleric cantrip of your choice. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for it. As a magic action, you can expend sorcery points and allow a creature in melee range to regain 2d8 hit points per point spent + your Constitution modifier.

## Fade From Sight

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

As a magic action, you can expend 2 sorcery points and turn invisible for 1 minute. If you use an attack or magic action, you become visible at the end of the turn unless you spend 1 sorcery point to remain invisible.

## Fearsome Gaze

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

As a magic influence action, you can expend a sorcery point to strike fear into creatures near you. You can force up to 1 + your power creatures to make a Wisdom save. If they fail, they are frightened of you for the next minute.

## Fey Step

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

Once on your turn, you can spend 2 sorcery points and teleport up to 1 area. You do not use your action or your movement to use this feat.

## Linguistic Arcana

**Sorcery Feat (Prerequisite: Sorcerous Technique Feature)**

As a magic action, you can expend a sorcery point and pick a creature within 10 areas. For the next 10 minutes, you and the chosen creature can converse as if you shared a language. You add your power as a bonus to all Charisma checks made to interact with the chosen creature using the influence action.
