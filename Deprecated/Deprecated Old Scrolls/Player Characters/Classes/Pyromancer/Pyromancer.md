## Becoming a Pyromancer …

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in the Core Pyromancer Traits table.
- Gain the Pyromancer's level 1 features, which are listed in the Pyromancer Features table.

### As a Multiclass Character

- Gain the Hit Point Die from the Core Pyromancer Traits table.
- Gain the Pyromancer's level 1 features, which are listed in the Pyromancer Features table. See the multiclassing rules in "Character Creation" to determine your available spell slots. You may choose whichever armor class formula grants you the highest armor class.

### Level 1: Spellcasting

You have learned to cast spells through your arcane arts. See [Spell Casting](../../../Spell%20Casting.md) for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Pyromancer spells, which appear in the Pyromancer spell list later in the class's description.

**Cantrips.** You know a number of Pyromancer cantrips as determined by the table above. You may select these cantrips however you want. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one of your cantrips from this feature with another Pyromancer cantrip of your choice.

Whenever you gain a Pyromancer level, you can replace one of your cantrips with another cantrip of your choice from the Pyromancer spell list.

**Spell Slots.** The Pyromancer Spell Slots table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells and what kind they are. You regain all expended quick spell slots when you finish a Short or Long Rest. You regain all expended slow spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+.** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature based on your level. Choose spells from the [Pyromancer Spell List](Pyromancer%20Spell%20List.md).

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Pyromancer levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Pyromancer Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional spells from the Pyromancer spell list until the number of spells on your list matches the number on the table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

If another Pyromancer feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Pyromancer spells for you.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you gain a Pyromancer level, you can replace one spell on your list with another Pyromancer spell for which you have spell slots.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Your chosen primary ability is your spellcasting ability for your Pyromancer spells.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use any Spellcasting Focus for your Pyromancer spells.

### Level 3: Pyromancer Subclass

You gain a Pyromancer subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Pyromancer levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Pyromancer level or lower.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at the levels denoted on the Pyromancer features table.

### Level 19: Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

### Level 20: Signature Spell

Work with your DM to create a brand new spell unique to your character!
