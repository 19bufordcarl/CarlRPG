# Flaming Touch

**1st-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Touch

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous

Make a melee spell attack against a creature you can reach. On a hit, the target takes 6d6 fire damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
