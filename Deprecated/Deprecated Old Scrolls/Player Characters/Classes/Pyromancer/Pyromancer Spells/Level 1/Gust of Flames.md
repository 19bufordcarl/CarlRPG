# Gust of Flames

**1st-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self (60-foot line)

**Components**: V, S, M (a bit of ash)

**Duration**: Instantaneous

A Line of strong winds carrying fire 60 feet long and 10 feet wide blasts from you in a direction you choose for the duration. Each creature in the Line must make a Strength saving throw.

On a failed save, the target takes 2d6 fire damage is pushed 15 feet away from you in a direction following the Line. On a successful save, the target takes half damage and is not pushed.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The Fire damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
