# Flame Cloak

**1st-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S, M (a shard of red glass)

**Duration**: 1 minute

Protective magical flames surrounds you. While protected in this way, whenever a creature is within 5 feet of you, you may use your Reaction to deal 2d6 Fire damage to it.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The Fire damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
