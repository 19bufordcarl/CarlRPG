# Flaming Bolt

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 60 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous

You launch a mote of flame at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Fire damage, and until the end of your next turn, it is slightly on fire. It emits Dim Light in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from the Invisible condition. The fire is not severe enough to do additional damage.

***Cantrip Upgrade.***

The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
