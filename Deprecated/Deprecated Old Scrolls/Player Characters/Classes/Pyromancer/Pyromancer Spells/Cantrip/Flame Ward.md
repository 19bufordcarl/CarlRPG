# Flame Ward

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: 1 minute (concentration)

You cloak yourself in distracting flames, whenever a creature makes an attack roll against you before the spell ends, the attacker subtracts 1d4 from the attack roll.
