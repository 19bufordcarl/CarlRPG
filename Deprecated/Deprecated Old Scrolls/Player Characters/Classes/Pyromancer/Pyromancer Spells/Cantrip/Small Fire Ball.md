# Small Fire Ball

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 60 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous

You hurl a small ball of fire at a point within range, where it explodes in a 5-foot-radius Sphere. Each creature in that Sphere must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Fire damage.

***Cantrip Upgrade.***

The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
