# Dancing Flames

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 120 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a bit of phosphorus)

**Duration**: 1 minute (concentration)

You create up to four torch-size harmless motes of flame within range that hover for the duration. Alternatively, you combine the four lights into one large glowing mote of flame. Whichever form you choose, each mote sheds Dim Light in a 10-foot radius.

Once on your turn you can move the motes up to 60 feet to a space within range. A mote must be within 20 feet of another mote created by this spell, and a mote vanishes if it exceeds the spell's range.
