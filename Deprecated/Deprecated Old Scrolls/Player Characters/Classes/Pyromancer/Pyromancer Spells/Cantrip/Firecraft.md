# Firecraft

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 30 feet

**Components**: V

**Duration**: Until dispelled

You manifest a minor wonder with fire within range. You create one of the effects below within range. You may cast this spell multiple times without dispelling prior effects.

**Wreathed in Flames.** You create a fire that surrounds your character in a visual manor of your choosing. This staves off the effects of cold weather for you and those near you. The magic flames are hot enough to be warm, but not hot enough to burn. They can be snuffed out by water, extreme blizzards, or similar events. You reduce any Fire or Cold damage you take by 1.

**Fire Augmentation.** You cause flames to flicker, brighten, dim, change color, or take a shape of your choosing. The effect cannot exceed the size of a 5 foot Cube.

**Fire Starter.** You instantaneously light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a small campfire.

*You may ask the GM if you can gain advantage on d20 tests by using this spell if it makes sense in the situation.*
