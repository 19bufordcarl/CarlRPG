# Flames

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 5 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous

Burning flames erupt from you in a 5-foot Emanation. Each creature of your choice that you can see in it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Fire damage.

***Cantrip Upgrade.***

The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
