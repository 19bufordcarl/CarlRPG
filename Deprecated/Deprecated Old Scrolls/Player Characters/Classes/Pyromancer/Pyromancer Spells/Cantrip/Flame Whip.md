# Flame Whip

**Pyromancy Cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 30 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a blackened whip)

**Duration**: Instantaneous

You create a flame-cloaked whip that lashes out at your command toward a creature in range. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 Fire damage, and if it is Large or smaller, you can pull it up to 10 feet closer to you.

***Cantrip Upgrade.***

The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
