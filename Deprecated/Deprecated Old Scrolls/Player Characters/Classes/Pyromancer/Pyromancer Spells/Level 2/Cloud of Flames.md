# Cloud of Flames

**2nd-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 60 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a sliver of volcanic glass)

**Duration**: 1 minute (concentration)

You conjure raging flames in a 5-foot Cube centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area takes 3d6 Fire damage. A creature also takes this damage if it enters the Cube or ends its turn there or if the Cube moves into its space. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

On your later turns, you can take a Magic action to teleport the Cube up to 30 feet.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
