# Dragon's Fire Breath

**2nd-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Touch

**Components**: V, S, M (a hot pepper)

**Duration**: 1 minute (concentration)

You touch one willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target can take a Magic action to exhale a 15-foot Cone. Each creature in that area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 fire damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

If you cast this spell on yourself, you may exhale fire on the turn you cast it without expending an action.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
