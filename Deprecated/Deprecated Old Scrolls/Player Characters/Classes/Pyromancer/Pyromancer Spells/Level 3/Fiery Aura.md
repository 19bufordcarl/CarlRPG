# Fiery Aura

**3rd-level Conjuration**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S, M (a volcanic rock)

**Duration**: 10 minutes (concentration)

Protective flames rage around you in a 15-foot Emanation for the duration.

All creatures other than you in the Emanation have their Speed halved, and whenever the Emanation enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, the creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 5d6 Fire damage. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
