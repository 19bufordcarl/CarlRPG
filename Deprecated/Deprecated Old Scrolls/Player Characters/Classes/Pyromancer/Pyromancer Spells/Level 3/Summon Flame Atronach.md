# Summon Flame Atronach

**3rd-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 90 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a ruby soul gem worth 300+ GP)

**Duration**: 1 hour (concentration)

You create a golem of Flames. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range and uses the Flame Atronach stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 Hit Points or when the spell ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the creature shares your Initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.

## Flame Atronach

**AC:** 12 + Spell Level
**HP:** 10 x Spell Level
**Attack Bonus:** Your Spell Attack Bonus
**Speed:** 30ft., Fly 30ft.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The atronach makes a number of Bolt or Slash attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down)

- **Bolt.** *Ranged Attack Roll:* Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 120 ft. *Hit:* 2d6 + 3 + Spell Level Fire Damage
- **Slash.** *Melee Attack Roll:* Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3d6 + 3 + Spell Level Fire Damage

**Fireball.** The atronach casts the spell Fireball, but it deals 4d6 + 3 + Spell Level Fire damage instead of 8d6 Fire damage. The atronach can only take this action once per hour.
