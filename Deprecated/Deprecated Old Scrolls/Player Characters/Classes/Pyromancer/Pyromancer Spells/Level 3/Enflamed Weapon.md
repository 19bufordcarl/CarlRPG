# Enflamed Weapon

**3rd-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Touch

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: 1 hour (concentration)

A weapon you touch becomes encased in magical flames. For the duration, the weapon has a +1 bonus to attack rolls and deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage of the chosen type when it hits.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

If you use a level 5-6 spell slot, the bonus to attack rolls increases to +2, and the extra damage increases to 2d6. If you use a level 7+ spell slot, the bonus increases to +3, and the extra damage increases to 3d6.
