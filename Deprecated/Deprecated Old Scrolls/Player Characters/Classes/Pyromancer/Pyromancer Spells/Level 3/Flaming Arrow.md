# Flaming Arrow

**3rd-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 reaction (which you take immediately after hitting or missing a target with a ranged attack using a weapon)

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous

As your attack hits or misses the target, the weapon or ammunition you're using transforms into a streak of fire. Instead of taking any damage or other effects from the attack, the target takes 6d6 Fire damage on a hit or half as much damage on a miss. Each creature within 10 feet of the target then makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

The weapon or ammunition then returns to its normal form.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage for both effects of the spell increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
