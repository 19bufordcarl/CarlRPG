# Controlled Inferno

**4th-level Pyromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: 10 minutes (concentration)

You create a raging inferno around you that you have great control over. The flames flit around you in a 15-foot Emanation for the duration. Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals an extra 3d6 Fire damage when you hit a creature in the Emanation.

In addition, the ground in the Emanation is Difficult Terrain for your enemies.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***

The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 4.
