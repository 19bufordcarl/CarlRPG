# Pyro-Blaster

*Bolts and blasts of flaming sorcery*

You focus on empowering your flames as much as possible for the largest possible infernos.

## Level 3: Empowered Pyromancy

When casting spells from the Pyromancy spell list, they count as being cast at one slot level higher than the one actually used to case them.

## Level 6: Fiery Fury

Add your pyromancy spell casting attack bonus to your pyromancy spell damage for 1 minute.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Level 10: Overchannel

You overcharge your flames and become restrained until the end of your turn. However, when you cast a spell, you may treat any below average rolls on your damage dice as the average rounded up. In return, you make a Constitution saving throw (DC 14), taking 2d6 Psychic Damage if you fail.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short rest.

*For example, if rolling a d6, any rolls less than 4 may be treated as a 4.*

## Level 10: Master Up-caster

When casting a spell at a higher level than its spell slot, you double the bonus you receive from the result of casting it at a higher level. This stacks with your Empowered Pyromancy feature.

*For example, casting the spell Burning Hands at 1st level has a base damage of 3d6. An additional 1d6 is added by Empowered Pyromancy, which is then doubled to 2d6, for a total of 5d6 Fire damage. If cast at 2nd level, it would be a total of 7d6 Fire damage.*

## Level 14: Max Channel

As a bonus action, you greatly overcharge your pyromancy spells, causing them to roll maximum possible damage. In return, you make a Constitution saving throw (DC 16), taking 7d6 psychic damage if you fail.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
