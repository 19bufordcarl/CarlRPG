# Pyro-Warrior

*A hack-and-slash fiery whirlwind*

You have honed the practice of pyromancy for the purpose of close quarters martial combat focusing as much on defense as offense.

## Level 3: Fiery Resilience

You learn how to weave flames to defend yourself from the worst attacks. Your base Pyromancer AC is increased by 2. You gain resistance to Fire and Cold damage.

## Level 3: Fiery Melee

You learn how to use fire magic to enhance your close range attacks. Whenever you deal damage to a creature within 5 feet of you, you deal an addition 1d6 Fire damage. This increases to 2d6 at level 6, 3d6 at level 11, and 4d6 at level 17.

## Level 6: Fiery Sculptor

You can create pockets of relative safety within the effects of your pyromancy spells. When you cast an pyromancy spell that affects other creatures that you can see, you can choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell's level. The chosen creatures automatically succeed on their saving throws against the spell, and they take no damage if they would normally take half damage on a successful save.

## Level 10: Fire Blast

When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can use this feature to send the creature flying backwards 60 feet in a fiery explosion.

Regardless of whether the target is immune to forced movement, it takes 10d10 Fire damage as it reels from the explosion. If the creature has fewer than 50 hit points remaining, it is knocked Unconscious from the blast.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Level 14: Inferno's Rage

When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to deal Fire damage to the attacker. The damage equals your Pyromancer level. The attacker must also make a Dexterity saving throw against your Pyromancer spell save DC. On a failed save, the attacker must either move 5 feet away from you immediately or suffer the Fire damage again.

You gain immunity to Fire damage.
