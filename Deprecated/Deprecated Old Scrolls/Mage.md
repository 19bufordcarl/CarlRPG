As a mage, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Mage Features table.

## Level 1: Spellcasting

As a student of arcane magic, you have learned to cast spells.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your Mage spells. If you make an attack roll with a spell, your bonus is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your Intelligence bonus + your proficiency bonus.

**Cantrips.** You know two mage cantrips of your choice. Pick two from the table given later in this section or roll to pick at random, rerolling any duplicates. When you gain a level, you can swap one cantrip you know for a different one from the mage list.

**Spells Known.** You start with two mage spells of your choice. You gain an additional spell at levels 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10. Each time you gain a mage level, you can replace one of your spells with a different one from the mage list.

**Casting Spells.** When you cast a spell, you can't cast it again until you recharge that spell. You can recharge one spell by making a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check as a magic action. You recharge all your spells by resting for one minute.

## Level 2: Arcane Flourish

Your mastery of arcane magic allows you to produce magical effects with ease. You gain an Arcane Flourish feat of your choice.

Whenever you gain a fighter level, you can replace the feat you chose with a different Arcane Flourish feat.

You gain additional Arcane Flourish feats at levels 4 and 6.

## Level 3: Sculpt Spells

When you cast a spell, you can choose to reduce the damage it does to creatures of your choice to 0.

## Level 5: School Specialization

Choose a school of magic. You gain a +1 bonus to your attacks with spells from that school and your save DC for those spells gains a +1 bonus.

## Level 7: Spell Focus

Choose one of your spells. You can attempt to recharge it as a bonus action. If you lose access to that spell for any reason, you can choose to apply this benefit to a different spell.

You choose a second spell to gain this benefit at level 9.

## Level 8: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to an ability of your choice.

## Level 10: Spell Mastery

When you cast a spell, you can choose to use its power tier 1 version. If you do so, you can cast the spell again without needing to recharge it.

# Mage Spell List

No full spell list yet, but this is what a spell looks like. The damage is balanced around roughly matching what a fighter using action surge can do over the course of an encounter. The idea is to make the mage feel powerful due to their relatively weak AC and HP.

## Fireball

**Evocation**

**Casting Time:** Action (Attack, Magic)

**Range:** Far

**Target:** A point in range

**Duration:** Instantaneous

**Concentration:** No

| Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3d6 fire | 4d6 fire | 8d6 fire |

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature near that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking damage based on your tier. A creature takes full damage on a failed save and half damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

# Arcane Flourish Feats

## Arcane Parry

**Arcane Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Arcane Flourish Feature)**

When an attack would hit you, you can give yourself a +4 bonus to AC against that attack and until the end of the current turn. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until your next turn.

## Elemental Affinity

**Arcane Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Arcane Flourish Feature)**

Pick one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, radiant, or thunder. You gain resistance against the chosen energy type. When you cast a spell that does damage, you can change that damage's type to your chosen damage type.

## Mystic Shield

**Arcane Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Arcane Flourish Feature)**

You can create an aura of protective magic around you, granting you an AC of 10 + your Intelligence bonus + your proficiency bonus.
