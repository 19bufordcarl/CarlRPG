### Level 1: Spellcasting

You are trained in the weaving of magical flames. Mana allows you to cast pyromancy spells and power some of your class features.

**Using Mana.** Mana is the magical energy you use to cast Pyromancer spells. You start with 2 mana points to spend and gain additional ones at levels 2, 5, and 8. You use mana to cast your prepared spells and use some of your class features. Once you expend a mana point it is lost. You regain one mana point when you finish a short rest and all mana points when you finish a long rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for Pyromancer spells. If you make an attack roll with a spell, your bonus is your primary modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your primary modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Caster Level.** When you cast a spell or use a class feature, its effects might be determined by your level. A spell's level must be less than or equal to your level. Many spells have effects, such as damage, that scale up with your level.

**Spells Prepared.** You start with two spells prepared. You gain additional prepared spells at levels 2, 5, and 8. You can prepare any spell from the Pyromancer list, or a spell granted to you by a class feature, with a level less than or equal to your level.

**Cantrips.** You know one pyromancer cantrip of your choice. Greater cantrips can only be prepared if you have a specific feature granting you that cantrip.

**Learning Spells.** Whenever you have a period of extended downtime, you can replace spells and cantrips you prepared with different spells of your choice.

**Casting Spells.** To cast a spell, you must spend a mana point and choose one of your prepared spells to cast. If you have no mana remaining, you cannot cast a spell unless it does not require mana. Cantrips are an exception to this. You can cast a cantrip without spending mana.

### Level 1: Specialty

Fire is energy incarnate. To some it means destruction, to others it means rebirth. There are many different ways to practice pyromancy. There are some mages who view it as a scholarly artform, creating masterful displays of fire for unique and complex purposes. There are others who view it simply as a powerful weapon of war capable of immense destruction.

The various specialties are listed at the end of this class description. Each specialty provides you with a variety of benefits that reflect its philosophy of pyromancy. You gain additional specialty benefits at levels 3 and 7.

### Level 1: Unstable Flame

When rolling fire damage, if you roll the maximum possible result on a die you may roll an additional die of that type as additional fire damage. This benefit applies to the additional die you roll.

### Level 1: Flickering Flame

As a magic action, you can cause fire to erupt from one of your hands. This fire sheds bright light in your area and dim light up to 1 area away. As a utilize action, you can touch a flammable object in melee range and cause it to ignite. You can choose to douse your hand on your turn without using an action.

You gain an additional benefit from your domain.

As a chosen warrior of the gods, death must struggle to claim your soul. You die after your fifth failed death save rather than after your third.

### Level 10: Boundless Healing

When you use Channel Healing on a creature, other creatures of your choice within 1 area regain hit points equal to your level.

## Pyromancer Domains

A domain reflects the aspects of the world that your deity claims power over. This claim shapes your capabilities. A domain sometimes grants spells and cantrips. You always have these spells prepared and cannot replace them. These spells are in addition to the ones you prepare with the Miracles class feature.

### Storm Domain

You follow the lords of storms, the mighty deities who command the sky. The sky provides nourishing rains and wrathful storms. It is as unpredictable and wild as the deities who shepherd it.

**Level 1.** You gain the storm strike cantrip.

**Level 3.** You gain the wind step cantrip.

**Level 7.** Once per turn when you damage a hostile creature, up to two creatures of your choice within 1 area take 2d6 lightning damage.

### Sun Domain

The sun stands watch over the world, casting light that drives away the horrors of the night. As a Pyromancer of the sun, you are charged with protecting your flock from the terrors that lurk in the shadows.

**Level 1.** You gain the radiant strike and light cantrips.

**Level 3.** You gain the light of revelation cantrip.

**Level 7.** Once per turn when you target a hostile creature with an attack or spell, you or a creature of your choice within 1 area regains 1d6 hit points.

### War Domain

From the first conflicts between law and chaos to bloody conflicts between kingdoms and other realms, war has marked the world's history from the very beginning.

**Level 1.** You gain proficiency with heavy armor and martial weapons. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can invoke a miracle to inflict 1d10 force damage per level to the creature you hit.

**Level 3.** You add your Wisdom modifier as a bonus to weapon damage rolls.

**Level 7.** You now add double your Wisdom modifier as a bonus to weapon damage rolls.

## Flame Flourish Feats

### Flame Affinity

**Flame Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Flame Flourish Feature)**

You gain resistance against fire damage. When you cast a spell that does fire damage you can choose to have it deal maximum damage instead of rolling dice. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Flame Parry

**Flame Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Flame Flourish Feature)**

When an attack would hit you, you can summon a shield of flames, giving yourself a +4 bonus to AC against that attack and until the end of the current turn. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until your next turn.

### Flame Step

**Flame Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Flame Flourish Feature)**

Once on your turn, you can cause a burst of flames to erupt from under you, allowing you to move up to 30 feet in any direction without provoking opportunity attacks. This does not expend an action or any movement.

### Flame Warrior

**Flame Flourish Feat (Prerequisite: Flame Flourish Feature)**

As a magic attack action you can make a weapon attack and cast a pyromancy spell.
