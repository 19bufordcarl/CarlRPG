# Moldvay Spells

## Spell Descriptions

## Acid Bubble

**Evocation Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an acidic bubble within range that pops and splashes acid over the target area. Pick a number of creatures up to half your level. Each chosen creatures takes acid damage equal to your level + your Intelligence modifier.

## Acid Rain

**Level 3 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** 1 Minute

You fill an area with a sudden burst of acidic rain, burning your enemies' flesh and wracking them with pain. Pick a area in range. Creatures in that area must make Constitution saves. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 acid damage per level and makes one move to leave the area if possible. On a successful save, it takes half that damage and does not need to move.

## Arcane Aegis

**Level 5 Abjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Duration:** Concentration

For the duration, friendly creatures within range have advantage on all saving throws against effects created by magic actions. If a creature in your area uses a magic action on its turn, you can end this spell to attempt to counter it. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check opposed by that creature's Intelligence (Arcana) check. If you succeed, its magic action has no effect. If you fail, you take a failed death save and the action works as normal.

## Arcane Slumber

**Level 1 Enchantment (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Pick an area in range. Living creatures in that area must make Wisdom saving throws or fall asleep if their current hit points are less than or equal to 4 + 2 per level.

## Arcane Shadow

**Level 7 Divination (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

Pick an area in range. An invisible, shadowy copy of yourself appears in that area. It is immune to all damage except force damage, has AC 20, and uses your saving throws. You can see into the copy's area and you gain the benefits of darkvision when peering through it. You can move the copy once on each of your turns. It can fit through a space that is up to 1 inch in diameter. The copy does not need to remain within the spell's range and can enter places you cannot see. As a magic action, you can end the spell and teleport to the shadow's location.

## Aura of Vitality

**Level 7 Life (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel life-giving energy through your soul, allowing it to flow through you and into creatures near you. At the end of each of your turns, you gain two effects. You and creatures of your choice within 1 area of you regain 2d6 hit points. Undead creatures of your choice within that area take 2d6 radiant damage.

## Augury

**Level 3 Knowledge (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You receive an omen from your deity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The DM chooses the omen from the table below.

| Omen         | For Results That Will Be … |
| ------------ | -------------------------- |
| Weal         | Good                       |
| Woe          | Bad                        |
| Weal and Woe | Good and bad               |
| Indifefrence | Neither good nor bad       |

## Bless

**Level 1 Protection (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** Up to Three Creatures

**Duration:** Concentration

You bless up to three creatures within range. Whenever a target makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the spell ends, the target adds 1d4 to the attack roll or save.

## Commune

**Level 9 Knowledge (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You contact a deity or a divine proxy and ask it up to three questions that can be answered with a yes or no. The answer is always correct, but the deity's knowledge may be limited as gods are not omniscient. At the DM's option, the god may demand a service in return for the answer. You can always refuse this request, but if you do so the spell ends. If you accept, you must fulfill the request within one month or you lose one level.

## Conjured Blizzard

**Level 9 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Targets:** 1 Area

**Duration:** 1 Minute

You conjure a howling gale of cold energy that washes over your enemies in the target area. Creatures of your choice in that area must make Constitution saving throws. On a failed save they take 1d8 cold damage per level and cannot move. On a successful save a creature takes half the cold damage. A creature unable to move due to this spell can make a Strength (Athletics) check in place of moving on its turn with a DC equal to your spell save DC. On a success, they can move.

## Conjure Elemental

**Greater Conjuration Cantrip**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You conjure a size Medium elemental that appears in range. The elemental is friendly, rolls initiative, and takes its own turns. You control its actions. The elemental has the following statistics:

- **AC.** 12 + Your Intelligence Modifier
- **Hit Points.** 5 Hit Points + 2 per Level
- Tests. Your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus, with a +0 bonus for

Intelligence and Charisma checks. When the elemental is reduced to 0 hit points, it is destroyed and you must make a DC 15 Constitution save. On a failed save, you choose between taking a failed death save or 15 force damage. The elemental disappears when the spell's duration ends. When you summon the elemental, pick one of the following options:

- Air. The elemental has a fly speed. It can make a ranged attack that does 1d6 + your

proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage and can pull the target.

- Earth. The elemental has +4 AC. It can make a melee attack that does 1d8 + your

proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage and can push the target.

- Fire. The elemental has immunity to fire damage. It can make a melee attack that

does 2d6 + your proficiency bonus fire damage.

- Water. The elemental has a swim speed and can move through spaces with a

diameter as narrow as 1 inch. It can make a melee attack that does 1d8 + your proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage. Level 4. You have advantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining a death save when the elemental dies. Level 6. When you cast this spell, you can summon up to two elementals. You can choose the same elemental type for both or pick two different ones.

## Crimson Chains

**Level 9 Conjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Concentration

You weave your magic into a writhing chain of fire that wraps around a foe, roasting them as it holds them in place. The target creature must make a Strength saving throw. If it fails it is paralyzed and takes 5d10 + your Intelligence modifier fire damage. At the end of each of the creature's turns, it can repeat the save and takes 1d10 + your Intelligence modifier fire damage. The spell ends if it succeeds that save.

## Death Echo

**Necromancy Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Living Creature

**Duration:** 1 round

You pluck at the threads of a creature's fate, sending an echo of its death through time to its heart. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d12 + your Intelligence modifier necrotic damage and cannot willingly move closer to hostile creatures on its next turn. On a successful save, it takes no damage. The damage increases to 2d12 at level 5 and 3d12 at level 9.

## Death Ward

**Level 7 Life (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 8 Hours

The next time the target would be reduced to 0 hit points, it is instead reduced to 1 hit point and is immune to all damage until its next turn.

## Divination

**Level 7 Divination, Knowledge (Cleric, Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of casting this spell, you name a location or person you have seen in the past week. If you name a person, you learn their whereabouts at the moment you cast the spell and their general mood or disposition, such as their emotional state. If you name a place, you gain a clear image of its current state including inhabitants. Your vision allows you to see one area and the areas adjacent to it. You see and hear things as if you stood in that area for the next round.

## Divine Judgment

**Level 9 Evocation (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You bring divine judgement down upon your enemies and a blessing upon your allies. Hostile creatures in the target area must make Dexterity saving throws. They take fire damage on a failed save and half damage on a successful one. Undead creatures automatically fail this saving throw. Friendly creatures in the target area regain hit points. The damage and healing are determined by your level. The damage is 1d10 radiant per level. The healing is 1d10 hit points + your level.

## Doom Foretold

**Level 1 War (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 1 round

You intone a dreadful prophecy upon a creature, invoking the gods to twist its fate as punishment for its heresies and crimes. The target makes a Wisdom save. On a failed save, it takes necrotic damage based on your level. On a successful save, the target takes half damage. Whether the target succeeds or fails, once before your next turn you can grant a bonus to a single attack roll against the target equal to your level. This spells damage is 2d6 + an additional 1d6 per level.

## Dragon Spirit

**Level 3 Conjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You summon the arcane echo of a dragon. It is a size Medium dragon that looks like miniaturized dragon. It appears in your area. It can occupy a space in your front or rear rank and has AC 15 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test. Once during your turn, you can move the dragon. It can fly. At the end of each of your turns creatures of your choice in melee range of the guardian take fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier + half your level.

## Emerald Guardian

**Level 3 Abjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You conjure an echo of the ancient emerald guardians, mighty constructs that ring the bounds of creation and protect it from intruders. It is a size Medium construct that looks like an animated suit of antique armor crafted from emerald energy. It appears in your area. It can occupy a space in your front or rear rank and has AC 18 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test. Friendly creatures within melee range of the guardian can use the higher of AC 20 or their AC against ranged attacks. Once during your turn, you can move the guardian. At the end of each of your turns up to one creature in melee range of the guardian take 1d6 + your Intelligence modifier + half your level force damage.

## Ethereal Cloak

**Level 3 Illusion (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Concentration

The creature you touch is invisible. While it is invisible, it exists within the ethereal plane. It sees everything in black and white and any light sources it carry do not shed light except for it and other ethereal creatures. Ethereal creatures can see it and interact with it. Each time the target uses an attack or magic action, or takes an action that requires it to interact with the physical world (such as opening a door), roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, the spell ends.

## Fiery Redoubt

**Level 7 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 Minutes

You wreathe yourself in a fiery shield. You shed dim light in your area and gain resistance to fire. Once per turn when a creature hits you or a friendly creature within melee range of you with a melee attack, it takes 1d8 fire damage.

## Fire Blast

**Level 5 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to an area in range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in that area makes a Dexterity saving throw on a failed saving throw, it takes 8d6 fire damage, +1d6 per level about 5. On a successful save it takes half damage. Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

## First Speech

**Level 5 Divination, Knowledge (Cleric, Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 1 Hour

Your magic aligns a creature's mind with the primordial speech from which all magic rises, allowing it to communicate with any creature. This spell grants the creature you touch the ability to understand any spoken or signed language that it hears or sees. Its speech or gestures are also understood by any creature that has a language.

## Flame Cage

**Level 1 Conjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 1 round

You cause tendrils of searing flame to erupt around a creature, hemming it into a cage of flame. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d10 fire damage plus additional d10s equal to half your level on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Until the start of your next turn, a flame cage forms around the target. If it moves, it takes the spell's damage again as if it failed the save. Once a creature takes this damage, it cannot take it again.

## Flame Wave

**Level 1 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** Up to 3 Creatures

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A jet of flames erupts from your outstretched hands, along you to spray fire over your foes. Targets of this spell must make Dexterity saving throws, taking 2d6 fire damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Flight

**Level 5 Transmutation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature or Object

**Duration:** 1 Hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target gains a fly speed. Alternatively, you can touch an object that is up to 10 feet on each side. That object floats 3 feet off the ground and becomes light enough that a creature in melee range of it can move the object along with them as they move. When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft unless it can stop the fall.

## Fog Bank

**Level 1 Conjuration (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** 10 Minutes

You create a thick fog bank that fills the target area. The sphere is heavily obscured and distorts sound. Creatures outside of the fog cannot hear any noise that would need to pass through it. Once on each of your turns, if you can see the fog bank you can move it once.

## Force Sphere

**Abjuration Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 1 round

You hurl a pebble-sized sphere of shimmering, blue force at a foe. On impact, it bursts into the size of a small boulder, sending the target flying back. You make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 + your Intelligence modifier force damage and you push it. The damage increases to 2d10 at level 5 and 3d10 at level 9.

## Grasp of Justice

**Level 3 Order (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Concentration

Choose a creature within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become paralyzed for the duration as a shimmering hand of divine magic (or a claw, or a tentacle, as befits your deity) grasps the target. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

## Hallowed Ground

**Level 5 Protection (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel divine energy, warding the area around you with protective magic. You and friendly creatures in your area gain the following benefits while within 1 area of you:

- +1 bonus to AC
- Advantage on death saves

Each time you cast a spell or use a feature that allows a creature to regain hit points, you and creatures of your choice within 1 area of you regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.

## Hold Portal

**Transmutation Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Door or Similar Portal

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel power into a door, transforming its structure and causing its hinges and handles to fuse into useless shapes. Creatures other than you, and up to one other creature you pick when you cast this spell, must make a Strength (Athletics) check as a utilize action to open it. A successful check ends this spell. The DC equals your spell save DC + your power. You and the chosen creature can open the target door as normal.

## Ice Storm

**Level 7 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You fill the target area with a howling ice storm. That area becomes heavily obscured and is difficult terrain, requiring a Constitution check with a DC equal to your spell save DC to enter. When you cast this spell, and once on each of your subsequent turns, creatures in the area take 1d6 cold damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level.

## Icy Grasp

**Evocation Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You send an icy claw at your enemy that clutches their legs in ice, leaving them unable to move. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d10 + your Intelligence modifier cold damage and cannot move on its next turn. The damage increases to 2d10 at level 5 and 3d10 at level 9.

## Illusory Image

**Level 1 Illusion (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 15-foot cube. The image appears at a spot in the target area. The image is accompanied by all appropriate sensory effects, though it cannot cause damage. Once per turn, you can cause the image to move once. It alters its appearance as you wish to reflect its movement. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, since things can pass through it. A creature that examines the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image.

## Legerdemain

**Conjuration Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

When you cast the spell, you project telekinetic force to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial. The action you take must be simple and quick enough that you can complete it in one action. The spell is precise enough that you can attempt fine manipulation or apply force as needed. If you need to make a check as part of the action you attempt, you use your Intelligence in place of the ability normally used for the check.

## Light

**Evocation Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Object

**Duration:** 1 Hour

You touch one Large or smaller object that isn't being worn or carried by someone else. Until the spell ends, the object sheds dim light in its area. The light can be colored as you like. The spell ends if you cast it again.

## Light of Revelation

**Sun Cantrip (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You shed bright sunlight, illuminating the area you occupy. Undead and fiends treat the area you occupy as difficult terrain. If they attempt to enter it, they must make a Wisdom save to do so. Invoke Miracle. As an action on your turn, or as part of the action used to cast this spell, you cause undead and fiends in the spell's area to take 1d6 radiant damage per level.

## Lightning Bolts

**Level 5 Evocation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** Up to 3 Creatures

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Strokes of lightning blast out from you. Each target makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d6 lightning damage plus an additional d6 per level above 5 on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. You can reduce the number of targets to two and increase the damage by 2d6, or to one and increase it by 4d6.

## Mass Healing

**Level 9 Life (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** 1 or More Creatures

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Creatures of your choice in range regain hit points equal to 1d8 per level + your Wisdom modifier.

## Mending

**Order, Transmutation Cantrip (Cleric, Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Object

**Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage. This spell can physically repair a magic item, but it can't restore magic to such an object.

## Modify Self

**Greater Transmutation Cantrip**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel magic into your body, altering your physical form as needed. Pick one of the following benefits:

- Growth. You become size Large. While size Large, you gain a +2 bonus to Strength

and your melee range includes everything within 1 area of you.

- Speed. You grant yourself superior speed and reflexes. You gain a +2 bonus to

Dexterity and you can move twice on your turn.

- Durability. You increase your durability and endurance. You gain a +2 bonus

Constitution and to AC.

- Aquatic Adaptation. You can breathe air and water and gain a swim speed.
- **Brachiation.** You Gain a Climb Speed

Level 4. You pick up to two benefits from this spell's list. Level 6. You gain an additional benefit to add to the list of options you can choose with this spell. You can choose to sprout wings, gaining a fly speed.

## Mystic Eye

**Level 1 Divination, Knowledge (Cleric, Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

As a magic action for the spell's duration, and as part of casting this spell, you can pick a point you can see within 5 areas. You view the area around that point as if you stood there when you use that action. Note that you can peer through keyholes, under doors, through cracks, and so on to peer at areas that would otherwise be out of view.

## Mystic Shield

**Greater Abjuration Cantrip**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Hour

An invisible field of energy surrounds you, deflecting attacks away from you. Your AC is 12 + your Intelligence modifier for the spell's duration. Level 4. When you are hit by an attack you can gain a +4 bonus AC against that attack, potentially turning it into a miss. This benefit lasts until the end of the current turn. Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until your next turn. Level 6. You can now use the level 4 benefit twice before your next turn.

## Necrotic Mote

**Level 3 Necromancy (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You call forth a sphere of energy from the death plane, a mote of anti-life that drains energy from those near it. It is a 5-foot-diameter sphere of swirling, enraged spirits that appears in your area. It can occupy a space in your front or rear rank and has AC 15 and hit points equal to your level times 3. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus. Once during your turn, you can move the shade once. It can move both horizontally and vertically. At the end of each of your turns up to 1d3 creatures in melee range of the mote take 1d6 + your Intelligence modifier + half your level necrotic damage.

## Phantom Figment

**Illusion Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

You create an auditory and visual illusion. A creature can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the illusion becomes faint to the creature. On your turn, you can move the image once within its area or to an adjacent one, but it must remain in your sight at all times. The illusion's appearance and effects can change over time as you wish. An illusion might say different phrases or alter its appearance. Sound. If you create a sound, its volume can range from a whisper to a scream. It can duplicate any sound you have heard. Image. If you create an image of an object no larger than a 5-foot cube. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, since things can pass through it. Smell. The illusion creates an odor of your choice, including the scent of a creature.

## Protective Ward

**Level 1 Protection (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Concentration

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is protected against hostile creatures. The target gains +1 AC and advantage on all saves. The target also can't be charmed or frightened. If the target is already charmed or frightened, that condition ends on it.

**Sun Cantrip (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** 1 round

You make a weapon against the target. If you hit, the target has disadvantage on attacks against targets other than you on its next turn. Invoke Miracle. When you hit with the attack, the target instead must make a Constitution save. If it fails, it is blinded. At the end of each of its turns, it repeats the save ending this effect on a success.

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Dead Creature

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature that has died within the last year. That creature revives with 1 hit point and loses all failed death saves.

**Level 3 Life (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** Up to 3 Creatures

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Each target regains 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier hit points and you can end one of the following conditions on it: blinded, deafened, or paralyzed.

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Dead Creature

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature revives with 1 hit point and loses all failed death saves. This spell cannot reattach lost limbs or a severed head or other extreme wounds that would make revivification impossible.

## Rolling Thunder

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 1 Area

**Target:** 1 Creature or Object

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an orb of rumbling thunder energy and send it bouncing along the ground toward your enemies. Make a ranged spell attack against target creature. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 thunder damage, plus additional d8s equal to half your level, and is knocked prone. On a miss, the target takes half damage. If the attack hits, you may repeat it against another target in range. You cannot target the same creature more than once with this spell.

## Sacred Redoubt

**Level 1 Protection (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Duration:** Concentration

You create a redoubt of protective energy in a halo around you that shields your allies from harm. You and friendly creatures gain +1 AC and cover against ranged attacks while in melee range of you.

## Silence

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Concentration

No sound can be created within or pass through the target area. Any creature or object entirely inside the area is immune to thunder damage, and creatures are deafened while entirely inside it. Creatures cannot use magic actions within this area.

## Spare the Dying

**Life Cantrip (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Dying Creature

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature within range that is dying. The creature regains 1 hit point. The range increases to 10 areas at level 9.

## Siren's Call

**Enchantment Cantrip (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Creature

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You bewitch a creature's senses, filling it with the urge to draw closer to you. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. If it fails, you pull it and it is charmed by you until the end of its next turn. You can target up to two creatures at level 5 or three creatures at level 9.

## Speak with Dead

**Level 5 Knowledge (Cleric)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Corpse

**Duration:** 10 Minutes

Until the spell ends, you can ask the target corpse up to five questions. The corpse knows only what it knew in life, including the languages it knew. The corpse is under no compulsion to offer a truthful answer if you are antagonistic toward it or it recognizes you as an enemy.

## Spell Storm

**Greater Evocation Cantrip**

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 10 Areas

**Target:** 1 Area

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a storm of energy into the target area. Creatures in that area must make Dexterity saving throws. They take 1d6 damage per level on a failed save and no damage on a successful one. When you cast this spell, you pick the damage type from among acid, cold, fire, or thunder.

**Level 4. Creatures that Succeed the save Take Half Damage**

Level 6. You now target two areas in range when you cast this spell.

## Spider Form

**Level 3 Transmutation (Wizard)**

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Melee

**Target:** 1 Creature
