### Level 1: Miracles

You are an initiate into the mysteries of divine power, allowing you to call upon the gods to invoke miracles. Miracles allow you to cast divine spells and power some of your class features.

**Invoking Miracles.** Miracles are the divine energy you invoke to cast cleric spells. You start with the ability to invoke two miracles and gain additional ones at levels 2, 5, and 8. You invoke miracles to cast your prepared spells and use some of your class features. Once you invoke a miracle you cannot use it again. You regain one invoked miracle when you finish a short rest and all invoked miracles when you finish a long rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is the spellcasting ability for cleric spells. If you make an attack roll with a spell, your bonus is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Caster Level.** When you cast a spell or use a class feature, its effects might be determined by your level. A spell's level must be less than or equal to your level. Many spells have effects, such as damage, that scale up with your level.

**Spells Prepared.** You start with two spells prepared. You gain additional prepared spells at levels 4, 7, and 10. You can prepare any spell from the cleric list, or a spell granted to you by a class feature, with a level less than or equal to your level.

When you finish a long rest, you can replace spells you prepared with different spells of your choice.

**Casting Spells.** To cast a spell, you must invoke a miracle and choose one of your prepared spells to cast. If you have no miracles remaining, you cannot cast a spell unless it does not require a miracle. Cantrips are an exception to this. You can cast a cantrip without invoking a miracle.

### Level 1: Channel Healing

As a magic action, you can invoke a miracle and cause a creature in melee range to regain hit points. You restore 1d8 hit points per level + your Wisdom modifier.

### Level 1: Domain

A domain reflects an area of divine influence. When the world was created, it was divided into distinct spheres of influence. Each god claimed one such area, with powerful gods claiming more than one. Some domains are claimed by several gods. These deities struggle for dominance, with conflicts ranging from political struggles to outright warfare.

The divine domains are listed at the end of this class description. Each domain provides you with a variety of benefits that reflect the nature of the domain. You gain additional domain benefits at levels 3 and 7.

### Level 2: Repel Undead

As a magic action, you force all undead creatures in your area to make a Charisma save (DC 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). If a creature fails the saving throw, it is frightened of you. It is no longer frightened after 1 minute, if it takes damage, or if you end your turn more than 10 areas from it. Creatures affected by this feature typically attempt to flee from you to end the effect.

If a creature succeeds the saving throw, it is immune to this class feature for the next hour.

**Invoke Miracle.** You can invoke a miracle when you use this class feature. If you do so, it deals 1d10 radiant damage per level to undead creatures that fail their save, or half damage to creatures that succeed their save. This damage does not end the frightened condition early.

### Level 3: Domain

You gain an additional benefit from your domain.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

### Level 5: Divine Warrior

As an attack action, you can make two weapon attacks. Alternatively, as a magic attack action you can make a weapon attack and use Channel Healing.

### Level 6: Divine Guidance

Pick a skill you are proficient with or have expertise with. You add your Wisdom modifier as a bonus to all checks made with that skill.

### Level 7: Domain

You gain an additional benefit from your domain.

### Level 8: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

### Level 9: Guarded Soul

As a chosen warrior of the gods, death must struggle to claim your soul. You die after your fifth failed death save rather than after your third.

### Level 10: Boundless Healing

When you use Channel Healing on a creature, other creatures of your choice within 1 area regain hit points equal to your level.

## Cleric Domains

A domain reflects the aspects of the world that your deity claims power over. This claim shapes your capabilities. A domain sometimes grants spells and cantrips. You always have these spells prepared and cannot replace them. These spells are in addition to the ones you prepare with the Miracles class feature.

### Storm Domain

You follow the lords of storms, the mighty deities who command the sky. The sky provides nourishing rains and wrathful storms. It is as unpredictable and wild as the deities who shepherd it.

**Level 1.** You gain the storm strike cantrip.

**Level 3.** You gain the wind step cantrip.

**Level 7.** Once per turn when you damage a hostile creature, up to two creatures of your choice within 1 area take 2d6 lightning damage.

### Sun Domain

The sun stands watch over the world, casting light that drives away the horrors of the night. As a cleric of the sun, you are charged with protecting your flock from the terrors that lurk in the shadows.

**Level 1.** You gain the radiant strike and light cantrips.

**Level 3.** You gain the light of revelation cantrip.

**Level 7.** Once per turn when you target a hostile creature with an attack or spell, you or a creature of your choice within 1 area regains 1d6 hit points.

### War Domain

From the first conflicts between law and chaos to bloody conflicts between kingdoms and other realms, war has marked the world's history from the very beginning.

**Level 1.** You gain proficiency with heavy armor and martial weapons. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can invoke a miracle to inflict 1d10 force damage per level to the creature you hit.

**Level 3.** You add your Wisdom modifier as a bonus to weapon damage rolls.

**Level 7.** You now add double your Wisdom modifier as a bonus to weapon damage rolls.
