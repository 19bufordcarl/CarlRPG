# Pyromancy Spells

## **Cantrips**

### Ball of Flames

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a small ball of flames and hurl it at a point within range where it explodes in flames affecting all creatures within 5 feet. Each affected creature takes fire damage equal to your level + your spellcasting modifier.

### Conjure Flame Atronach

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a size Medium atronach that appears within range. The atronach is friendly, rolls initiative, and takes its own turns. You control its actions. The atronach has the following statistics:

- **AC.** 12 + your spellcasting modifier
- **Hit Points.** 5 hit points + 2 per level
- **Tests.** Your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus, with a +0 bonus for Intelligence and Charisma checks.

When the atronach is reduced to 0 hit points, it is destroyed and you must make a DC 15 Constitution save. On a failed save, you choose between taking a failed death save or 15 force damage. The atronach disappears when the spell's duration ends.

When you summon the atronach, pick one of the following options:

- **Slinger.** The atronach has a fly speed. It can make a ranged attack that does 1d10 + your proficiency bonus fire damage.
- **Brawler.** The atronach has immunity to fire damage. It can make a melee attack that does 2d6 + your proficiency bonus fire damage.

**Level 4.** You have advantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining a death save when the atronach dies.

**Level 6.** When you cast this spell, you can summon up to two atronachs. You can choose the same atronach type for both or pick two different ones.

### Searing Strike

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You make a melee attack against a creature within range. On a hit, you can change the weapon's damage to lightning. If you do so, creatures of your choice within 5 feet of you take fire damage equal to your spellcasting modifier.

**Expend Mana.** When you hit with the attack, you can spend a mana to increase this cantrip's damage by 1d10 per level.

## **Level 1**

### Flame Cage

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You cause tendrils of searing flame to erupt around a creature within range, hemming it into a cage of flame. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d10 fire damage plus additional d10s equal to half your level. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

Until the start of your next turn, a flame cage forms around the target. If it moves, it takes the spell's damage again as if it failed the save. Once a creature takes this damage, it cannot take it again.

### Flame Wave

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A jet of flames erupts from your outstretched hands, allowing you to spray fire over your foes. Up to three creatures within range must make Dexterity saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d6 fire damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

### Dragon Spirit

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a dragon made of pure flames. It is a size Medium and it appears next to you. It has AC 15 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test.

Once during your turn, you can move the dragon. It can fly up to 60 feet. At the end of each of your turns, creatures of your choice in melee range of the dragon take fire damage equal to your spellcasting modifier + half your level.

### Fire Storm

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 Minute

You fill a 30 foot cube centered on a point you choose within range with raging flames, burning your enemies' flesh and wracking them with pain. Creatures in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 fire damage per level and immediately moves to leave the area if possible. On a successful save, it takes half that damage and does not need to move.

### Flaming Weapon

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create a floating, flaming weapon of your choice, lasting for the duration. The weapon appears at a point you choose within range, and you can immediately make one melee spell attack against one creature within 5 feet of it. On a hit, the target takes fire damage based on your level. Once on each of your later turns, you can move the weapon up to 30 feet and make another attack with it against a creature within 5 feet of its new location. It deals damage according to you level based on the table below.

| Level 3–4 | Level 5–6 | Level 7–8 | Level 9–10 |
| --------- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 1d8       | 2d8       | 3d8       | 4d8        |

### Fire Blast

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 8d6 fire damage +1d6 per level above 5. On a successful save it takes half damage.

### Fire Flight

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You command flames so strong they allow you to defy gravity. For the duration, you gain a fly speed of 60 feet. When the spell ends, you fall if you are still aloft unless you can stop the fall.

### Flame Cloak

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Wrathful flames enshroud the space around you. At the end of each of your turns for the duration, hostile creatures within 20 feet of you take 2d8 fire damage.

### Fiery Redoubt

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You wreathe yourself in a fiery shield. You shed dim light in a 20-foot radius and gain resistance to fire. Once per turn when a creature hits you or a friendly creature within 5 feet of you with a melee attack, it takes 1d8 fire damage.

## **Level 9**

### Crimson Chains

**Level:** 9

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You weave your magic into a writhing chain of fire that wraps around a creature you can see within range, roasting it as it holds it in place. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is paralyzed and takes 5d10 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. At the end of each of its turns, it can repeat the save and takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The spell ends if it succeeds on that save.
