# Pyromancer

As a Pyromancer, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Pyromancer Features table.

### Level 1: Pyromancy

You are trained in the weaving of magical flames. Mana allows you to cast pyromancy spells and power some of your class features.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is the spellcasting ability for pyromancer features. If you make an attack roll with a pyromancer spell or feature, your bonus is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Spell Power.** When you use a pyromancer feature, its effects might be determined by your power. Many features have effects that scale, such as damage, based on your power.

**Mana Points.** Your magic is powered by mana. You gain mana points as indicated in the Pyromancer Features table. You can spend a number of points per turn up to your power.

You regain up to a number of mana points equal to your power when you finish a short rest, and all expended mana points when you finish a long rest.

**Fire Bolt.** As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against a target within 120 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 2d10 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d10 if your power is 5 or greater.

Before rolling your attack you can expend a mana point to increase the damage by 1d10 per point spent, and the target takes half damage on a miss.

### Level 1: Philosophy

Fire is energy incarnate. To some it means destruction, to others it means rebirth. There are many different ways to practice pyromancy. Choose one of the philosophies listed at the end of this class description. Each philosophy provides you with a variety of benefits that reflect its approach to pyromancy. You gain all philosophy benefits at your level and lower as you level up.

### Level 1: Unstable Flame

When rolling fire damage, if you roll the maximum possible result on a die you may roll an additional die of that type as additional fire damage. This benefit applies to the additional die you roll.

### Level 1: Flickering Flame

As a magic action, you can cause fire to erupt from one of your hands. This fire sheds bright light for 20 feet and dim light for an additional 20 feet. As a magic action, you can touch a flammable object in melee range and cause it to ignite. You can choose to douse your hand on your turn without using an action.

### Level 1: Gout of Flame

As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against up to three targets within 30 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 2d6 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d6 if your power is 5 or greater.

You can expend mana points to increase the attacks' damage by 2d6 per point spent, and a target takes half damage on a miss. Spend these points before rolling your attacks.

### Level 2: Adept Pyromancy

As you gain more pyromantic power, you exert more control over your magic and begin to shape it into more specific effects. You gain a Pyromancy feat of your choice.

Whenever you gain a pyromancer level, you can replace the feat you chose with a different Pyromancy feat.

You gain an additional Pyromancy feat at levels 4, 6, and 8.

### Level 3: Cinder Cloud

You can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to cause a great gout of smoke and cinders to erupt from your hands. Choose a point within range. The area within a 20-foot radius of that point fills with smoke that heavily obscures it for 1 minute per mana point spent or until you use this feature again. At the end of each of your turns while this effect lasts, creatures in the smoke-filled area take 1d6 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect.

### Level 3: Raging Flame

On your turn, you can choose to channel a raging flame. For the next minute, when you roll fire damage you gain the benefits of your unstable flame feature when you roll the maximum value or one less than the maximum value on a die.

Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

### Level 4: Adept Pyromancy

You gain an additional Pyromancy feat.

### Level 5: Fireball

You can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to hurl a burning ember that detonates in an area. Choose a point within range. Each creature within a 20-foot radius of that point must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect, or half as much damage on a successful save.

Until the end of your next turn, creatures that end their turn in the target area take 2 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect.

### Level 6: Adept Pyromancy

You gain an additional Pyromancy feat.

### Level 7: Conjure Flame Atronach

You can use a magic action and expend 4 mana points to conjure a vicious flame atronach to serve you. It lasts for up to 10 minutes, disappearing when it is reduced to 0 hit points.

The flame atronach is size Medium and appears in your area. It has AC 16 and hit points equal to your power times 5. If the flame atronach needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

The flame atronach uses your initiative and takes its turn after yours. It can use a melee attack with an attack bonus equal to 2 + your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting modifier that does 2d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage.

### Level 8: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

### Level 8: Adept Pyromancy

You gain an additional Pyromancy feat.

### Level 9: Fiery Form

You can use a magic action and expend 5 mana points to transform into a flame atronach for 1 minute and gain the following benefits:

- You are immune to fire damage.
- You can fly up to 60 feet on your turn.
- Once per turn when a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 5d6 fire damage.
- Your Unstable Flame class feature now adds two additional dice when it triggers.

### Level 10: Pyromancy Master

Work with your DM to craft a custom pyromancy feature to reflect your character's mastery over fire!

## Pyromancer Philosophies

A philosophy reflects the approach that you take as a pyromancer as your relationship to magic and how you wield it in the world.

### Way of the Phoenix

The way of the phoenix represents those who see fire as part of a natural and beautiful cycle of life, death, and rebirth. They seek to bring balance with their power, not chaos.

#### Level 1: Phoenix Initiate

You can use fire to cauterize your own wounds and burn away affliction. As a magic action, you can expend mana points and regain 2d8 hit points per mana point spent + your Wisdom modifier.

#### Level 5: Phoenix Adept

You can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to cause a blinding nova of fire to erupt around you. This is a pure, cleansing fire that seeks to root out the unnatural. All unholy creatures within 30 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d10 damage per mana point spent on this effect or half that much on a successful save. You choose if the damage is fire or radiant damage.

Until the end of your next turn, creatures that failed the save are also blinded.

#### Level 10: Phoenix Soul

When you would die, you can attempt to burn away your wounds and rebirth yourself from the ashes, following the way of the phoenix. Roll a Constitution check with a DC equal to 15 + 5 per each time you have successfully used this class feature.

If you succeed, you are immediately immolated and reborn from the ashes at full hit points and with all expended mana points recovered.

If you fail, your body explodes in a fiery inferno. Your physical body and all non-magical equipment you carry is utterly destroyed and creatures of your choice in your area take 5d10 force damage.

### Way of the Dragon

The way of the dragon represents those who see fire as primal dominance, apex might, and overwhelming presence. Dragons are the ultimate expression of elemental power. They are awe-inspiring, terrifying, and power hungry. These pyromancers seek mastery rather than balance.

#### Level 1: Draconic Initiate

**Fire Breath**. As a magic attack action, you exhale fire in a 15-foot cone or a 30-foot line. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. You can expend mana points to increase the damage by 1d6 per point spent.

#### Level 5: Draconic Adept

Your fire breath range increases to a 30-foot cone or a 60-foot line.

**Frightful Presence**. As a magic action, you sprout fiery draconic wings, gaining a fly speed of 30 feet and each humanoid creature of your choice that is lower level than you and within 30 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

#### Level 10: Draconic Soul

**Draconic Apotheosis.** You learn the profane rituals to undergo a draconic apotheosis. You must spend 10 minutes preparing the ritual and expend 5 mana points. Upon completion of the ritual, you gain the following benefits for the next hour:

- You appear as a Large dragon made of flames.
- You are immune to fire damage.
- You gain a fly speed of 90 feet.
- Your AC becomes 20.
- Your fire breath attack deals double damage.
- Your fire breath range increases to a 60-foot cone or a 90-foot line.
- At the start of your turn, roll a d6. On a 5 or a 6, you regain a mana point.
- As an attack action, you can make 3 melee attacks. Each attack deals 2d6 slashing damage and 1d10 fire damage.

When the hour ends, you take 1 failed death save. This death save is permanent and cannot be healed. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Way of the Volcano

The way of the volcano represents those who see fire as an unstoppable, world-reshaping force. Volcanoes destroy and create in equal measure. They cause lava flows, ash clouds, and eruptions that remake the land itself.

#### Level 1: Volcanic Initiate

**Volcanic Tremor.** As a magic action, you can expend 1 mana point to cause the ground in a 20-foot radius centered on a point you can see within 60 feet to crack and shake as lava bubbles up to the surface, causing it to become difficult terrain for 1 minute. Creatures that enter the area or start their turn there take 1d6 fire damage.

#### Level 5: Volcanic Adept

**Lava Burst.** As a magic action, you can expend 2 or more mana points to cause a lava burst at a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in a 20-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 fire damage per 2 mana points spent on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The area becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute.

**Meld into Stone.** As a magic action, you can turn your body into molten rock and enter a nearby bit of stone and then cool yourself off, merging with the stone. You can remain there for up to 8 hours, and you can sense what happens around you while merged.

#### Level 10: Volcanic Soul

**Cataclysmic Eruption**. As a magic action, choose a point you can see within 120 feet. A 60-foot radius sphere centered on that point erupts in fire, ash, and molten rock. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d10 fire damage and 10d10 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The area becomes difficult terrain permanently, and structures in the area take double damage.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Purifying Breath.** You gain the ability to breathe in smog, smoke, and other similarly toxic fumes.

## Pyromancy Feats

Here are the Pyromancy feats you can choose from.

### Ember Aegis

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

Once per turn when you take damage, you can spend 2 mana points to surround yourself with a swirling barrier of heat and embers. You gain resistance to all damage types except psychic until the end of that turn. That resistance applies to the damage that triggered the use of this feat.

### Heat Sense

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

You attune your eyes and body to detect minor differences in heat, which can allow your sense to penetrate even the deepest darkness. You gain a blindsight of 120 feet.

### Dancing Flames

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

You learn to sculpt fire into subtle illusions of light and heat. You gain the minor illusion and light cantrips from the Illusion spell list, manifesting them as dancing flames and heat shimmers. You use your pyromancer spellcasting ability for them.

### Deft Fire

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

You learn to deftly weave your fire around your allies. When you use magic that deals fire damage, you may reduce the damage it does to creatures of your choice to 0.

### Cleansing Flame

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

Your fire can cauterize wounds and burn away affliction. As a magic action, you can expend mana points and touch a creature, which regains 2d8 hit points per mana point spent + your Wisdom modifier.

### Smoke Veil

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

As a magic action, you can expend 2 mana points to wreathe yourself in a cloak of shimmering heat and smoke, turning invisible for 1 minute. If you use an attack or magic action, you become visible at the end of the turn unless you spend 1 mana point to remain invisible.

### Infernal Stare

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

As a magic influence action, you can expend a mana point to let fire blaze in your eyes and voice. You can force up to 1 + your power creatures to make a Wisdom save. If they fail, they are frightened of you for the next minute.

### Flame Step

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

Once on your turn, you can spend 2 mana points and cause a burst of flames to erupt from under you, allowing you to move up to 30 feet in any direction without provoking opportunity attacks. This does not expend an action or any movement.

### Flaming Weapon

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

As a magic attack action, you can expend a mana point to cause a weapon you are wielding to become wreathed in flames. For the next 10 minutes, whenever you attack with the weapon it deals an additional 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 3d6 if your power is 3 or greater and 5d6 if your power is 5 or greater. On the turn that you take this action, you may also attack with the weapon once without using an action.

### Flame Whip

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

As a magic attack action, you can expend a mana point to make a melee attack against a creature within 30 feet using a conjured whip made of pure fire. On a hit, this attack does 2d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage and you can move the target 10 feet in any direction if they are size Medium or smaller. The damage increases to 4d6 if your power is 3 or greater and can move Large targets. The damage increases to 6d6 if your power is 5 or greater and can move Huge targets.

### Fireproof

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

You gain resistance to fire damage. If this would result in you taking 5 or less damage, you take 0 damage instead.

### Fire Within

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

The fire that flows through you fortifies your physical form, granting you superior durability and strengthened willpower. You gain +1 AC and proficiency with Constitution saving throws.

### Immolating Rebuke

**Pyromancy Feat (Prerequisite: Pyromancy Feat Feature)**

When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can expend 1 mana point and deal 2d6 fire damage to it.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a short rest.
