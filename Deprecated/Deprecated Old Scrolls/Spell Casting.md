# Spell Casting

There are two types of resources that players can use to cast spells. Quick spell slots and Slow spell slots. The player's class and level determines how many they have of each and what level of spell they can cast.

## Quick Spell Slots

A quick spell slot usually can only cast lower level spells compared to a slow spell slot. These are restored after a short or long rest.

## Slow Spell Slots

A slow spell slot can be used to cast a players more powerful spells that require more power and focus. These are restored after a long rest. When a player uses a magic action to cast a spell with a slow spell slot, their turn ends immediately after the casting completes.

## Undercasting

When you use a slow spell slot to cast a spell of a level lower than the slot's level, you may attempt a DC 15 spell casting ability check. On a success, this spell consumes a quick spell slot instead of a slow spell slot. On a critical success, no spell slot is consumed. Your turn still ends after the spell is cast.

You gain advantage on this roll if the spell is a 5th level or lower spell, and this is more than 1 level below the level of the slot you are using.
