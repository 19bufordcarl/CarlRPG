# Electromancy Spells

## **Cantrips**

### Conjure Storm Atronach

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a size Medium atronach that appears within range. The atronach is friendly, rolls initiative, and takes its own turns. You control its actions. The atronach has the following statistics:

- **AC.** 12 + your spellcasting modifier
- **Hit Points.** 5 hit points + 2 per level
- **Tests.** Your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus, with a +0 bonus for Intelligence and Charisma checks.

When the atronach is reduced to 0 hit points, it is destroyed and you must make a DC 15 Constitution save. On a failed save, you choose between taking a failed death save or 15 force damage. The atronach disappears when the spell's duration ends.

When you summon the atronach, pick one of the following options:

- **Thunder.** The atronach has a fly and swim speed and can move through spaces with a diameter as narrow as 1 inch. It can make a ranged attack that does 1d6 + your proficiency bonus thunder damage and can push the target 10 feet.
- **Lightning.** The atronach has immunity to lightning damage. It can make a melee attack that does 1d12 + your proficiency bonus lightning damage.

**Level 4.** You have advantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining a death save when the atronach dies.

**Level 6.** When you cast this spell, you can summon up to two atronachs. You can choose the same atronach type for both or pick two different ones.

### Storm Strike

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You make a melee attack against a creature within range. On a hit, you can change the weapon's damage to lightning. If you do so, creatures of your choice within 5 feet of you take lightning damage equal to your spellcasting modifier.

**Expend Mana.** When you hit with the attack, you can spend a mana to increase this cantrip's damage by 1d8 per level and force the affected creatures to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they have disadvantage on any spellcasting checks until the end of their next turn.

### Spell Storm

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a storm of energy into a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Creatures in that area must make Dexterity saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 lightning or thunder damage per level, your choice. On a successful save, it takes no damage.

**Level 4.** Creatures that succeed on the save take half damage.

**Level 6.** You can fork the spell into two storms in opposite directions in range when you cast this spell.

### Wind Step

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 round

You step through the air, gaining a 30 foot fly speed until the end of your turn.

**Expend Mana.** When you cast this spell, you can spend a mana to instead gain a 30 foot fly speed for 1 minute per level.

## **Level 1**

### Feather Fall

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You can cast this spell in response to falling or seeing someone fall outside. Choose up to five falling creatures within range. A falling creature's rate of descent slows to 60 feet per round. If a creature lands before the spell ends, the creature takes no damage from the fall, and the spell ends for that creature.

### Rolling Thunder

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an orb of rumbling thunder energy and send it bouncing along the ground toward your enemies. Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 thunder damage, plus additional d8s equal to half your level, and is knocked prone. On a miss, the target takes half damage.

If the attack hits, you may repeat it against another creature within range. You cannot target the same creature more than once with this spell.

### Zephyr Steps

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Until the spell ends, you can take the Dash action on your turn without using an action. While walking during your turn, you do not fall unless you wish to do so. You can walk horizontally and downward vertically but not upward through the air. You fall at the end of your turn if you stand in midair and cannot otherwise remain aloft.

### Wind Disk

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You conjure a buffeting wind that swirls under you in the rough form of a disk, allowing you to ride it into the air. When you move, you can move vertically straight up or down. You can end your turn in midair. You do not fall unless you wish to and take no damage from falls. You cannot move horizontally unless you are on a solid surface.

### Lightning Bolts

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Strokes of lightning blast out from you at up to three creatures within range. Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 6d6 lightning damage + 1d6 per level above 5. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

You can reduce the number of targets to two and increase the damage by 2d6, or to one and increase it by 4d6.

### Storm Cloak

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Chaotic lightning surrounds you. At the end of each of your turns for the duration, hostile creatures within 20 feet of you take 2d8 lightning damage.

### Storm Flight

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You weave your power over lightning deftly to craft a means to allow levitation. For the duration, you gain a fly speed of 60 feet. Alternatively, you can touch a willing creature or object that is up to 10 feet on each side. A creature gains a fly speed while an object floats 3 feet off the ground and becomes light enough that a creature within 5 feet of it can move the object along with them as they move. When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft unless it can stop the fall.
