# Spells

Spells descriptions are given below.

## Alarm

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 8 hours

You set an alarm against intrusion. Choose a door, a window, or an area within range that is no larger than a 20-foot cube. An alarm alerts you whenever a creature touches or enters the warded area. When you cast the spell, you can designate creatures that won't set off the alarm. You also choose whether the alarm is audible or mental:

**Audible Alarm.** The alarm produces the sound of a handbell for 10 seconds within 60 feet of the warded area.

**Mental Alarm.** You are alerted by a mental ping if you are within 1 mile of the warded area. This ping awakens you if you're asleep.

## Augury

**Level:** 2

You receive an omen from the gods about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The DM chooses whether to answer weal, woe, or a mix of the two, depending on the most likely outcome of the action.

## Bless

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You bless up to three creatures within range. A blessed creature adds 1d4 to their attack rolls and saves.

## Command

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 60 feet

You speak a one-word command to a creature in range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or follow the command on its next turn. The DM adjudicates its actions, or you can choose one of these options:

**Approach.** The target moves toward you.

**Drop.** The target falls prone.

**Flee.** The target moves away from you.

**Halt.** On its turn, the target doesn't move.

You can use a higher-level spell slot to cast this spell. A level 2 slot allows you to target two creatures and a level 3 one allows you to target three creatures.

## Cure Wounds

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Touch

A creature you touch regains 2d8 plus your Wisdom bonus hit points.

The healing increases to 4d8 + your Wisdom bonus if you use a level 2 slot and 6d8 + your Wisdom bonus if you use a level 3 slot.

## Burning Hands

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

A thin sheet of flames shoots forth from you. Each creature in a 15-foot cube makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half damage on a successful one. At least one part of the cube must be within 5 feet of you.

The damage increases to 5d6 if you use a level 2 slot and 7d6 if you use a level 3 slot.

## Comprehend Languages

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You understand the literal meaning of any language that you hear or see signed. You also understand any written language that you see, but you must be touching the surface on which the words are written. This spell doesn't decode symbols or secret messages.

## Darkness

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Darkness spreads from a point within range and fills a cube that measures 15 feet on each side.

Darkvision can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it. Magical sources of light created by level 2 or lower spells shed no light in this area.

## Detect Magic

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You see the presence of magical effects within 30 feet on objects that are visible to you. Magic appears as a faint aura, the details of which allow you to determine its origin and the basic details of its effect.

## Detect Thoughts

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You sense the presence of thoughts within 30 feet that belong to creatures that know languages or are telepathic.

You don't read the thoughts, but you know that a thinking creature is present.

The spell is blocked by 1 inch of solid material.

If you can see a creature, as an action or when you cast this spell you can force it to make a Wisdom save. If it fails, you search its thoughts and learn the answer to one question you ask it. You learn the truth as the creature understands it. If it succeeds, the spell ends.

## Disguise Self

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You make yourself, including your belongings, look different. You can seem 1 foot shorter or taller and can appear heavier or lighter.

The changes wrought by this spell fail to hold up to physical inspection.

An observer can notice this is an illusion with an Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC.

## Dispel Magic

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 120 feet

Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Any ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 plus that spell's level). On a successful check, the spell ends.

## Fear

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Each creature in a 30-foot cube projected from you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or drop whatever it is holding and become frightened of you for the duration.

If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn't have line of sight to you, the creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.

## Feather Fall

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You can cast this spell in response to falling or seeing someone fall. Choose up to five falling creatures within range. A falling creature's rate of descent slows to 60 feet per round. If a creature lands before the spell ends, the creature takes no damage from the fall, and the spell ends for that creature.

## Fireball

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 150 feet

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 fire damage on a failed save or half damage on a successful one.

## Fire Bolt

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 120 feet

You hurl a mote of fire at a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 fire damage.

The damage increases to 2d10 at level 5 and 3d10 at level 9.

## Fly

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target gains a fly speed.

When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft unless it can stop the fall.

## Grease

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

Grease covers the ground in a 10-foot square centered on a point within range.

When the grease appears, each creature standing in its area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. A creature that moves into or in area or ends its turn there must also succeed on that save or fall prone. A creature need only make this save once per turn.

## Guiding Bolt

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You hurl a bolt of light toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 4d6 radiant damage and attacks against the target gain advantage.

The damage increases to 6d6 if you use a level 2 slot and 8d6 if you use a level 3 slot.

## Haste

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a willing creature within range. On each of its turns, it can choose to take a second action. If it does so, roll a d20. On an 11 or higher, it gains a failed death save.

## Hold Creature

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become paralyzed. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

If you use a level 3 slot to cast this spell, you can target two creatures.

## Inflict Wounds

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Touch

A creature you touch makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half damage on a successful one.

The damage increases to 4d10 if you use a level 2 slot and 6d10 if you use a level 3 slot.

## Invisibility

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A creature you touch becomes invisible. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

If you cast this spell with a level 3 slot, you can target two creatures.

## Levitate

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

One creature or loose object of your choice within range rises vertically up to 20 feet and remains suspended there. The spell can levitate an object that weighs up to 500 pounds. An unwilling creature that succeeds on a Constitution saving throw is unaffected.

The target can move only by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach (such as a wall or a ceiling), which allows it to move as if it were climbing. You can change the target's altitude by up to 20 feet in either direction on your turn. If you are the target, you can move up or down as part of your move. Otherwise, you can take an action to move the target, which must remain within the spell's range.

When the spell ends, the target floats gently to the ground if it is still aloft.

## Light

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

An object you touch sheds dim light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. The spell ends if you cast it again.

## Lightning Bolt

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 300 feet

You can make ranged spell attacks against up to three targets. The damage is 6d10 lightning if you attack three targets, 8d10 lightning if you attack two, or 10d10 lightning if you attack one. If the attack misses, the target takes half damage.

## Mage Armor

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a creature. Until the spell ends, the target can treat its AC as 13 plus its Dexterity modifier.

## Mage Hand

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

A spectral, floating hand appears at a point you choose within range. The hand vanishes if it is ever more than 30 feet away from you or if you cast this spell again.

When you cast the spell, you can use the hand to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial.

As an action on your later turns, you can control the hand thus again. As part of that action, you can move the hand up to 30 feet.

The hand can't attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 10 pounds.

## Magic Missile

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 120 feet

You create three glowing darts of magical force. Each dart strikes a creature of your choice that you can see within range. A dart deals 1d4 + 1 force damage to its target.

The damage increases to 2d4 + 2 if you use a level 2 slot and 3d4 + 3 if you use a level 3 slot.

## Mending

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** Touch

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch. If the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

## Minor Illusion

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You create a sound or an image of an object within range that lasts for the duration. See the descriptions below for the effects of each. The illusion ends if you cast this spell again.

A creature can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC.

**Sound.** If you create a sound, its volume can range from a whisper to a scream that can last up to the spell's duration.

**Image.** An image you create cannot be larger than a 5-foot cube or a size Medium creature. The image can't create sound, light, smell, or any other sensory effect. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion.

## Mirror Image

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 minute

Three illusory duplicates of yourself appear in your space. The duplicates move with you and mimic your actions, shifting position so it's impossible to track which image is real.

Each time a creature hits you with an attack roll during the spell's duration, roll a d6 for each of your remaining duplicates. If any of the d6s rolls a 3 or higher, one of the duplicates is hit instead of you, and the duplicate is destroyed. The duplicates otherwise ignore all other damage and effects. The spell ends when all three duplicates are destroyed.

A creature is unaffected by this spell if it cannot see or can use other senses to pinpoint you.

## Misty Step

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Self

Briefly surrounded by silvery mist, you teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Casting this spell does not use your action.

## Prestidigitation

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 10 feet

**Duration:** Up to 1 hour

You create a minor magical effect within range. You can light a torch, create a glowing sigil, clean off an object, or create an object that can fit in the palm of your hand. An object created in this manner is an obvious, worthless duplicate. The spell ends if you cast it again.

## Protection from Energy

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A willing creature you touch has resistance to one damage type of your choice: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder.

## Ray of Frost

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 60 feet

A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d8 cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 20 feet until after its next turn.

The damage increases to 2d8 at level 5 and 3d8 at level 9.

## Remove Curse

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

At your touch, all curses affecting one creature or object end. If the object is a cursed magic item, its curse remains, but the spell allows the owner to remove or discard it.

## Restorative Touch

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Touch

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: blinded, paralyzed, or poisoned.

## Revivify

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature returns to life, loses all failed death saves, and regains 1 hit point.

## Sacred Flame

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 60 feet

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 radiant damage.

The damage increases to 2d8 at level 5 and 3d8 at level 9.

## Scorching Ray

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 120 feet

You hurl three fiery rays. You can hurl them at one target within range or at several. Make a ranged spell attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 fire damage.

If you cast this spell with a level 3 slot, the damage increases to 3d6.

## See Invisibility

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You see creatures and objects that are invisible as if they were visible.

## Silence

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

No sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the sphere has immunity to thunder damage. Spells cannot be cast in the spell's area.

## Spare the Dying

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 30 feet

Choose a living creature within range that has 0 hit points. It regains 1 hit point.

## Speak with Dead

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 10 feet

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Pick a corpse within range. You cause it to partially animate, allowing it to speak in whatever languages it may know. It has no special compulsion to tell you the truth or even interact with you. The dead often demand a service in return for the information they provide. If you accept such a deal, you cannot cast this spell again until you complete the task or make a good faith effort to do so.

## Spirit Guardians

**Level:** 3

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Protective spirits flit around you in a 15-foot radius. This radius moves with you.

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. The designated creatures ignore the spell's effects.

A creature's speed is halved while within the spell's area. A creature that starts its turn in the area takes 2d8 radiant or necrotic damage (you choose when casting the spell). A creature can take this damage only once per round.

## Spiritual Hammer

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a floating, spectral hammer that appears within range in a space of your choice, and you can immediately make one melee spell attack against one creature within 5 feet of it. On a hit, the target takes force damage equal to 2d6 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

On your later turns, you can move the force up to 20 feet and repeat the attack against a creature within 5 feet of it.

If you cast this spell with a level 3 slot, the damage is 4d6.

## Thaumaturgy

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Up to 1 minute

You manifest a minor wonder within range. You can create minor illusions, alter one of your physical traits, change the sound or volume of your voice, and otherwise make cosmetic changes to yourself or an object in range. The spell ends if you cast it again.

## Thunderwave

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

You unleash a wave of thunderous energy. Each creature in a 15-foot cube originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage.

Unsecured objects that are entirely within the cube are pushed 10 feet away from you, and a thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet.

The damage increases to 3d8 if you use a level 2 slot and 4d8 if you use a level 3 slot.

## True Strike

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** Self

Guided by a flash of magical insight, you make one attack with a weapon.

The attack uses your spellcasting ability for the attack and damage rolls instead of using Strength or Dexterity. If the attack deals damage, it can be Radiant damage or the weapon's normal damage type (your choice).

You deal an additional 1d6 radiant damage with this attack at level 5 and 2d6 radiant damage at level 9.

## Water Breathing

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 24 hours

This spell grants up to ten willing creatures of your choice within range the ability to breathe underwater until the spell ends. Affected creatures also retain their normal mode of respiration.

## Zone of Truth

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You create a magical zone that guards against deception in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range. A creature that enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, a creature can't speak a deliberate lie while in the radius. You know whether a creature succeeds or fails on this save.

An affected creature is aware of the spell and can avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive yet must be truthful.
