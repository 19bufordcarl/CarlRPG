# Cryomancy Spells

## **Cantrips**

### Conjure Frost Atronach

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a size Medium atronach that appears within range. The atronach is friendly, rolls initiative, and takes its own turns. You control its actions. The atronach has the following statistics:

- **AC.** 12 + your spellcasting modifier
- **Hit Points.** 5 hit points + 2 per level
- **Tests.** Your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus, with a +0 bonus for Intelligence and Charisma checks.

When the atronach is reduced to 0 hit points, it is destroyed and you must make a DC 15 Constitution save. On a failed save, you choose between taking a failed death save or 15 force damage. The atronach disappears when the spell's duration ends.

When you summon the atronach, pick one of the following options:

- **Bulwark.** The atronach has +4 AC. It can make a melee attack that does 1d8 + your proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage and can push the target 10 feet.
- **Striker.** The atronach has immunity to cold damage. It can make a ranged attack that does 1d10 + your proficiency bonus cold damage.

**Level 4.** You have advantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining a death save when the atronach dies.

**Level 6.** When you cast this spell, you can summon up to two atronachs. You can choose the same atronach type for both or pick two different ones.

### Freeze Object

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You channel freezing magic into a door or other object within range. For example, with a door, you cause its hinges and handles to freeze and become unmovable. Creatures must make a Strength (Athletics) check as an action to open it. A successful check ends this spell. The DC equals your spell save DC. You can unfreeze the target at any time without using an action.

### Frost Shield

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You create several floating barriers made of hardened ice, deflecting attacks away from you. Your AC is 12 + your spellcasting modifier for the spell's duration.

**Level 4.** When you are hit by an attack, you can gain a +4 bonus to AC against that attack, potentially turning it into a miss. This benefit lasts until the end of the current turn. Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until your next turn.

**Level 6.** You can use the level 4 benefit twice before your next turn.

### Ice Sphere

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You hurl a pebble-sized sphere of shimmering, blue ice at a foe within range. On impact, it bursts into the size of a small boulder, sending the target flying back. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier force damage and you push it.

**Level 5.** The damage increases to 2d10.

**Level 9.** The damage increases to 3d10.

### Icy Grasp

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You send an icy claw at a creature within range that clutches its legs in ice, leaving it unable to move. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier cold damage and cannot move on its next turn.

**Level 5.** The damage increases to 2d10.

**Level 9.** The damage increases to 3d10.

## **Level 1**

### Mist Bank

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You create a cold, thick, misty fog bank that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. The area is heavily obscured and distorts sound. Creatures outside of the fog cannot hear any noise that would need to pass through it. Once on each of your turns, if you can see the fog bank, you can move it once.

### Conjure Frost Guardian

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a potent frost atronach guardian. It is a size Medium construct that is a being of spiked shards of ice with metallic rings of armor bounding it together. It appears next to you and it has AC 18 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test.

Friendly creatures within melee range of the guardian can use the higher of AC 20 or their AC against ranged attacks as the guardian will use its ice shield to defend them. Once during your turn, you can move the guardian. At the end of each of your turns, up to one creature in melee range of the guardian takes 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier + half your level cold damage.

### Frozen Grasp

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Choose a creature within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become paralyzed for the duration as you conjure a freezing mist that encapsulates them and freezes them in place. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

### Ice Disk

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You conjure a floating disk of solid ice that hovers under you, allowing you to ride it into the air. When you move, you can move it in any direction. You can end your turn in midair. You can be knocked off the disk, if you are, you must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw to catch yourself back on your ice disk. The disk can also be used to carry up to 500 pounds.

### Ice Weapon

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create a floating weapon of you choice made of magical ice lasting for the duration. The weapon appears at a point you choose within range, and you can immediately make one melee spell attack against one creature within 5 feet of it. On a hit, the target takes cold damage based on your level. Once on each of your later turns, you can move the weapon up to 30 feet and make another attack with it against a creature within 5 feet of its new location. It deals damage according to you level based on the table below.

| Level 3–4 | Level 5–6 | Level 7–8 | Level 9–10 |
| --------- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 1d8       | 2d8       | 3d8       | 4d8        |

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

### Ice Storm

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You fill a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range with a howling ice storm. That area becomes heavily obscured and is difficult terrain, requiring a Constitution check with a DC equal to your spell save DC to enter. When you cast this spell, and once on each of your subsequent turns, creatures in the area take 1d6 cold damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level.

### Blizzard

**Level:** 9

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You conjure a howling gale of cold energy that washes over your enemies in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Creatures of your choice in that area must make Constitution saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d8 cold damage per level and cannot move. On a successful save, it takes half the cold damage. A creature unable to move due to this spell can make a Strength (Athletics) check in place of moving on its turn with a DC equal to your spell save DC. On a success, it can move.
