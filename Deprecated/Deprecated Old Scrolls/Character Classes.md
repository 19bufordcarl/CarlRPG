# Classes

## Equipment Focused

### Warrior

- Not a spell caster
- Broad weapons specialist, tends towards melee, excels at skirmish combat
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- Champion (Tank style, big weapons, good armor)
	- Scout (Movement, speed, and stealth focused)
	- Berserker (Charge attack focused)
	- Brawler (Grappling focused)

### Archer

- Not a spell caster
- Ranged weapons specialist with melee as a backup
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- Ranger (Skill and knowledge focused with better melee weapon skills)
	- Sharpshooter (Pure damage focus with options for stealth)

### Fencer

- Not a spell caster
- Melee weapons focused, particularly excels at duels and single target combat
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- Defensive
	- Offensive
	- Sneaky

### Rogue

- Not a spell caster
- Focused primarily on utility skills and knowledge rather than combat expertise
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- Thief
	- Assassin
	- Mariner
	- Scholar
	- Merchant
	- Bard

## Magic Focused

### Pyromancer

- Spell caster
- Fire based magic
- Primarily damaged focused
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Flame Striker (Single target focused)
	- Flame Blaster (AOE blast focused)
	- Flame Warrior (Melee focused)
	- Flame Conjurer (Summon focused)

### Cryomancer

- Spell caster
- Ice based magic
- Hybrid defense and offense (mostly combat focused)
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Frost Striker (Single target focused)
	- Frost Blaster (AOE blast focused)
	- Frost Warrior (Melee focused)
	- Frost Conjurer (Summon and utility focused)

### Electromancer

- Spell caster
- Lightning based magic
- Primarily damage focused with utility
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Lightning Striker (Single target focused)
	- Lightning Blaster (AOE blast focused)
	- Lightning Warrior (Melee focused)
	- Lightning Conjurer (Summon and utility focused)

### Illusionist

- Spell caster
- Illusion and light based magic
- Primarily utility focused with indirect damage
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Imaginist (Minor Illusion - classic visual influence effects)
	- Prismaticist (Prismatic spray - random damage / effect - specialty)

### Enchanter

- Spell caster
- Charming and mind influencing magic
- Primarily utility focused with indirect damage
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Pacifist (calming effects specialty)
	- Chaoticist (frenzy inducing effects specialty)
	- Controller (domination effects specialty)
	- Summoner (use domination to control extraplanar creatures)

### Alchemist

- Spell caster
- Transmutation magic and the ability to craft magical items, from minor potions at low levels to magic swords at higher levels
- Primarily utility focused with indirect damage
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- Brewer (Potion creation specialty)
	- Arcane Smith (Weapons and armor specialty)

### Healer

- Spell caster
- Focused on restoration of the body and mind as well as magical defenses.
- Primarily utility focused with minimal damage
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Medic (Spell based healing with some martial training specialty)
	- Sage (Ritual focus on slower and more powerful healing of stronger ailments)
	- Tank (Spell based healing, empowering auras, and weapon enhancements)

### Necromancer

- Spell caster
- Generally considered an evil practice of magic focused on undeath and death rather than true life
- Hybrid mixture of utility and damage
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Study of Bones (Animate undead servants specialty)
	- Study of Spirits (Bind undead spirits to your service)
	- Study of the Vampire (Spells focused on dealing damage and life steal)
	- Study of the Lich (Spells focused on extending ones life at great cost)

### Diviner

- Spell caster
- Focused on study and arcane knowledge beyond the mundane world
- Primarily utility focused
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Study of the Celestial (Focused on good and using the stars and gods to predict future events and gain boons)
	- Study of the Infernal (Focused on evil and making pacts to influence future events)
	- Study of the Arcane (Focused on reason and logic to influence and predict the future)
	- Study of the Eldritch (Focused on what is beyond mortal understanding to influence or predict the future)

### Eldritch Mystic

- Spell caster
- Very rare form of magic. The realm beyond what is understood by mortals and their institutions.
- Hybrid of all forms of magic.
- Subclasses (Level 1)
	- The Mind (Psychic and telepathic utility focus)
	- The Void (Damage, aoe, and summons hybrid)

### Druid

- Spell caster
- Very rare form of nature magic. Exists outside of standard civilization.
- Hybrid mixture of utility and damage.
- Subclasses (Level 3)
	- Way of the Moon (water and cold based weather, radiant magic)
	- Way of the Stars (radiant attacks and navigation specialties and flexibility)
	- Way of the Sun (Radiant and fire attacks, daylight creation)
	- Way of the Sea (water and cold based attacks, water shaping)
	- Way of the Volcano (lava and fire based attacks)
	- Way of the Sky (lightning, thunder, and other weather based effects)
	- Way of the Beasts (Animalistic powers and transformations as well as animal and spirit allies)
	- Way of the Trees (Wood based creations and ent like companion creatures)
