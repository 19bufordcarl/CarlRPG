# Mysticism

## **Cantrips**

### Mending

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

This spell can physically repair a magic item, but it can't restore magic to such an object.

### Modify Self

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You channel magic into your body, altering your physical form as needed. Pick one of the following benefits:

- **Growth.** You become size Large. While size Large, you gain a +2 bonus to Strength and your melee range includes everything within 20 feet of you.
- **Speed.** You grant yourself superior speed and reflexes. You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity and you can move twice on your turn.
- **Durability.** You increase your durability and endurance. You gain a +2 bonus to Constitution and to AC.
- **Aquatic Adaptation.** You can breathe air and water and gain a swim speed.
- **Brachiation.** You gain a climb speed.

**Level 4.** You pick up to two benefits from this spell's list.

**Level 6.** You gain an additional benefit to add to the list of options you can choose with this spell. You can choose to sprout wings, gaining a fly speed.

### Telekinesis

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

When you cast the spell, you project telekinetic force to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial within range. The action you take must be simple and quick enough that you can complete it in one action. The spell is precise enough that you can attempt fine manipulation or apply force as needed. If you need to make a check as part of the action you attempt, you use your spellcasting ability in place of the ability normally used for the check.

### Alarm

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 8 hours

You set an alarm against intrusion. Choose a door, a window, or an area within range that is no larger than a 20-foot cube. An alarm alerts you whenever a creature touches or enters the warded area. When you cast the spell, you can designate creatures that won't set off the alarm. You also choose whether the alarm is audible or mental:

- **Audible Alarm.** The alarm produces the sound of a handbell for 10 seconds within 60 feet of the warded area.
- **Mental Alarm.** You are alerted by a mental ping if you are within 1 mile of the warded area. This ping awakens you if you're asleep.

## **Level 1**

### Commanding Voice

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You speak a one-word command, lacing it with mystical commanding power. You can target up to a number of creatures equal to your level. A target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or follow the command. You can create a one-word command of your choice, though the DM adjudicates the actual effects, or pick the command from these options:

- **Approach.** You pull the target.
- **Fight.** The target uses an attack action on its next turn if a hostile creature is in range.
- **Flee.** You push the target.
- **Grovel.** The target falls prone.
- **Halt.** The target cannot move on its next turn.

### Detect Magic

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You see the presence of magical effects within 30 feet on objects that are visible to you. Magic appears as a faint aura, the details of which allow you to determine its origin and the basic details of its effect.

### Mystic Eye

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create an invisible sensor at a point you can see within 70 feet. As a magic action for the spell's duration, and as part of casting this spell, you can view the area around that point as if you stood there. You can peer through keyholes, under doors, through cracks, and so on to peer at areas that would otherwise be out of view. The sensor perceives everything within 20 feet of it.

### Augury

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You receive an omen from supernatural forces (consulting the stars, reading the bones, or another mystical ritual) about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The DM chooses the omen from the table below.

| Omen         | For Results That Will Be … |
| ------------ | -------------------------- |
| Weal         | Good                       |
| Woe          | Bad                        |
| Weal and Woe | Good and bad               |
| Indifference | Neither good nor bad       |

### Detect Thoughts

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You sense the presence of thoughts within 30 feet that belong to creatures that know languages or are telepathic.

You don't read the thoughts, but you know that a thinking creature is present.

The spell is blocked by 1 inch of solid material.

If you can see a creature, as an action or when you cast this spell you can force it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you search its thoughts and learn the answer to one question you ask it. You learn the truth as the creature understands it. On a successful save, the spell ends.

### Silence

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

No sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the area is immune to thunder damage, and creatures are deafened while entirely inside it. Creatures cannot use magic actions within this area.

### Spider Form

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

One willing creature you touch gains several additional spider legs that emerge from its body, granting it the ability to move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving its hands free. It gains a spider climb speed.

### Weaver's Web

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a mass of sticky webbing at a point within range. The web fills a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. The area within the web is heavily obscured and is difficult terrain, requiring a Strength (Athletics) check with a DC equal to your spell save DC to cross it.

If the web takes fire damage, it is destroyed and creatures in its area take 2d6 fire damage.

### Dispel Magic

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Any ongoing spell from a source that is lower level than you ends. For each ongoing spell from a source higher level than you, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 + the level). On a successful check, the spell ends.

### First Speech

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

Your magic aligns a creature's mind with the primordial speech from which all magic rises, allowing it to communicate with any creature. The creature you touch can understand any spoken or signed language that it hears or sees, and its speech or gestures are understood by any creature that has a language.

### Temporal Bubble

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Choose a willing creature within range. The target slips into the space between the seconds, allowing it to think and act with incredible speed. On its turn, the target gains the following benefits:

- It can move one additional time.
- It can use the dash, hide, or use actions without using an action, or it can use the attack action but is limited to making only one attack.

### Water Breathing

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 24 hours

This spell grants creatures of your choice within range the ability to breathe underwater until the spell ends. Affected creatures also retain their normal mode of breathing.

### Dimension Door

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 200 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You use your magic to burrow through the firmament of reality, allowing you and others to cover a distance in one step. You teleport to a point you choose within range when you cast this spell, and as part of teleporting create spatial distortions in the area where you cast this spell and in your destination area (each a 20-foot-radius sphere). Once during a creature's turn, you can cause that creature to teleport from the area one distortion occupies to the other. The creature must allow you to teleport it in this manner.

### Divination

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of casting this spell, you name a location or person you have seen in the past week. If you name a person, you learn their whereabouts at the moment you cast the spell and their general mood or disposition, such as their emotional state. If you name a place, you gain a clear image of its current state including inhabitants. You can see and hear as if you stood in that location, perceiving everything within 60 feet of it for the next round.

### Commune

**Level:** 9

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You contact a supernatural entity and ask it up to three questions that can be answered with a yes or no. The answer is always truthful, but the entity's knowledge may be limited as they are not omniscient. At the DM's option, the entity may demand a service in return for the answer. You can always refuse this request, but if you do so the spell ends. If you accept, you must fulfill the request within one month or you lose one level, or another consequence the entity chooses.
