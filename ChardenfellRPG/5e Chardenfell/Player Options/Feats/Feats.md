# Feats

The feats in this folder are all the feats available in the 2024 Player's Handbook.

## Origin Feats

These are feats that are taken during character creation at 1st level.

## General Feats

These are feats taken starting at level 4 when the general feat selection option becomes available at level up.

## Fighting Style Feats

These are special feats only available to martial characters and chosen at certain times. For example, a Fighter chooses one of these at 1st level, while a Ranger chooses one at 2nd level.
