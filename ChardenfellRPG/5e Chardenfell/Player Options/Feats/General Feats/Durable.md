# Durable

*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+)*

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

You gain the following benefits.

**Defy Death.**

You gain a 4th Death Save. (*Chardenfell house rule modification*)

**Speedy Recovery.**

As a Bonus Action, you can expend one of your Hit Point Dice, roll the die, and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll.

*Source: PHB'24, page 203*
