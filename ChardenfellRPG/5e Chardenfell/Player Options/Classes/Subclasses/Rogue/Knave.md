# Knave

When heads need crackin' and people beatin', Knaves are those you turn towards. These brutes may not be professional soldiers, but honor don't win you fights - violence does. 

## Level 3: Mean Mug

You gain proficiency in Intimidation, or expertise in Intimidation if you were already proficient.

## Level 3: Right Tools

You gain proficiency with martial weapons, medium armor, and shields.

## Level 3: Heavy Beating

You can use Sneak Attack even when using non-finesse melee weapons.

## Level 9: Menacing

An enemy hit by your Sneak Attack will have disadvantage on their attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn.
