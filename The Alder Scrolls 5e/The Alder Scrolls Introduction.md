# The Alder Scrolls Introduction

The Alder Scrolls TTRPG is a compilation of streamlined rules for 5e inspired by the playtests of Mike Mearls, one of the lead designers on 5e for his take on what a streamlined spiritual successor to 5e would look like. It has an opinionated take on classes, moving away from some of the classics expected by many prior iterations of fantasy tabletop orthodoxy.

The Alder Scrolls, very legally distinct, is a world where magic is prevalent, but limited. The gods are but distant echoes of their legends. And the myths of the ancient past still haunt the present. While the world tends to take itself seriously, it also is full of weird and whacky characters that add comedy and joy to it, making it a fun place to return to at any time, just like real life.

Most of the rules will be quite similar to 5e with a few places streamlined and a few places with additional options added. Overall, it is a lighter ruleset with less of a focus on precisely simulating reality with its rule text and more of a focus on plain English descriptions of abilities.
