# Wizard Spells

## Acid Bubble

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an acidic bubble within range that pops and splashes acid over a 5-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose. Pick a number of creatures in that area up to half your level. Each chosen creature takes acid damage equal to your level + your spellcasting modifier.

---

## Animate Armor

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You touch a suit of armor and empower it with arcane force, allowing it to move on its own. It is a size Medium construct. It has AC 18 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test.

Friendly creatures within melee range of the guardian can use the higher of AC 20 or their AC against ranged attacks. Once during your turn, you can move the guardian up to 30 feet. At the end of each of your turns, up to one creature in melee range of the guardian takes 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier + half your level force damage.

---

## Arcane Aegis

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

For the duration, friendly creatures within range have advantage on all saving throws against effects created by magic actions. If a creature within 60 feet of you uses a magic action on its turn, you can end this spell to attempt to counter it. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check opposed by that creature's Intelligence (Arcana) check. If you succeed, its magic action has no effect. If you fail, you take a failed death save and the action works as normal.

---

## Arcane Shield

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

An invisible field of energy surrounds you, deflecting attacks away from you. Your AC is 12 + your spellcasting modifier for the spell's duration.

**Level 4.** When you are hit by an attack, you can gain a +4 bonus to AC against that attack, potentially turning it into a miss. This benefit lasts until the end of the current turn. Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until your next turn.

**Level 6.** You can use the level 4 benefit twice before your next turn.

---

## Arcane Shadow

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

Choose a point within range. An invisible, shadowy copy of yourself appears there. It is immune to all damage except force damage, has AC 20, and uses your saving throws. You can see into the copy's location and you gain the benefits of darkvision when peering through it. You can move the copy once on each of your turns. It can fit through a space that is up to 1 inch in diameter. The copy does not need to remain within the spell's range and can enter places you cannot see. As a magic action, you can end the spell and teleport to the shadow's location.

---

## Arcane Slumber

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a point within range. Each living creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature falls asleep if its current hit points are less than or equal to 4 + 2 per level.

---

## Aura of Vitality

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel life-giving energy through your soul, allowing it to flow through you and into creatures near you. At the end of each of your turns, you and creatures of your choice within 20 feet of you regain 2d6 hit points. Undead creatures of your choice within that area take 2d6 radiant damage.

---

## Conjured Blizzard

**Level:** 9

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You conjure a howling gale of cold energy that washes over your enemies in a 60-foot cone emanating from you. Creatures of your choice in that area must make Constitution saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d8 cold damage per level and cannot move. On a successful save, it takes half the cold damage. A creature unable to move due to this spell can make a Strength (Athletics) check in place of moving on its turn with a DC equal to your spell save DC. On a success, it can move.

---

## Conjure Atronach

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You conjure a size Medium atronach that appears within range. The atronach is friendly, rolls initiative, and takes its own turns. You control its actions. The atronach has the following statistics:

- **AC.** 12 + your spellcasting modifier
- **Hit Points.** 5 hit points + 2 per level
- **Tests.** Your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus, with a +0 bonus for Intelligence and Charisma checks.

When the atronach is reduced to 0 hit points, it is destroyed and you must make a DC 15 Constitution save. On a failed save, you choose between taking a failed death save or 15 force damage. The atronach disappears when the spell's duration ends.

When you summon the atronach, pick one of the following options:

- **Fire.** The atronach has immunity to fire damage. It can make a melee attack that does 2d6 + your proficiency bonus fire damage.
- **Frost.** The atronach has +4 AC. It can make a melee attack that does 1d8 + your proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage and can push the target.
- **Shock.** The atronach has a fly speed. It can make a ranged attack that does 1d6 + your proficiency bonus lightning damage and can pull the target.

**Level 4.** You have advantage on the Constitution saving throw to avoid gaining a death save when the atronach dies.

**Level 6.** When you cast this spell, you can summon up to two atronachs. You can choose the same atronach type for both or pick two different ones.

---

## Crimson Chains

**Level:** 9

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You weave your magic into a writhing chain of fire that wraps around a creature you can see within range, roasting it as it holds it in place. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is paralyzed and takes 5d10 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. At the end of each of its turns, it can repeat the save and takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The spell ends if it succeeds on that save.

---

## Death Echo

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You pluck at the threads of a creature's fate, sending an echo of its death through time to its heart. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d12 + your spellcasting modifier necrotic damage and cannot willingly move closer to hostile creatures on its next turn. On a successful save, it takes no damage.

**Level 5.** The damage increases to 2d12.

**Level 9.** The damage increases to 3d12.

---

## Divination

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of casting this spell, you name a location or person you have seen in the past week. If you name a person, you learn their whereabouts at the moment you cast the spell and their general mood or disposition, such as their emotional state. If you name a place, you gain a clear image of its current state including inhabitants. You can see and hear as if you stood in that location, perceiving everything within 60 feet of it for the next round.

---

## Dragon Spirit

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You summon the arcane echo of a dragon. It is a size Medium dragon that looks like a miniaturized dragon. It appears next to you. It has AC 15 and hit points equal to your level times 4. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses the bonus you would use for that test.

Once during your turn, you can move the dragon up to 60 feet. It can fly. At the end of each of your turns, creatures of your choice in melee range of the dragon take fire damage equal to your spellcasting modifier + half your level.

---

## Ethereal Cloak

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Concentration

You touch a creature, rendering it invisible. While invisible, it exists within the Ethereal Plane. It sees everything in black and white, and any light sources it carries do not shed light except for it and other ethereal creatures. Ethereal creatures can see it and interact with it. Each time the target uses an attack or magic action, or takes an action that requires it to interact with the physical world (such as opening a door), roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, the spell ends.

---

## Fiery Redoubt

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You wreathe yourself in a fiery shield. You shed dim light in a 20-foot radius and gain resistance to fire. Once per turn when a creature hits you or a friendly creature within 5 feet of you with a melee attack, it takes 1d8 fire damage.

---

## Fire Blast

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 8d6 fire damage + 1d6 per level above 5. On a successful save, it takes half damage.

---

## Flame Cage

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You cause tendrils of searing flame to erupt around a creature within range, hemming it into a cage of flame. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d10 fire damage plus additional d10s equal to half your level. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

Until the start of your next turn, a flame cage forms around the target. If it moves, it takes the spell's damage again as if it failed the save. Once a creature takes this damage, it cannot take it again.

---

## Flame Wave

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A jet of flames erupts from your outstretched hands, allowing you to spray fire over your foes. Up to three creatures within range must make Dexterity saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d6 fire damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

---

## Flight

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target gains a fly speed. Alternatively, you can touch an object that is up to 10 feet on each side. That object floats 3 feet off the ground and becomes light enough that a creature within 5 feet of it can move the object along with them as they move. When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft unless it can stop the fall.

---

## Fog Bank

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You create a thick fog bank that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. The area is heavily obscured and distorts sound. Creatures outside of the fog cannot hear any noise that would need to pass through it. Once on each of your turns, if you can see the fog bank, you can move it once.

---

## Force Sphere

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

You hurl a pebble-sized sphere of shimmering, blue force at a foe within range. On impact, it bursts into the size of a small boulder, sending the target flying back. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier force damage and you push it.

**Level 5.** The damage increases to 2d10.

**Level 9.** The damage increases to 3d10.

---

## Glyph of Warding

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Until dispelled or triggered

When you cast this spell, you inscribe a glyph that creates a magical effect triggered by other creatures either on a surface or within an object. The glyph can be no bigger than a 10-foot diameter. Choose one of the following effects:

- **Explosive Runes.** The glyph deals 1d8 damage per level of a damage type of your choosing to all creatures that fail a Dexterity saving throw within 20 feet. Creatures take half damage on a successful save.
- **Spell Glyph.** You can store another prepared spell in the glyph. When the glyph is triggered, the spell is cast from the glyph without you needing to use a magic action. You control the casting of the spell as normal, except it comes from the glyph rather than you.

When a creature touches the glyph, you may choose to have it trigger the glyph. You are immediately mentally aware of whenever a creature is within the immediate area of the glyph, no line of sight or other senses required.

---

## Hold Portal

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel power into a door within range, transforming its structure and causing its hinges and handles to fuse into useless shapes. Creatures other than you, and up to one other creature you pick when you cast this spell, must make a Strength (Athletics) check as a utilize action to open it. A successful check ends this spell. The DC equals your spell save DC + your power. You and the chosen creature can open the target door as normal.

## Ice Storm

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You fill a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range with a howling ice storm. That area becomes heavily obscured and is difficult terrain, requiring a Constitution check with a DC equal to your spell save DC to enter. When you cast this spell, and once on each of your subsequent turns, creatures in the area take 1d6 cold damage plus additional d6s equal to half your level.

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You send an icy claw at a creature within range that clutches its legs in ice, leaving it unable to move. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier cold damage and cannot move on its next turn.

**Level 5.** The damage increases to 2d10.

**Level 9.** The damage increases to 3d10.

## Illusory Image

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 15-foot cube. The image appears at a spot within range. The image is accompanied by all appropriate sensory effects, though it cannot cause damage.

Once per turn, you can cause the image to move once. It alters its appearance as you wish to reflect its movement.

Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, since things can pass through it. A creature that examines the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image.

## Light

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch one Large or smaller object that isn't being worn or carried. Until the spell ends, the object sheds dim light in a 20-foot radius. The light can be colored as you like. The spell ends if you cast it again.

---

## Lightning Bolts

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Strokes of lightning blast out from you at up to three creatures within range. Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 6d6 lightning damage + 1d6 per level above 5. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

You can reduce the number of targets to two and increase the damage by 2d6, or to one and increase it by 4d6.

---

## Mending

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

This spell can physically repair a magic item, but it can't restore magic to such an object.

---

## Minor Illusion

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You create an auditory and visual illusion within range. A creature can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the illusion becomes faint to the creature.

On your turn, you can move the image once up to 30 feet, but it must remain in your sight at all times. The illusion's appearance and effects can change over time as you wish. An illusion might say different phrases or alter its appearance.

- **Sound.** If you create a sound, its volume can range from a whisper to a scream. It can duplicate any sound you have heard.
- **Image.** You create an image of an object no larger than a 5-foot cube. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, since things can pass through it.
- **Smell.** The illusion creates an odor of your choice, including the scent of a creature.

---

## Minor Telekinesis

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

When you cast the spell, you project telekinetic force to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial within range. The action you take must be simple and quick enough that you can complete it in one action. The spell is precise enough that you can attempt fine manipulation or apply force as needed. If you need to make a check as part of the action you attempt, you use your spellcasting ability in place of the ability normally used for the check.

---

## Modify Self

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration

You channel magic into your body, altering your physical form as needed. Pick one of the following benefits:

- **Growth.** You become size Large. While size Large, you gain a +2 bonus to Strength and your melee range includes everything within 10 feet of you.
- **Speed.** You grant yourself superior speed and reflexes. You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity and you can move twice on your turn.
- **Durability.** You increase your durability and endurance. You gain a +2 bonus to Constitution and to AC.
- **Aquatic Adaptation.** You can breathe air and water and gain a swim speed.
- **Brachiation.** You gain a climb speed.

**Level 4.** You pick up to two benefits from this spell's list.

**Level 6.** You gain an additional benefit to add to the list of options you can choose with this spell. You can choose to sprout wings, gaining a fly speed.

---

## Mystic Eye

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You create an invisible sensor at a point you can see within 60 feet. As a magic action for the spell's duration, and as part of casting this spell, you can view the area around that point as if you stood there. You can peer through keyholes, under doors, through cracks, and so on to peer at areas that would otherwise be out of view. The sensor perceives everything within 20 feet of it.

## Necrotic Mote

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You call forth a sphere of energy from the death plane, a mote of anti-life that drains energy from those near it. It is a 5-foot-diameter sphere of swirling, enraged spirits that appears within range. It has AC 15 and hit points equal to your level times 3. If it needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

Once during your turn, you can move the mote once up to 30 feet. It can move both horizontally and vertically. At the end of each of your turns, up to 1d3 creatures within 5 feet of the mote take 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier + half your level necrotic damage.

---

## Rolling Thunder

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an orb of rumbling thunder energy and send it bouncing along the ground toward your enemies. Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 thunder damage, plus additional d8s equal to half your level, and is knocked prone. On a miss, the target takes half damage.

If the attack hits, you may repeat it against another creature within range. You cannot target the same creature more than once with this spell.

---

## Siren's Call

**Level:** Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You bewitch a creature's senses, filling it with the urge to draw closer to you. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you pull it and it is charmed by you until the end of its next turn.

**Level 5.** You can target up to two creatures.

**Level 9.** You can target up to three creatures.

## Spell Storm

**Level:** Greater Cantrip

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a storm of energy into a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Creatures in that area must make Dexterity saving throws. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 damage per level. On a successful save, it takes no damage. When you cast this spell, you pick the damage type from among acid, cold, fire, or thunder.

**Level 6.** You now target two areas in range when you cast this spell.

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

One willing creature you touch gains several additional spider legs that emerge from its body, granting it the ability to move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving its hands free. It gains a spider climb speed.

## Temporal Bubble

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

Choose a willing creature within range. The target slips into the space between the seconds, allowing it to think and act with incredible speed. On its turn, the target gains the following benefits:

- It can move one additional time.
- It can use the dash, hide, or utilize actions without using an action, or it can use the attack action but is limited to making only one attack.

## Vapors of the Rotting Mire

**Level:** 5

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You create a cloud of yellow, nauseating gas that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. The cloud provides heavy obscurement.

Each living creature that starts its turn in the cloud must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d6 poison damage + 1d6 per level above 5 and is dazed for that turn. On a successful save, it takes half that damage and is not dazed. A dazed creature can either use an action or move on its turn.

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

**Level 5.** You can target two areas.

**Level 9.** You can target three areas.

## Void of the Forsaken

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

You unravel the primordial bounds of the world to channel a shard of darkness from the great prison where unspeakable, primordial horrors from before the dawn of time are imprisoned. A 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range becomes cloaked in impenetrable darkness. Darkvision can't see through it. If the area is exposed to daylight, this spell ends but all creatures in the area and less than 20 feet from the edge of the area take 4d10 psychic damage as they capture a brief glimpse of the realm from which you drew this darkness.

Creatures treat the area as difficult terrain, as it creates a fear response even among undead or constructs. They must make a Wisdom saving throw using your spell save DC to enter it.

## Water Breathing

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 24 hours

This spell grants creatures of your choice within range the ability to breathe underwater until the spell ends. Affected creatures also retain their normal mode of breathing.

## Weaver's Web

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Attack Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration

If the web takes fire damage, it is destroyed and creatures in its area take 2d6 fire damage.

## Wind Disk

**Level:** 3

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You conjure a buffeting wind that swirls under you in the rough form of a disk, allowing you to ride it into the air. When you move, you can move vertically straight up or down. You can end your turn in midair. You do not fall unless you wish to and take no damage from falls. You cannot move horizontally unless you are on a solid surface.

---

## Worm Hole

**Level:** 7

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 1 minute

You use your magic to burrow through the firmament of reality, allowing you and others to cover a distance in one step. You teleport to a point you choose within range when you cast this spell, and as part of teleporting create spatial distortions in the area where you cast this spell and in your destination area (each a 10-foot-radius sphere). Once during a creature's turn, you can cause that creature to teleport from the area one distortion occupies to the other. The creature must allow you to teleport it in this manner.

---

## Zephyr Steps

**Level:** 1

**Casting Time:** Magic Action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 10 minutes

Until the spell ends, you can take the Dash action on your turn without using an action. While walking during your turn, you do not fall unless you wish to do so. You can walk horizontally and downward vertically but not upward through the air. You fall at the end of your turn if you stand in midair and cannot otherwise remain aloft.
