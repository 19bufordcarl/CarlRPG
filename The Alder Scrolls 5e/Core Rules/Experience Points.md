# Experience Points

At the end of each session, the GM awards each PC a number of XP points between 1 and 10 depending on the events that occurred that session. When the PCs reach 10 XP, they can level up the next time the GM determines there is sufficient reason in the fiction of the world to have the PCs gain power. This may be because they have a period of extended downtime and training, or it could be because they have absorbed the soul of a dead wizard.

As the party reaches higher levels, gaining XP may require more heroic tasks, and potentially even the acquiring of magical items or tutoring from powerful sages to unlock the highest levels of power. The exact nature will vary in any campaign, but expect gaining levels to require a justification in the fiction of the world. Until that requirement is met, as determined by the GM, a PC will wait at 10 XP being "ready" to level up when the moment presents itself.
