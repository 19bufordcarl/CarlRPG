# Stealth

You can try to evade detection by moving in the shadows and keeping quiet.

## Hide

You can attempt to hide as an action.

## Requirements

You can hide if you meet **at least one** of the following requirements:

- **Sight.** A creature is unable to see you, such as if you are behind a wall, invisible, or in darkness.
- **Cover.** If a creature could see you, you have cover against it.
- **Inattention.** If a creature could see you, the DM rules it is not looking where you are hiding.

You must also meet this requirement for keeping quiet:

- **Sound.** You avoid making noise louder than a whisper.

## Check

You make a Dexterity (Stealth) check. The DC is normally 15, but the DM can adjust the DC up or down depending on how easy it is to hide in an area. Typically, foes with magical senses such as blindsight will have a higher DC required to foil their heightened senses of perception.

## Result

If you succeed, record your result. You are hidden if you continue to meet the requirements given above. If you fail, you do not become hidden.

## Hidden

While you are Hidden, Hostile and Indifferent creatures cannot target you with attacks, spells, and other effects. They are unaware of your location, though they may suspect your presence. You have advantage on attacks against creatures you are Hidden from.

## Becoming Revealed

You are no longer hidden if you target a creature you are hidden from with any attack, effect, or spell. You are also no longer hidden if you fail to meet the requirements for hiding. You might also be revealed if a creature makes a check to find you.

**On Another Creature's Turn.** If you are revealed during another creature's turn, you are immediately no longer hidden from it and anyone it alerts to your presence.

**On Your Turn.** If you are revealed during your own turn, you can't use hide actions for the rest of the turn and no longer gain the benefits of being hidden at the end of your turn. You still gain the benefits during your turn, as your foes struggle to react.
