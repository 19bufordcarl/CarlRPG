# Stealth

## Hide

To Hide, you can use your action make a Dexterity (Stealth) check. The result determines if you become Hidden. It also determines the DC of any checks made to locate you. If a result specifies you are Hidden from a special sense, those senses cannot automatically foil your stealth. Creatures with them must Search to find you.

**15 to 19 — Partially Hidden.** You are Hidden from creatures while you are Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters or Total Cover. You are Hidden from Darkvision. The DC to find you is 15.

**20 to 29 — Hidden.** You are Hidden from creatures while you are Lightly or Heavily Obscured or behind any cover. You are Hidden from Darkvision and Tremorsense. The DC to find you is 25.

**30 or Higher — Completely Hidden.** You are Hidden from creatures while you are Lightly or Heavily Obscured or behind any cover. You are Hidden from Blindsight, Darkvision, Tremorsense, and Truesight. The DC to find you is 30.

## Hidden

While you are Hidden, Hostile and Indifferent creatures cannot target you with attacks, spells, and other effects. They are unaware of your location, though they may suspect your presence. You have advantage on attacks against creatures you are Hidden from.

## Remaining Hidden

You must meet requirements to remain Hidden based on the result of your Hide action. You lose this condition if you target a creature you are Hidden from with any attack, effect, or spell.

If you lose this condition on your turn, you immediately lose the benefits of being Hidden, but you retain the Hidden condition until the end of your turn and cannot become Hidden or Hide again that turn.

**Noise.** The DM may judge that you made enough noise to lose this condition.

**Line of Sight.** The DM may judge that a creature is distracted or not looking in your direction, allowing you to remain or become Hidden even if it would normally spot you.

## Passive Perception

Passive Perception is meant as an *optional* tool to speed up play. It catches situations where the characters would be vigilant to danger, but the DM does not want to call for endless rolls to check every doorknob, corner, or shadow for hidden dangers.

**Start of Encounters.** Before rolling initiative, the DM checks the Passive Perception against each Hidden creature that they could detect. Treat the Passive Perception as the result of a check made as part of the Search action to detect hidden creatures. A creature is no longer Hidden when the encounter begins if it would be detected via Passive Perception. If all the Hidden creatures on one side are detected, they cannot surprise their opponents.

**Speeding Up Stealth.** When a Hidden creature moves through an area, assume that any creatures on watch are using the Search action each round to detect intruders. Use their Passive Perception instead of rolling to determine if they spot a Hidden character.

**Traps.** If a PC would trigger a trap, use Passive Perception to see if a character notices it. The DM can subtract 12 from the DC needed to spot the trap to determine the trap's Stealth bonus. Then the DM rolls d20 + its Stealth bonus, using Passive Perception as the DC. If the trap succeeds, it triggers. If it fails, the characters spot it before triggering it.
