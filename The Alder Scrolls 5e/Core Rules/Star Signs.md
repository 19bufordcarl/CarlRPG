# Star Signs

Mortals born under a certain sign are thought to have certain characteristics granted by the celestial realms. The year has twelve months, each watched over by a sign. The thirteenth sign, the Serpent, has no month and wanders, thought by many to represent a cursed birth.

---

## The Ritual

*The first month, known as Morning Star and the bringer of the new year.*

**Blessed Touch.** You have an innate connection to the mystical powers of the celestial realm and can calm someone's nerves with a simple touch and kind word. You are responsible and motivated by duty to your word and oaths.

## The Lover

*The second month, known as Sun's Dawn and the herald of a hopeful spring to come.*

**Mooncalf.** You are a hopeless romantic and live for the drama. You care deeply for those close to you and love heavily, even when it gets you in trouble. You are never slow to defend what you love, and will fight if that's what it takes to safeguard it.

## The Lord

*The third month, known as First Seed and the bringer of the spring equinox.*

**Kings Blood.** You are arrogant and haughty, but also highly capable and often correct, however frustrating that is for those around you. You have lofty ambitions and philosophize about the nature of mortals often. Ultimately, you seek to improve the lives of those around you, but it doesn't always come across that way. Sometimes its because you are lost. Usually its because others are too blind to see the obviously correct path you laid out.

## The Mage

*The fourth month, known as Rain's Hand and the bringer of life to nature.*

**Fay.** You are focused on the nature of reality and seek to understand great mysteries. The world is a puzzle to you, and like any puzzle it can be solved, and you will solve it. You get bored easily, and are always looking for your next challenge.

## The Shadow

*The fifth month, known as Second Seed and the beginning of summer.*

**Moonshadow.** You are quiet and reserved, yet observant. You often go unnoticed, but rarely fail to notice something out of the ordinary. You are ever shifting and elusive to others.

## The Steed

*The sixth month, known as Midyear and the bringer of the summer solstice.*

**Charioteer.** You are bold and charismatic, and have the energy to go great distances to get what you want. You never stop until you have what you want.

## The Apprentice

*The seventh month, known as Sun's Height and the peak of summer and festivals.*

**Elfborn.** You are always curious and humble about your knowledge, never feeling content with what you know, ever yearning for more. You often take on new problems and the problems of others even before figuring out your own.

## The Warrior

*The eighth month, known as Last Seed and the end of summer.*

**Warwyrd.** You are a protector and guardian. You have ambitions to stand out from the crowd, but also have warmth to care for those you love.

## The Lady

*The ninth month, known as Hearthfire and the bringer of the autumnal equinox.*

**Lady's Favor.** You have elegant and almost regal presence about you. You always try to have a solution for every problem, and attempt to right any ship that seems to veer off course. You judge harshly, but with care and intent to improve it. Something that isn't working is harm waiting to happen.

## The Tower

*The tenth month, known as Frostfall and the end of autumn and harvest festivals.*

**Tower Key.** You are solitary by nature. You often avoid conflict, retreating into yourself or your hobbies and fantasies to avoid confrontation. You are stubborn, but underneath it all you have a strong moral compass and eye for the truth. You see all the sides of a disagreement and, given time, can find the path towards consensus.

## The Atronach

*The eleventh month, known as Sun's Dusk and the beginning of winter.*

**Wombburn.** You are guarded and reserved. You put up walls for protection and it works. You absorb the energy of those around you. But, you can lower your walls when you feel safe. Inside, there is a great warmth, power, and ability to connect with others, should you choose to embrace it.

## The Thief

*The twelfth month, known as Evening Star and the bringer of the winter solstice.*

**Hara.** You are cunning and clever, and always looking for the next thing to add to your collection. You have an eye for beauty and aren't afraid to be the best. You are outgoing and friendly, but also a little untrustworthy.

## The Serpent

*The Serpent has no month. It wanders through the sky at will, unbound to the calendar, and its appearance is said to herald upheaval.*

**Star-Cursed.** You are severed spiritually from others, but yearn to be connected. You are comfortable in silence and emptiness. You feel a latent power rests deep within you, destined to be awoken one day. Until then, you can't help but envy the connection shared by those around you. One day, you can't help but feel that everything will change.
