# Character Creation

Use the following steps to create a character.

## The Six Abilities

**Strength** measures physical might and power.

**Dexterity** measures agility, coordination, reflexes, and balance.

**Constitution** gauges health and durability.

**Intelligence** reflects logical reasoning, memory, and knowledge.

**Wisdom** measures intuition, insight, and willpower.

**Charisma** reflects personal magnetism and charm.

## Determine Abilities

Roll six d6s and allocate each die to a different ability, choosing how to allocate them or doing it at random. Use the table below to determine their abilities. Then allocate 2 points to your abilities, with each point granting +1. You can allocate both points to the same ability for a +2 bonus.

| d6  | Ability Score |
| --- | ------------- |
| 1   | -1            |
| 2   | +0            |
| 3-4 | +1            |
| 5-6 | +2            |

If you did not roll at least one 5 on your six d6 rolls, you may re-roll.

## Class

Choose one of the available [Classes](Classes.md) that fits with your character's abilities.

## Ancestry

You can pick one of the ancestries listed below. Your size is Medium and your creature type is humanoid. You know the Common language and one language or dialect that makes sense in the world as determined by both you and the DM.

### Catfolk

**Nocturnal.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Claws.** You can use your claws as natural weapons for your Unarmed Strikes. They have the finesse property and you count as being proficient with them when attacking. They deal 1d6 slashing damage on a hit.

### Elf

**Elven Background.** Pick one of the following options.

- **Fleet of Foot.** You gain proficiency with Dexterity saves and one related skill. If your class grants you proficiency with such saves or skills, you instead gain expertise with them.
- **Resilient Body.** You gain proficiency with Constitution saves and one related skill. If your class grants you proficiency with such saves, you instead gain expertise with them.
- **Wizened Mind.** You gain proficiency with Wisdom saves and one related skill. If your class grants you proficiency with such saves, you instead gain expertise with them.

**Arcane Soul.** You know one cantrip of your choice from any spell list. Choose any stat as your casting stat for it.

### Human

**Jack of all Trades.** Increase your proficiency bonus by +1.

### Lizardfolk

**Scales.** Your scales give you +1 AC.

**Amphibious.** You can breathe both water and air.

**Reptilian.** You gain resistance to poison damage.

### Orc

**Orcish Determination.** On your turn, you can enter a focused, determined state. For the next 10 minutes you gain advantage on all attacks, checks, and saving throws. You also gain a bonus to damage rolls equal to half your level (minimum +1). Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Indomitable.** On your turn, you can tap into a well of energy and toughness. You regain 1d8 + your Constitution modifier hit points. This amount increases by 1d8 when you reach level 5 (2d8) and 9 (3d8). Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Background

You have decided to embark on a life of adventure. Why grind away at a mundane career when you can risk life and limb in search of treasures hidden in monster infested holes in the ground? You background determines your skills and languages.

### Skills

You select one of the skill packages listed below.

- **Merchant**: Deception, Insight, Performance, Persuasion
- **Scholar**: Arcana, History, Medicine, Religion
- **Soldier**: Athletics, Intimidation, Medicine, Perception
- **Thief**: Athletics, Investigation, Sleight of Hand, Stealth
- **Wanderer**: Animal Handling, Nature, Perception, Survival

With your DM's permission, you can create your own skill package by picking four skills.

Expertise and Ancestry. If your background gives you a skill granted by your ancestry, you gain expertise with that skill.

### Languages

You know Common. The DM may provide you a list of languages in their world that scholar's might reasonably study. If they do, you may choose a number of those languages to learn equal to to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0).

#### Old Scrolls Default Languages

Common, High Elvish, Dark Elvish, Wood Elvish, Orcish, Saxhleel, Taagra, and Daedric.

## Equipment

You start with a belt pouch, and a pack of choice. There is one pack for each of the 5 standard backgrounds; bard's pack, scholar's pack, soldier's pack, thieves' pack, and a wanderer's pack. Each contains basic adventurer equipment like torches and rations along with a few special items tailored to their specific context. Your class grants you starting weapons and armor.

| Pack            | Bonus Specialty Items                                                 |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Merchant's Pack | Scale, Lockbox, Fine Clothes, Mirror                                  |
| Scholar's Pack  | Book, Ink pen, 10 pieces of parchment and envelopes, Signet ring, Wax |
| Soldier's Pack  | Crowbar, Rope (50'), 8 oil flasks                                     |
| Thieves' Pack   | Crowbar, Thieves' Tools, Bag of ball bearings, 10 caltrops            |
