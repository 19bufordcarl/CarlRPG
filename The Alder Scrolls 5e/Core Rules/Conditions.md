# Conditions

Various spells and monster attacks imbue conditions on a character.

## Asleep

While asleep you can't do anything on your turn. Your class features provide no benefit and your spells that require concentration end. You wake up if you take damage or if the DM judges that a sound or other event is enough to rouse you. Your AC is 2 while you are asleep and attacks that hit you are critical hits.

## Blinded

A blinded creature cannot see.

## Charmed

A charmed creature is friendly toward the creature that charmed it. It does not necessarily obey its orders but cannot target it with attacks, offensive abilities, or anything else that harms or hinders it.

## Dazed

A dazed creature can either move or use its action on its turn, not both.

## Dying

A creature with 0 hit points is dying and is vulnerable to taking death saves.

## Frightened

A frightened creature cannot willingly move closer to the creature or effect that frightened it.

## Goaded

A creature that is goaded has disadvantage on attacks against creatures that are not goading it.

## Grappled

A grappled creature cannot move further than 5 feet away from the creature or effect that is grappling it. In place of moving, a creature can make an opposed check using Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) to break free.

## Hidden

While you are Hidden, Hostile and Indifferent creatures cannot target you with attacks, spells, and other effects. They are unaware of your location, though they may suspect your presence. You have advantage on attacks against creatures you are Hidden from.

## Invisible

You are treated as Heavily Obscured for purposes of becoming or remaining Hidden. Attacks against you have disadvantage unless the attacker has means allowing them to see through your invisibility.

## Paralyzed

While paralyzed you can't do anything on your turn. Your AC is 2 while you are paralyzed and attacks that hit you are critical hits.

## Prone

While prone instead of moving you can stand up. You can choose to fall prone in place of moving on your turn. Melee attacks against you have advantage.

## Unconscious

While unconscious you can't do anything on your turn. Your class features cannot benefit other creatures and effects that require concentration end. Your AC is 2 while you are unconscious and attacks that hit you are critical hits.
