# Influence

You urge a creature to do something. Describe or roleplay how you're communicating with the monster. Are you trying to deceive, intimidate, amuse, or gently persuade? The DM then determines what happens next.

## Skills

Influence usually uses Charisma and one of the following skills:

- **Animal Handling** allows you to sway a beast or a creature of similar intelligence.
- **Deception** allows you to trick a creature.
- **Intimidation** uses threats and fear to force a creature to act.
- **Performance** is useful for manipulating crowds or creating distractions.
- **Persuasion** uses reason and logic to sway a creature.

The DM determines the appropriate ability and skill based on the situation.

## Traits

Traits determine how a creature reacts to your influence attempt. They are a creature's core beliefs and desires. Traits have a modifier, like abilities, that ranges from +2 up to +8.

**Willing.** If your urging aligns with the monster's traits, no check is necessary. The monster fulfills your request as it sees fit.

**Unwilling.** If your urging is counter to its traits or goals, it doesn't comply.

**Hesitant.** If you urge the monster to do something that its traits make it hesitant to do, you make a check opposed by the trait's bonus.

On a successful check, the creature fulfills your request within the bounds of its personality and goals as determined by the DM.

**Scope of Influence.** During combat, you can get a creature to change its actions for the next round.

- Intimidate a creature into moving away from you.
- Use Deception to lure a creature into moving closer to you.
- Use Performance to distract a creature so your allies can hide.
- Persuade a creature into a truce.
- Use Animal Handling to distract a creature with food.

**Influencing Multiple Creatures.** You can always influence one creature but can attempt to influence up to a number equal to your Charisma modifier. Performance allows you to sway every creature in your area. Note that if you influence an enemy leader, their actions or decisions might sway all your enemies into taking a course of action.
