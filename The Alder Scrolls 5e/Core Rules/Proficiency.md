# Proficiency

Where the abilities describe your natural talents, your proficiency reflects your training and experience. Your proficiency is a modifier that usually ranges from +2 to +6. The rules specify when to apply it during the game.

## Expertise

You might acquire expertise in a proficiency. If you do so, add your proficiency bonus twice when it applies to a roll and you have expertise.

***Why Not Double Proficiency?** Throughout these rules, you'll notice that we avoid doubling any values. Instead, you are told to add something twice. This approach means you never have to worry about the order in which you apply bonuses or penalties.*

## Skill Proficiencies

Most checks involve using a skill. A check specifies which skill applies to it.

If you are proficient in a skill, you apply your proficiency bonus to checks involving it.

Note that skills are not tied to specific abilities. You might make a Strength (Athletics) check to push a boulder off a path, then a Dexterity (Athletics) check to walk across a narrow, swaying bridge.

**Acrobatics.** This skill covers speed, agility, balance, and grace.

**Animal Handling.** Animal Handling allows you to interact with creatures that lack any language by using sounds, gestures, and your intuition to read their bearing and attitude.

**Arcana.** Arcana is the study of arcane magic, the planes of existence, and magic items. It allows you to recall the lore and analyze magical phenomena.

**Athletics.** Athletics represents strength and endurance training.

**Deception.** This skill is the art of masking your true intentions as you mislead others.

**History.** This skill improves your ability to recall key facts about the past and study ruins and relics of bygone eras.

**Insight.** Insight gives you a sense of a creature's attitude toward you and others. Its main use is detecting falsehoods or attitudes behind a statement.

**Intimidation.** Intimidation allows you to strike fear in other creatures.

**Investigation.** The Investigation skill allows you to connect seemingly unrelated information, such as physical clues, to understand the true nature of an object or event.

**Medicine.** This skill is the study of anatomy, poisons, medicines, and other elements related to providing medical care to other creatures.

**Nature.** Nature is the study of the natural world. It gives you insight into the nature of wild animals, plants, and other elements of the wilderness.

**Perception.** Perception represents the acuity of your senses and your ability to sift through noise and sights to focus on the telling elements of your environment.

**Performance.** The Performance skill represents training in acting, music, and other types of performance, allowing you to entertain or distract a large audience.

**Persuasion.** This skill represents your ability to sway others into agreeing with you or aligning with your views.

**Religion.** The Religion skill focuses on the lore about the gods, divine magic, religious relics, and the history of various sects and cults.

**Sleight of Hand.** The Sleight of Hand skill represents focused training on fine manual dexterity. It allows you to manipulate small objects, such as locks and traps.

**Stealth.** This skill allows you to move quietly and without drawing attention to yourself.

**Survival.** The Survival skill represents a broad set of training in surviving in the wilderness, including tracking.

## Weapon Proficiencies

Weapon proficiency represents training with a type of weapon. When you attack with a weapon, you add your proficiency bonus to the d20 roll if you are proficient with the weapon.

**Specific Weapons.** Sometimes you gain proficiency with a specific weapon. Your proficiency bonus applies to attacks made with that weapon.

**Weapon Categories.** You might gain proficiency with a category of weapons. When you do so, you are proficient with all weapons in that category.
