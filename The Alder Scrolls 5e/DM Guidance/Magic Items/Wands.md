# Wands

Wands contain arcane power that a magic-user can tap into. Each wand specifies a spell. You must have the spell on your class's list to use the wand.

When using a wand, use your spellcasting ability for the wand's required spell and your proficiency bonus to determine its effects, including save DCs, attack bonus, and so on.

## Wand of Detection

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Detect Magic

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can point the wand in a space up to 300 feet away. You hear noises as if you stood at that point.

As an action, you can cast detect magic.

## Wand of Flames

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Fireball

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can cause a flame equal to that of a torch to sprout from its end. It casts dim light in a 30-foot radius and can be used to ignite flammable material.

As an action, you can cast a reduced strength fireball. The spell's damage is 4d6. If any of the damage dice roll a 1, the wand overheats and none of its features can be used for 10 minutes.

## Wand of Glamours

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Disguise Self

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can cast disguise self.

As an action, you can create an illusion that fills an area up to 20 feet on each side. The illusion can include sounds and odors, but it fails to hold up to physical inspection. It lasts for up to 1 hour or until you use this feature of the wand again.

## Wand of Lightning

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Lightning Bolt

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can fire a single bolt of electricity from the wand. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within 300 feet. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 lightning damage, or 4d6 lightning damage if you are level 5 or higher.

As an action, you can cast a reduced strength lightning bolt. The spell's damage is 4d6. If any of the damage dice roll a 1, the wand generates a backlash of energy, leaving it unusable for 10 minutes.

## Wand of Protection

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Mage Armor

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you gain the benefits of using a shield (+2 AC).

As an action, you can cast mage armor.

## Wand of Terror

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Fear

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you can alter your voice, granting you a ferocious growl that confers a +2 bonus to all Intimidation checks.

As an action, you can point the wand at a creature within 300 feet and force it to make a Wisdom save. If it fails, the creature is frightened of you. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

## Wand of Unerring Darts

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Magic Missile

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you gain a +1 bonus to all spell attack rolls.

The wand has a reservoir of 5 charges. As an action, you can cast magic missile. Expend charges from the wand to create a spell slot used to cast the spell. The wand regains 1 charge per hour.

## Wand of Vigilance

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Alarm

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you add your Intelligence modifier as a bonus to initiative rolls.

As an action, you can cast alarm.
