## Level 1: Warlock Pact

You have through either ritual, pact, or sheer force of will, gleamed a power from a supernatural entity that is beyond mortal comprehension. Choose one of the pacts listed at the end of this class description which will grant you your eldritch powers.

Choose either Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your primary ability for your pact. Your attack rolls for pact features is equal to your proficiency plus your primary ability. You save DC for your pact features is equal to 8 + proficiency bonus + primary ability.

## Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

# Warlock Pacts

## Great Old One

A mysterious entity whose nature is utterly foreign and beyond the very fabric of reality. You gain the following abilities which you can use at will.

**Level 1: Mind Spike.** Choose a target within 30 feet, they must make an Intelligence save or take 1d6 psychic damage per warlock level.

**Level 1: Awakened Mind.** You can telepathically speak to any creature you can see within 30 feet of you regardless of whether you share any languages.

**Level 5: Detect Thoughts.** You sense the presence of thoughts within 30 feet that belong to creatures that know languages or are telepathic.

You don't read the thoughts, but you know that a thinking creature is present.

If you can see a creature, as an action or when you cast this spell you can force it to make a Wisdom save. If it fails, you search its thoughts and learn the answer to one question you ask it. You learn the truth as the creature understands it.

**Level 5: Thought Shield.** All creatures within 30 feet of you must make an Intelligence save or take 1d6 psychic damage and cannot move until the start of your next turn.

**Level 9: Mind Stun.** All creatures within 30 feet of you must make an Intelligence save or take 1d6 psychic damage and cannot move until the start of your next turn.

**Level 9: Telekinesis.** You can try to move a Huge or smaller creature or similar size object. Make an ability check with your primary ability contested by the creature's Strength check, objects fail automatically. If you succeed, you can move the creature up to 30 feet in any direction. Until the end of your next turn, a creature who fails their save is also restrained. Reminder: Creatures take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet they fall.

## Undying One

An entity of great necromantic power and unnaturally long life who holds secrets of the world innumerable and utterly profane.

**Level 1: Death's Touch.** Make a melee attack using your primary ability against a target within 10 feet. They take 1d8 necrotic damage per warlock level.

**Level 1: False Life.** Once on your turn, you can regain 1 hit point by siphoning energy from another living creature within 5 feet of you. They lose 1 hit point.

**Level 5: Necromantic Ritual.** You summon a cursed spirit to aid you in combat. The spirit has the following statistics.

>**AC** 13 — **HP** 15 — **Tests** +1 (Cha +4) — 40 feet (Fly)
>
>**Incorporeal.** Can move through but cannot end a turn inside a solid object.
>
>**Insubstantial (16+).** The first time this creature takes damage on a turn, roll a d20. On 16+ it takes no damage that turn.
>
>**Initiative 10: Necrotic Aura.** Hostile creatures in melee range must make a Constitution save using the warlock's save DC. **Failure:** 1d4 necrotic damage per 2 warlock levels, and frightened of the spirit for 1 round. **Success:** Half damage

**Level 5: Ray of Sickness.** One target you can see within 60 feet must make a Constitution save. On a failure, they take 2d10 necrotic damage and have disadvantage on all rolls until the end of your next turn.

**Level 9: Ritual of the Vampire.** You undergo the death ritual to become a vampire, dying in a coffin and being reborn in blood. You no longer require food, water, or sleep. Instead, you must consume the blood of a living or recently dead humanoid creature.

In return, you gain resistance to damage from non-magical weapons, resistance to cold damage, +2 to all ability scores, bypassing the normal maximum of +5. When you would die from reaching 3 death save failures, you do not die unless you take radiant damage, are in sunlight, or are killed in your coffin. Instead, you turn into mist and return to the coffin where you performed the ritual of the vampire.

As a cost, you are vulnerable to radiant damage, fire damage, and damage from silvered weapons. While in direct sunlight, you take 5 radiant damage at the start of your turn (doubled to 10). A hooded cloak can be worn to avoid this effect in normal daylight.

## Cryptid

This entity is an evil and malevolent creature. A bedtime story told to children to discourage them from going out into the dark woods. Except these dark creatures and the mythical powers they say to wield are not always as fake as they may seem. Sometimes, there really is a monster under your bed, outside your window, or watching you from behind that tree…

**Level 1: Curse of Lycanthropy.** Under the cover of darkness, you transform into a werewolf, or similar hybrid perversion of nature. You gain an insatiable bloodlust for the hearts of humanoid creatures. If you do not consume a heart by sunrise, you take a death save failure.

While transformed, you gain a +3 to your Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution, bypassing the normal maximum of +5. Your unarmed strikes deal a base 2d6 damage. You also have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, unless it is coming from a silvered weapon, or more powerful magic weapon.

**Level 1: Beast Sense.** While transformed or not transformed, you have advantage on perception checks, and your sense of hearing, smell, and sight are unmatched. You cannot be surprised and have blindsight out to 30 feet.

**Level 5: Bloodthirsty Lycanthrope.** You can transform into a werewolf at will. If it is daylight, this transformation lasts only 1 hour and you take a death save failure if it ends without you consuming a humanoid heart.

**Level 5: Extra Attack.** When you attack, you can make two unarmed attacks.

**Level 9: Elder Lycanthrope.** You are a master of the rituals of the hunt and know how to convert others to become a lycanthrope by sharing in the beast blood ritual. You can grant the Curse of Lycanthropy and Beast Sense to creatures using this ability. Once cursed in this way, you do not know how to lift the curse from a creature.

**Level 9: Extra Attack.** When you attack, you can make three unarmed attacks.
