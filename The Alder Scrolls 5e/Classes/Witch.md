## Level 1: Witch Magic

As a witch, you can learn different types of ancient and eldritch magic called rites. These are a specific type of spell that do not have levels. Intelligence is your casting ability for your rites.

**Hexes** are bound to a creature's soul. **Enchantments** are bound to an object. **Runes** are bound to a location. **Divinations** are unbound, they derived from the stars and the realms beyond.

You can learn a number of rites equal to your Intelligence. You can change the rites you know with different rites whenever you finish a period of extended downtime.

## Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 9: Bestow Curse

You have mastered the profane magics of witchcraft, allowing you bestow a hex upon a humanoid for whom you know their true name. The hex requires an hour long ritual with several prepared components including at least the victim's blood, bones, and a cauldron. The DM adjudicates how effective the curse is and may call for a primary ability check to determine the results.

# Witch Rites

## Augury

**Rite: Divination**

You receive an omen from mystical forces about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The DM chooses whether to answer weal, woe, or a mix of the two, depending on the most likely outcome of the action.

## Blessed Water

**Rite: Enchantment**

You touch a bottle of water, blessing it with pure magic. When a creature drinks the water, they regain 2d8 + your spellcasting bonus hit points.

## Death Curse

**Rite: Hex**

Choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. From now until they die, whenever they take damage, they take an additional 1d6 necrotic damage. At level 9 this becomes 2d6 necrotic damage.

## Detect Magic

**Rite: Divination**

You see the presence of magical effects within 30 feet on objects that are visible to you. Magic appears as a faint aura, the details of which allow you to determine its origin and the basic details of its effect.

## Fear Curse

**Rite: Hex**

Choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. They must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, from now until they die, whenever they see you, they see their deepest fear instead. They must drop whatever they are holding and are frightened of you as long as you remain within line of sight of them.

A creature can repeat the saving throw if they see you unconscious or if they are not a humanoid they may repeat it at the end of their turn.

## Hallowed Ground

**Rite: Rune**

You can take 1 minute to prepare a 60-foot-radius circle on the ground of benevolent eldritch magic. When a creature within the circle makes an attack roll or saving throw, they may add 1d4 to the roll.

This magic ends after 1 hour.

## Hexed Ground

**Rite: Rune**

You can take 1 minute to prepare a 60-foot-radius circle on the ground of malevolent eldritch magic. When a creature within the circle makes an attack roll or saving throw, you may subtract 1d4 from the roll.

This magic ends after 1 hour.

## Hexed Water

**Rite: Enchantment**

You touch a bottle of water, corrupting it with foul magic. When a creature drinks the water, they start convulsing uncontrollably. They are effectively paralyzed by this until the end of your next turn.

## Spirit Circle

**Rite: Rune**

You can take 1 minute to prepare a 10-foot-radius circle on the ground of eldritch magic. Within this circle, only creatures that you designate when you cast this spell may enter it. If another creature wishes to enter, you may deal 2d12 necrotic damage per witch level to them at will. You can choose to deal this damage once per turn (not just your turn).

While within this circle, you cannot be charmed, frightened, or possessed by creatures outside the circle.

This magic ends after 1 hour.
