## Level 1: Spellcasting

You can cast spells from the Druid spell list. Wisdom is your casting ability. You know two cantrips.

You prepare a number of spells equal to your Intelligence modifier. You may change your prepared spells after an extended period of downtime.

You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest. Once per day, you may regain 1 expended spell slot when you finish a short rest.

## Level 1: Wild Shape

As an action, you can transform into a beast you have seen. While transformed, you use the beast's AC, hit points, and Speed, but retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma, and your spellcasting ability. If you are reduced to 0 HP while in your beast form, you return to your human form with your prior HP, taking any excess damage. You also take a death saving throw failure. You can't cast spells while transformed.

You can remain transformed for up to 1 hour, and you can transform back as an action. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short rest.

Several beast forms are detailed at the bottom of the class description. A form requires the druid be a certain level to transform into them. When you transform, any non-metal items you are wearing or carrying can meld into your new form, while metallic items drop to the ground.

## Level 1: Beast Bond

Your druidic magic has taught you how to perform a ritual that will grant you the ability to speak with animals. You must perform a ritual that takes 1 minute in order to activate this ability, which then lasts for 1 hour.

## Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Wild Shape Forms

Each form lists a level. You can only transform into a form whose level is less than or equal to your druid level.

**Damage.** The feature lists the damage dice an attack uses. You also may add the form's Strength or Dexterity modifier to the roll, whichever is higher.

**Saving Throws.** The saving throw DC of any beast form feature that requires one is 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus.

# Druid Spell List

Spells descriptions are listed below.

---

## Cure Wounds

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Touch

A creature you touch regains 2d8 + your spellcasting bonus hit points.

The healing increases to 4d8 + your spellcasting bonus if you use a level 2 slot and 6d8 + your spellcasting bonus if you use a level 3 slot.

---

## Detect Magic

**Level:** 1

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You see the presence of magical effects within 30 feet on objects that are visible to you. Magic appears as a faint aura, the details of which allow you to determine its origin and the basic details of its effect.

---

## Entangle

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 90 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Grasping plants sprout from the ground in a 20-foot square within range. For the duration, these plants turn the ground in the area into difficult terrain. They disappear when the spell ends. Each creature (other than you) in the area when you cast the spell must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the restrained condition until the spell ends.

A restrained creature can take an action on their turn to repeat the save. On a success, it frees itself from the grasping plants and is no longer restrained by them.

---

## Fly

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target gains a fly speed.

When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft unless it can stop the fall.

---

## Levitate

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

One creature or loose object of your choice within range rises vertically up to 20 feet and remains suspended there. The spell can levitate an object that weighs up to 500 pounds. An unwilling creature that succeeds on a Constitution saving throw is unaffected.

The target can move only by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach (such as a wall or a ceiling), which allows it to move as if it were climbing. You can change the target's altitude by up to 20 feet in either direction on your turn. If you are the target, you can move up or down as part of your move. Otherwise, you can take an action to move the target, which must remain within the spell's range.

When the spell ends, the target floats gently to the ground if it is still aloft.

---

## Light

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** Touch

**Duration:** 1 hour

An object you touch sheds dim light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. The spell ends if you cast it again.

---

## Lightning Bolt

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 300 feet

You can make ranged spell attacks against up to three targets. The damage is 6d10 lightning damage if you attack three targets, 8d10 lightning damage if you attack two, or 10d10 lightning damage if you attack one. If the attack misses, the target takes half damage.

---

## Mending

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** Touch

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch. If the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

---

## Ray of Frost

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 60 feet

A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d8 cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 20 feet until after its next turn.

The damage increases to 2d8 cold damage at level 5 and 3d8 cold damage at level 9.

---

## Restorative Touch

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Touch

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: blinded, paralyzed, or poisoned.

---

## Revivify

**Level:** 3

**Range:** Touch

You touch a creature. If that creature died within the last minute, it returns to life, restores one failed death save, and regains 1 hit point. It cannot restore failed death saves again until it finishes a long rest.

---

## See Invisibility

**Level:** 2

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 hour

You see creatures and objects that are invisible as if they were visible.

---

## Silence

**Level:** 2

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

No sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the sphere has immunity to thunder damage. Spells cannot be cast in the spell's area.

---

## Thaumaturgy

**Level:** Cantrip

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Up to 1 minute

You manifest a minor wonder within range. You can create minor illusions, alter one of your physical traits, change the sound or volume of your voice, and otherwise make cosmetic changes to yourself or an object in range. The spell ends if you cast it again.

---

## Wall of Fog

**Level:** 1

**Range:** 120 feet

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You create a thick, billowing wall of fog that blocks sight and sound. It is one-foot thick, 10 feet high, and up to 30 feet long. The wall can bend and turn as you wish, but all of it must be within the spell's range when you create it.

---

## Water Breathing

**Level:** 3

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 24 hours

This spell grants up to ten willing creatures of your choice within range the ability to breathe underwater until the spell ends. Affected creatures also retain their normal mode of respiration.
