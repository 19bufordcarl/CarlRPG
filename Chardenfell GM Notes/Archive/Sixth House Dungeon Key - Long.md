# Key to the Sixth House Lair

## Dungeon Level 1

The entrance to the lair of the Sixth House lies hidden beneath the Bitter Coast's swampy earth. The air grows thick and heavy as you descend, carrying the faint scent of incense and decay. The walls here are a mixture of packed earth, ancient stonework, and the unmistakable signs of recent excavation—as if something has been burrowing upward from below. Strange symbols, half-glimpsed in the flickering torchlight, seem to writhe at the edges of your vision.

Wandering monsters are encountered whenever the DM deems appropriate.

| Roll | Encounter | Details |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Ash slaves (2-8) | AC 13; HP 13 (3d8); Atk 1x at +3 for (1d6+1) bludgeoning; immune to poison and disease |
| 2 | Dreamer cultists (1-4) | AC 12 (leather); HP 16 (3d8+3); Atk 1x at +3 for (1d6+1) or spell; spellcasting (1st level, DC 11) |
| 3 | Corprus stalker | AC 14; HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d8+2) piercing; DC 12 Con save or contract a wasting disease |
| 4 | Ash ghoul | AC 15 (natural); HP 30 (4d10+8); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d6+3) slashing; DC 13 Con save or be poisoned for 1 minute |
| 5 | Dreamer priests (1-4) | AC 13 (chain shirt); HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d6+2) or spell; spellcasting (2nd level, DC 12) |
| 6 | Ascended dreamer | AC 16 (natural); HP 39 (6d8+12); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d6+3) psychic damage; DC 14 Wis save or be frightened for 1 round |

The dungeon's air is unnaturally still. Sounds echo strangely, and the ever-present smell of ash and incense seems to seep into your very lungs. The deeper you go, the more you feel as though you are being watched—not by anything with eyes, but by something that exists in the space between moments.

## 1. Wooden Stairs

```boxed
The stairs descend into darkness, their wooden planks slick with moisture and what might be blood. The air grows colder with each step, and you begin to hear a faint, rhythmic thrumming—like a heartbeat, but wrong, too slow and too deep.
```

The muddy wooden stairs are treacherous. Characters who do not take special precautions (roping, etc.) must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity check or slip and tumble to the bottom. Characters who fall will knock any characters in front of them down as well (Dexterity check to avoid being knocked prone). The stairway descends for 60' before ending in the chamber below. No damage is taken from the fall, but the noise alerts the guards in room 2. If the party falls, the guards have advantage on initiative.

## 2. Entry Room

```boxed
The stairs open into a wide chamber of packed earth and rough stone. Four figures stand at attention, their eyes glassy and unfocused. They wear the tattered remnants of what might have once been fine robes, now stained with ash and age. They do not speak, but one of them turns its head slowly to regard you—and a thin, unsettling smile spreads across its face.
```

Four **dreamer cultists** (CR 1/4 each) guard the entrance. If characters have taken the rapid route to the bottom of the stairway, the cultists have advantage on initiative in the first round of combat.

```statblock
**Dreamer Cultist (4):** AC 12; HP 13 (3d8); Atk 1x at +3 for (1d6+1) or spell (DC 11); darkvision 60'; immune to sleep and frightened conditions
```

Their eyes are accustomed to the dim light—this chamber is unlit. They have no treasure, but each wears a simple pewter amulet bearing the symbol of the Sixth House (worth 5 gp each).

## 3. Chamber of the Dreamer

The wooden door to this room is swollen from the dampness, requiring a DC 12 Strength check to open.

```boxed
This room is filled with a thick, pungent smoke that stings your eyes and throat. In the center of the chamber, a brazier of black iron burns with an eerie green flame. Figures kneel around it—six of them, swaying slightly as if in a trance. One of them, an old Dark Elf with sunken eyes, turns to face you. His mouth opens, but the voice that emerges is not his own.

"The Sleeper has seen you coming. You are... welcome."
```

Six **dreamer cultists** (CR 1/4) are in a trance around the brazier. The leader of the group is a **dreamer priest** (CR 1). If the party is quiet (DC 14 Stealth) they can surprise them; otherwise, the priest will use his first action to cast *command* ("Kneel") on the most armored-looking character.

```statblock
**Dreamer Priest (1):** AC 13; HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d6+2) or spell (DC 12); spells: *command*, *bane*, *inflict wounds*, *hold person*
```

**Treasure:** The brazier is made of black iron and worth 50 gp to the right collector. The priest wears a silver ring worth 25 gp. A small iron box beneath the altar contains 47 gp and a scroll of *protection from evil and good*.

## 4. Cult Treasure Chamber

```boxed
The ceiling of this chamber is 8' above the floor. Three large chests rest upon a raised platform of stone and packed earth in the center of the room. The air here smells of dust and old coin. As you enter, you notice that the shadows in the corners seem to lean inward, watching.
```

The chests are locked (DC 13 Dexterity check to pick) but are not trapped. The platform is solid and hollow beneath, containing nothing of interest.

- **First chest:** 1,207 gp
- **Second chest:** 456 gp and 45 gp worth of various coins
- **Third chest:** 87 ornamental stones (moonstone, malachite, and obsidian) worth an average of 10 gp apiece, and six semi-precious stones (onyx, zircon, and jasper) worth 25 gp apiece

**Special:** Any character who takes more than their share of the treasure (more than an equal split) must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or be affected by a *dream vision*—they see a hooded figure in a volcanic landscape, speaking words they cannot understand. They are distracted for 1d4 hours, with disadvantage on Perception checks until they take a long rest.

## 5. Cave-in

```boxed
The west wall of the tunnel has collapsed here, burying the supporting timbers under tons of ash and mud. The rubble has created a small cavern, and the floor is covered in a layer of gray ash at least 3' deep. Something seems to be moving beneath the surface.
```

Movement through this chamber is at half speed for all creatures. The ash is unstable—if any creature enters the room without moving carefully (at half speed or less), they trigger a **small collapse**. All creatures in the room must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 bludgeoning damage from falling debris.

Buried under the ash is the corpse of a previous adventurer. Searching the ash reveals a tarnished silver ring (worth 15 gp), a broken shortsword, and a journal written in a language none can read (actually Daedric, detailing the location of a hidden cache elsewhere in the dungeon—the DM may place an appropriate reward).

## 6. Burial Pool

The door, swollen from moisture, requires a DC 12 Strength check to open.

```boxed
Beyond the door is a chamber almost entirely filled with a murky pool of dark water. The smell of ash and something fouler hangs heavy in the air. A thick, slime-covered column rises from the center of the pool to support the sagging timbers of the ceiling. Across the chamber, a small, muddy shelf extends into the pool.

The water is still. Too still. You realize it's not water at all—it's ash, mixed with something dark and thick.
```

Hiding in the water near the center of the pool are two **ash ghouls**. They will attack anyone stepping through the door, bursting from the ash-mud with terrifying speed.

```statblock
**Ash Ghoul (2):** AC 15; HP 30 (4d10+8); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d6+3) slashing; DC 13 Con save or be poisoned for 1 minute (repeat save at end of each turn); immune to poison and disease
```

The pool is 3' deep throughout, with an additional 1' of ash on the bottom. Wading through it is at half speed. The ash-water has a powerful odor—any creature spending more than 1 round in it must make a DC 12 Constitution save or become nauseated (disadvantage on attack rolls) for 1 minute.

**Treasure:** There is no treasure here, but a DC 15 Perception check reveals a humanoid skeleton at the bottom of the pool clutching a small iron key (fits the chest in room 18).

**Secret Door:** The secret door is actually a plug of ash and dirt that fills a 3' diameter hole. It may be detected with a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check (elves have advantage). If noticed, the "door" may be removed in two turns of diligent digging, revealing a narrow tunnel that leads to room 8.

## 7. Home of the Corprus Stalker

In the center of the muddy section of this corridor, a **corprus stalker** lies partially buried, awaiting the unwary foot.

```statblock
**Corprus Stalker:** AC 14; HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d8+2) piercing; DC 12 Con save or contract a wasting disease (effects at DM's discretion, possibly permanent ability score reduction until cured)
```

If the party is prodding the mud before them, the chance for surprise is normal. If the characters merely walk through the mud, they are surprised (the stalker has advantage on its first attack). Movement through the mud is at half normal speed.

The door just beyond the pool is false—it is painted to look like a door but leads nowhere.

## 8. Dreamer Cultists' Rooms

These five rooms shelter the cultists who have been assigned to serve as guards and attendants for the lair. Four were already encountered at the entrance, and the other twelve will be here. Loud noises in this area will bring all of them running in 1-4 rounds. These rooms are lit by eerie green torches that seem to burn without smoke.

### 8a

The door is unlocked.

```boxed
In this room, four cultists sit listlessly on wooden bunks, their eyes closed. Their lips move silently, as if in prayer—or conversation with someone who is not present. A spear rests beside each of them.
```

Four **dreamer cultists** (CR 1/4) are in a meditative trance. They will attack strangers on sight, but suffer disadvantage on their first round of combat while they shake off the trance. As with all cultists here, they have no personal treasure.

### 8b

This door is locked (DC 12 Dexterity check to pick). This empty room belongs to the four cultists on guard duty. Four bunks and a small table with a dream journal are the only items in here. The journal contains cryptic ramblings about "the Sleeper's will" and "the coming of the Sixth House."

### 8c

This door is locked (DC 12 Dexterity check to pick).

```boxed
The room contains four cultists, asleep. Their breathing is shallow and irregular, and their eyes twitch beneath closed lids. They are dreaming—but their dreams are not their own.
```

Four **dreamer cultists** (CR 1/4) are asleep. Their weapons are nearby, but they will not stop to put on their armor in an emergency. If they are attacked without being alerted, they have the Incapacitated condition for the first round of combat and are surprised.

### 8d

This room is the same as 8c, including four sleeping cultists.

### 8e

```boxed
This room has a long table with benches on either side. Dirty platters and stained mugs are strewn across the tabletop. At the west end of the room is a small oven, vented through an 8" diameter hole in the ceiling. Several closed wooden boxes contain more platters, pans, mugs, bottles of cheap sujamma, and dried mushrooms.
```

There is nothing of interest or value here.

## 9. Storage Room

The door to this room is locked (DC 14 Dexterity check to pick). Beyond, crates, barrels, cases, and racks contain most of the lair's supplies. Ten spears, four daggers, and a shortsword are protected from the dampness by a rack on the wall. The crates contain dried saltrice, kresh fiber, ash yams, mushrooms, oil, and salt. Boxes of large spikes, several hammers, a pile of stout timbers, and four shovels are the building materials present. Several dozen torches are stored in a barrel.

A DC 15 Investigation check reveals a hidden compartment in one of the crates containing a **potion of healing** and 25 gp.

## 10. Empty Pool

```boxed
This shallow pool of clear, still water was revealed when a section of the tunnel wall collapsed. Hundreds of pale, eyeless fish dart through the water—they seem to have no natural predators here. The water is cold and strangely pure compared to the ash-choked air.
```

There is nothing of interest here, but the water is safe to drink. A DC 12 Perception check reveals a faint magical aura—the pool is a hidden shrine to Almalexia, placed here long ago by priests who did not know what this place would become. A character who drinks the water and prays to Almalexia receives the benefits of a *bless* spell for 1 hour.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 11. Wine Storage

This room is stoutly locked (DC 15 Dexterity check to pick). When the door is tried, a little dirt falls from the ceiling. If the door is forced (DC 17 Strength check), a large section of dirt will collapse onto any characters within 10' of the door, dealing 1d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 13 Dexterity save for half).

The room contains 15 casks and kegs, raised off the dirt floor on wooden shelves. Most contain cheap sujamma and beer, but four small casks are private stock for the Ascended Sleeper and their higher minions. Two contain a strong, dark ale; the others contain **Velunan Fireamber** and **Orlane Special** wines (reflavored as high-quality Chardenfell brews). The casks weigh 250 gp each and are worth 100 gp (ale) and 200 gp (wine).

## 12a.—12b. Empty Rooms

These chambers have unlocked but stuck doors (DC 10 Strength check to open). Each has a pile of ash and mud in a far corner from a slowly eroding wall. They contain nothing of value.

## 13. Entry Hall

```boxed
This lofty room has a ceiling 15' above the floor, supported by six stout columns of unadorned wood. A pool of ash and mud in the center of the floor reduces movement. Strange symbols are carved into the columns—they seem to writhe in the corner of your eye, but hold still when you look at them directly.
```

The pool of ash in the center will reduce movement by half but is otherwise unremarkable. The symbols on the columns are Daedric—a successful DC 15 Arcana check reveals them to be praises to Dagoth Ur, the Sleeping God.

## 14. Lieutenants' Quarters

Herein dwell the cult's higher-ranking human followers: two assassins, a warrior, and a priest. These rooms are the most comfortable in the dungeon, and three of the four residents will be in them. (The fourth, a priest, is tending to the altar on the second level.)

The outer door is heavily barred (DC 17 Strength check to force). If it is smashed in on the first attempt, the assassins will be in room 14a and the warrior will be in room 14c. If the first attempt to break the door is unsuccessful, they will have assumed defensive positions.

**Cult Lieutenants:**

```statblock
**Vedran Sarano (Warrior):** AC 16 (scale mail); HP 30 (4d10+8); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d8+3); S 16, D 14, C 15, I 10, W 11, Ch 8; has *tough* feat
```

```statblock
**Kyran Ethas (Assassin):** AC 15 (studded leather); HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d6+3); S 13, D 17, C 13, I 12, W 10, Ch 6; *sneak attack* (2d6)
```

```statblock
**Morag Aran (Assassin):** AC 14 (studded leather); HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +5 for (1d6+3) or crossbow (1d8+3); S 13, D 16, C 13, I 12, W 10, Ch 6; *sneak attack* (2d6)
```

### 14a

```boxed
This room contains four soft chairs and several small tables. A fire burns in a large fireplace, casting strange shadows. A keg of sujamma (about 1/4 full) sits in the corner, and two glasses are on one of the tables. The walls are covered in sketches of distorted faces and inhuman figures.
```

### 14b

Kyran and Morag share this room. It contains two beds and mattresses, two chairs, and six wall pegs. Two woolen cloaks hang here, but the room is otherwise empty. A successful DC 12 Investigation check reveals a hidden pouch under one of the mattresses containing 45 gp.

### 14c

This is Vedran's room, containing a bed, chair, and several pegs on the wall. A woolen cloak hangs from one of these, and a thick quilt covers the bed. A **potion of healing** is hidden under the quilt.

### 14d

This door is locked (DC 15 Dexterity check to pick) and leads to the priest's room.

```boxed
A soft bed, desk, chair, and wooden chest are the only objects in the room. A small obsidian image of an Ascended Sleeper sits on the desk.
```

The chest is locked (DC 14 Dexterity check) and protected by a *glyph of warding* that will paralyze a creature opening it for 1d4 hours (DC 13 Constitution save to resist). The code word to open it safely is "Sleep." Within the chest is a dark clerical robe, a mace, and a scroll with two *cure wounds* spells.

## 15. Ash Pool

```boxed
This large room has no doors; the corridors simply end at the edge of a vast pool of dark, ash-choked water. While very shallow around its perimeter, the pool reaches a depth of 4' in the center. The muck on the bottom reduces movement to half speed. A stagnant, musty smell is strong here. The water is dark and still, and you can see shapes moving beneath the surface.
```

The residents of the chamber are resting on a mud shelf at the northeast end of the room. Any ripple in the water will cause them to enter the pool and swim toward the source of the movement.

```statblock
**Dreugh (3):** AC 14 (natural); HP 19 (3d10+3); Atk 2x at +4 for (1d6+2) bludgeoning; can grapple (escape DC 14)
```

*The dreugh are not loyal to the cult, but they have been here so long they have grown accustomed to the cultists feeding them. They will attack any creature that disturbs them.*

They have no treasure, but a DC 16 Investigation check of the murky water reveals the skeleton of a previous victim wearing a **ring of swimming** (advantage on Athletics checks related to swimming).

### 15a. Hidden Boat

This small alcove hides a small boat the cult members use to cross the ash pool. The boat holds up to 10 characters, and the dreugh will not attack it unless disturbed.

## 16. Arched Chamber

The door to this room is unlocked and has been scorched by the cult members with a large black symbol of the Sixth House. Within, four thick columns support the beams of an arched chamber ceiling, 25' high. A 20' wide, 20' high corridor leads from the southern side of the room.

As soon as the **ascended dreamer** in room 17 senses creatures enter, it will begin its psychic assault. All characters must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw. Those who fail are affected as if by a *confusion* spell for 1 minute (repeat save at the end of each of their turns). Those who succeed are unaffected but feel a cold presence in their minds.

The ascended dreamer will then drift down the passage, its form flickering between solidity and something less substantial. It will attack the party directly, focusing on the character who appears to be the leader.

```statblock
**Ascended Dreamer:** AC 16; HP 45 (6d8+18); Atk 2x at +6 for (1d6+3) psychic; DC 14 Wis save or be frightened for 1 round; resistant to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-magical weapons
```

If the dreamer is reduced to half health, it will retreat back to its den (room 17) to recover. It will not pursue beyond the chamber.

## 17. Den of the Ascended Dreamer

The southern chamber can barely be seen from the north end of the corridor. A character could reach it by moving along the walls with a DC 14 Acrobatics or Athletics check. One of the 20' timbers from room 9 could be brought and used as a bridge, but would require a combined Strength score of 40 to lift and carry through the dungeon.

```boxed
This chamber is much like chamber 16, except that the four columns have been carved with disturbing murals of sleeping figures. The ascended dreamer's lair is here—the walls are lined with the belongings of those it has killed. Jewelry, weapons, and personal effects hang from hooks on the walls.
```

**Treasure:** The dreamer has collected:
- Jewelry worth 250 gp, 100 gp (x2), 90 gp, 75 gp, and 50 gp (x4)
- A ruby pendant worth 800 gp
- A **ring of feather falling**
- A **wand of magic missiles** (7 charges)

A hole in the ceiling, unreachable by climbing, leads to an exit through a hummock in the marsh above.

## 18. Lair of the Corprus Stalker

The watery corridor leading to this muddy den may be waded through, as it is only 2' deep. Because of the muck on the bottom, movement is at half speed.

If the corprus stalker has not been encountered as a wandering monster, it will be found in its lair. At the sound of a group approaching, it will enter the corridor and attack.

```statblock
**Corprus Stalker:** AC 14; HP 22 (4d8+4); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d8+2) piercing; DC 12 Con save or contract a wasting disease
```

```boxed
This is the lair of the corprus stalker. Here, torn leather pouches, rusty buckles, and a tattered tunic lie scattered on the ground. The walls are smeared with dark, crusty stains.
```

Buried in the mud of the chamber are 300 gp and 80 sp. A character who digs through the mud will find 10% of these each turn spent searching.

## 19. Corridor

This watery hall is similar in depth and effect on movement to the route to the corprus stalker's lair. It leads to a dead end.

## 20. Ash Slave Guardroom

This network of chambers gives access to the lower level of the dungeon, and as such a permanent guard of ash slaves has been assigned here. Each door will be barred on the side opposite the adventurers' approach. They are difficult to force open (DC 15 Strength check).

### 20a

```boxed
The outer guardroom has two ash slaves within, each carrying a stone club. Three wooden benches are the only items of furniture in the room.
```

```statblock
**Ash Slave (2):** AC 13; HP 13 (3d8); Atk 1x at +3 for (1d6+1) bludgeoning; immune to poison and disease
```

The ash slaves will become alert as soon as the door is hit, and one will bang on the door leading to 20b and 20c to alert the other four ash slaves. The latter will arrive in two rounds, and if the battle is still in progress, they will unbar the door and join the melee.

If the first two guards are killed, or if the four hear no sound beyond the door, they will leave the bar across it and wait for the party to come through. The four late arrivals also have 13 HP apiece.

### 20b

This is simply a large, empty room.

### 20c

```boxed
This is where the guards "rest"—they do not sleep in any meaningful sense. Many bones are scattered around the floor, and there is a low table in the center of the room.
```

Each ash slave has a small leather pouch at its waist with a few coins inside. The total carried by all six is 16 gp, 20 ep, 57 sp, and 18 cp.

## 21. Ash Cavern

```boxed
Two large columns support the roof in this large room, and the floor is coated with ash. A strange, rhythmic pulsing seems to emanate from the walls themselves.
```

The ash slows movement by half. The stepping stones allow the party to cross the room at normal speed in single file. There is nothing else of interest here, but a DC 14 Perception check reveals faint voices whispering from the walls—they speak of "the coming dream" and "the Sleeper's awakening."

## 22. Dreamer's Ambush

```boxed
This appears to be nothing more than an empty, ash-filled room. The adventurer who steps in will quickly learn otherwise.
```

**Two ascended dreamers** (CR 3) hide in the shadows of this chamber. They will attack the first character to enter, attempting to isolate and overwhelm them.

```statblock
**Ascended Dreamer (2):** AC 16; HP 45 (6d8+18); Atk 2x at +6 for (1d6+3) psychic; DC 14 Wis save or be frightened for 1 round
```

The dreamers will surprise the party on a roll of 1-4 on a d6. If the party enters with a source of light, the dreamers are visible and no surprise occurs.

**Treasure:** The dreamers have collected the belongings of their victims. A small pile in the corner contains 35 gp, 120 sp, and a **scroll of sleep**.

## 23. Second Treasure Room

```boxed
From the entrance, this large room appears to be filled with a pool of murky brown water. Actually, hidden around a bend in the wall is a shelf of dry earth and the cult's second treasure stash.
```

A secret door, made of wood but plastered with ash to match the wall, can be opened by prying at the edge with a sharp object (DC 12 Strength check). Within, a small boat may be found that can carry two man-sized characters, or one character and an equivalent amount of treasure (about 2500 gp weight).

On the hidden shelf is a raised wooden platform with two medium-sized chests. The chests are locked (DC 14 Dexterity check). One contains a poison needle trap in the latch that will be released by any unsuccessful attempt to pick the lock unless it is successfully removed first (DC 13 Dexterity check with thieves' tools). The poison needle deals 2d6 poison damage (DC 14 Constitution save for half).

- **Trapped chest:** 300 pp, 600 gp, and 1,000 sp
- **Other chest:** 600 ep, a 1,000 gp jeweled bracelet, and 20 small gems worth 25 gp apiece

## 24. Chamber of the Ash Ghost

The door to this room is swollen with moisture (DC 12 Strength check to open).

```boxed
There is a thick column in the center of the room, and the smell of death permeates the air. Behind the pillar, a spectral figure hovers—it resembles a Dark Elf, but its features are blurred and distorted.
```

This is an **ash ghost** (treat as a [specter](https://slyflourish.com/5e_artisanal_database/monsters/html/5.2_srd_(2025_mm)/specter_mm_2024.html) with the following adjustments: it is vulnerable to fire damage, and its *life drain* deals necrotic damage and reduces maximum HP by 1d4).

```statblock
**Ash Ghost:** AC 12; HP 22 (5d8); Atk 1x at +4 for (1d6+2) necrotic; target's max HP is reduced by the same amount until a long rest; resistant to non-magical weapons; immune to charm, exhaustion, paralyzed, poison, unconscious
```

The ghost will step into view as the characters enter the room. It will advance silently, its form flickering with every step. When it is hit by a fire-based attack, it will recoil and suffer disadvantage on its next attack.

**Treasure:** There is no treasure in this room. The door to the north is locked (DC 14 Dexterity check to pick) and must be opened to proceed.

## 25. Cells of the Doomed

The door leading to the five cells is locked (DC 14 Dexterity check to pick). A party entering here will find three cells unoccupied. The other two contain prisoners who have resisted the dreamer's influence—they have been kept here, awaiting whatever fate the Sixth House has in store for them.

- **Cell 1:** The son and daughter of Seyda Neen's carpenter—**Galmus** and **Sedyni Daras** (if not already rescued from the temple).
- **Cell 2:** A prosperous merchant from Balmora, taken with his ill-fated caravan in the Bitter Coast, and a poor farmer from one of the farms east of Seyda Neen who saw his family charmed into a life devoted to the Sixth House.

The prisoners will be extremely grateful to be rescued. They will tell all they know of the Ascended Sleeper's chamber (room 37). They will not fight, however, and none of them has seen the secret passage between rooms 27 and 37.

## 26. Dreamer's Workshop

```boxed
This room is bare except for five stone tables about 3' off the floor. Strange tools lie on the tables—knives, chisels, and other implements you cannot identify. The air is cold, and you feel a deep sense of unease.
```

This is where the cult performs rituals to create ash slaves from the bodies of those who have resisted the dreams. There are neither corpses nor ash slaves in the room now.

## 27. Altar of the Sleeper

The door to this room is unlocked. **Ulath** the priest is aware of the party's approach and is prepared.

```boxed
This is a long room whose walls are covered in wooden panels carved with disturbing symbols. At the far end is a raised dais with an altar supporting a statue of the Ascended Sleeper—a featureless humanoid figure with a hidden face. Next to the altar stands a Dark Elf in stained robes, smiling. A shadowy form stirs behind the statue.
```

This is where **Ulath** (CR 3 [priest](https://slyflourish.com/5e_artisanal_database/monsters/html/5.2_srd_(2025_mm)/priest_mm_2024.html)) pays homage to the image of the Ascended Sleeper. He wears chain mail and carries a shield and a ceremonial dagger. He has a ring of keys on his belt containing the keys for all doors in rooms 24 and 25, as well as the chests in rooms 4 and 23.

Ulath's spells:
- Cantrips: *guidance*, *sacred flame*, *thaumaturgy*
- 1st level (4 slots): *bane*, *command*, *cure wounds*, *inflict wounds*
- 2nd level (3 slots): *hold person*, *silence*, *spiritual weapon*
- 3rd level (2 slots): *animate dead*, *bestow curse*

After the party enters the room, Ulath's sinister assistant will reveal itself—an **ash ghoul**.

```statblock
**Ash Ghoul:** AC 15; HP 30 (4d10+8); Atk 2x at +5 for (1d6+3) slashing; DC 13 Con save or be poisoned for 1 minute
```

Ulath will allow the ash ghoul to attack first while he casts *bane* on the party, followed by *hold person* on the party's caster.

## 30. Ash Trap

```boxed
This dank and dingy chamber contains a small chest upon a raised wooden platform.
```

The chest is bolted firmly to the platform and may not be moved. It appears to be locked, but any attempt to pick the lock will cause it to open. A successful DC 14 Investigation check by a character with thieves' tools, or a DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check, will reveal the trap.

When the chest, which is empty except for the trap mechanism, is opened, all the ceiling supports in the room will be released and a mass of ash and mud will fall into the chamber from above. Characters in the room must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be struck by falling debris for 1d6 bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. If they fail by 5 or more, they are pinned under a timber and will suffocate unless rescued.

Characters that are not trapped under the timbers can escape the room immediately. A character can attempt to rescue a pinned comrade by making a DC 15 Strength check. Each rescue attempt takes one round. For each round after the first two that a character remains in the room, their chance to escape decreases by 10%.

## 37. Throne Room of the Ascended Sleeper

```boxed
This huge cavern is lit by a ghostly green glow emanating from the eight columns supporting the vaulted ceiling. A large flatbottomed boat sits at the shore of a pool of dark water before you. The pool seems to occupy most of the cavern, and at the far end, on a raised dais, something waits. It is tall, humanoid, and utterly wrong. Its face is a smooth expanse of flesh with no features—and yet you feel it watching you.
```

The boat can carry ten persons and has a long pole inside which can be used to propel it at a speed of 20 feet per round.

**The Ascended Sleeper** awaits the party in its alcove. This being is a nightmare given form—a tall, eerily humanoid creature with a featureless face and an aura of pure wrongness. It is served by the dreams of those who slumber in its presence.

```statblock
**Ascended Sleeper (Boss):** AC 17; HP 85 (10d10+30); Atk 3x at +7 for (1d8+4) psychic; 
**Special Abilities:**
- **Nightmare Gaze:** At the start of each of the party's turns, one creature of the Sleeper's choice within 30 feet must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or be frightened until the start of their next turn.
- **Dream Eater (Recharge 5-6):** The Sleeper targets one creature within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 15 Charisma save or take 4d8 psychic damage and be incapacitated for 1 round.
- **Incorporeal Movement:** The Sleeper can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, but takes 1d10 force damage if it ends its turn inside a solid object.
- **Resistant:** Resistant to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-magical weapons.
- **Damage Immunities:** Psychic, poison; immune to charm and frightened conditions.
```

**Strategy:** The Sleeper will fight intelligently, using its incorporeal movement to avoid melee attacks while focusing its psychic damage on the party's casters and support characters.

When the Sleeper is reduced to 25 HP or less, it will begin to phase in and out of reality. On each of its turns, it may attempt to escape by phasing through the walls to the secret passage behind its dais. If it escapes, it will haunt the party at the worst possible moments—treat this as a continuing threat that can be resolved by the party finding and destroying the Sleeper's phylactery-like "dream heart" hidden in a secret compartment in the lair.

**Treasure:** The Ascended Sleeper's collected treasures are piled at the base of its dais:

- 1,100 pp
- 8,900 gp
- 13,020 sp
- A jeweled bracelet worth 400 gp
- A ruby ring worth 800 gp
- A **bag of holding** (empty, 500 lb capacity)
- A pair of **elven boots**
- A **horn of bubbles**
- A **ring of protection +1**
- A **ring of free action**
- Six potions: *gaseous form*, *invisibility*, *clairvoyance*, *philter of love*, and two *healing*

Two ivory scroll tubes are also buried in the treasure pile. Each is sealed and elaborately carved. By making a DC 15 Dexterity check with thieves' tools, a character may open them. If the tubes are broken, the scrolls will explode, dealing 1d8 force damage to anyone within 10' (DC 13 Dex save for half). Intact, each scroll tube is worth 400 gp.

- **Scroll 1 (wizard):** *slow*, *stinking cloud*, *detect magic* (caster level 7)
- **Scroll 2 (cleric):** *heal*, *cure disease*, *dispel magic* (caster level 11)

### Aftermath

If the Ascended Sleeper is destroyed, all creatures who have been charmed or corrupted by its influence will be freed from the enchantment. They will have vague, dreamlike memories of cult activities, but will not remember the experience as being real. This includes all humans, Dark Elves, and other humanoids, no matter where they are. The ash slaves and corprus stalkers, however, were created not charmed, and they will remain hostile.

The destruction of the Sleeper sends a tremor through the dungeon. The walls begin to crumble. The party must make their escape—they have 10 minutes (60 rounds) to reach the surface before the entire lair collapses. The route is now clear of monsters, but the collapsing ceilings present hazards: the party must make DC 10 Dexterity checks every 2 rounds to avoid falling debris (1d4 bludgeoning damage on a failure, save for half).
