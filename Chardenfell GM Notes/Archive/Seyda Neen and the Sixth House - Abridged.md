# Seyda Neen and the Sixth House

## Beginning the Adventure

The journey to Seyda Neen is swift and uneventful. The DM may wish to require a traveling order to get players used to being on guard.

```boxed
You finally exit the dreaded cave of that soul sucking gem. It exits in what you immediately recognize as the Bitter Coast region of Chardenfell. On the horizon to the south west, you see the peaks of the Ascadian Isles, and just behind you, the cave mouth is coming just out of major foyada, the remnants of the ancient lava rivers that once dominanted ancient Chardenfell. 

The bitter coast is hard to navigate. It is mostly wetland, and in addition to the many willow and oak trees that create a thick and low canopy, giant mushrooms called parasols rise up to impressive heights, often towering above the tree line and casting great shadows. After a short period of gathering yourselves and getting your bearings straight, you are graced with incredible luck and spot a silt strider path.
```

```boxed
Being more or less entirely lost, this is by far your best chance at finding civilization from the wilderness. You follow the path of the distinctive circular footmarks of the great beast, hulking animals with 12 long legs and a chitinous shell at the top where its riders sit. They are the primary means of overland transport throughout Chardenfell and critical to trade routes throughout much of the heartland where the ashlands and foyadas would otherwise prove impassable.
```

```boxed
After a few hours you spot civilization! It is a much needed stopping point as you grow weary, hungry, and thirsty. It is a simple village on the horizon. You come across a signpost that says "Seyda Neen" in Dark Elvish. The is modestly sized, you'd estimate maybe holding only a few hundred residents. It has a very simple silt strider port on the north end , and generally seems to be set in a very idyllic and fertile plain of the Bitter Coast, and you can see many clearly worked fields of kresh fiber and ash yams, two crops very common in Chardenfell.
```

```boxed
Approaching the village, the first building you come across seems to be a simple farmhouse with a barn attached. A young dark elf woman is grinding herbs, flowers, and mushrooms with a mortar and pestle, but she sees your group and runs into the house at your approach. Shadowy forms are visible behind closed curtains.
```

The adventure begins as characters enter along the west edge of the village map.

Most buildings are unmapped. If entered, describe a typical one-story interior with front and back doors. Many have porches.
