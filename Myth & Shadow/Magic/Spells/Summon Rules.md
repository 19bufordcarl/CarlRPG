# Summon Rules

Some [Spells](../Spells.md) allow you to summon creatures to your aid. Typically, this involves summoning an [Extraplanar](../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Creature%20Types/Extraplanar.md) entity bound to your service.

The following rules apply to summons unless otherwise stated.

## One Bound Soul

A PC can only have one soul bound to them at a time.

## Unwilling Servant

If you lose [Concentration](../../Concentration.md), the summoned creature remains on this plane, but becomes hostile towards its mind controller — you. It will, accordingly, also be hostile to your allies.

When you lose [Concentration](../../Concentration.md), you can use a [Reaction](../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Reaction.md) to make a [Spellcasting Check](../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the creature's [Magicka](../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [Spell Resist DC](Spell%20Resist%20DC.md) to banish the creature to its plane of origin.
