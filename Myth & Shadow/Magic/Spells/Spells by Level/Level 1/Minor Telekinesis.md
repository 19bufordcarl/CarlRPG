# Minor Telekinesis

| Level    | [1st Level Spell](1st%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled                                                       |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You gain the ability to move small things from a distance with your mind, as if you had an invisible, spectral, floating hand.

When you cast the spell, you can manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial.

As a Magic [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) on your later turns, you can exert any of the telekinetic effects again.

You can't use this telekinesis to [Attack](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Attack.md), activate magic items, or carry more than 10 pounds.

If you fall [Unconscious](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Unconscious.md), this spell ends.

---
*Upcast: Increase the range by 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) per spell level.*
