# Chromatic Orb

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](../Level%202/2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | None                                                                  |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You hurl an orb of energy at a target you can see within range and choose [Cold](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Cold.md), [Fire](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Fire.md), [Lightning](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Lightning.md), [Necrotic](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Necrotic.md), or [Radiant](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Radiant.md). You must make this choice when you [Learn](../../../Spellcasting/Spell%20Learning/Known%20Spells.md#Spell%20Learning) this spell and it cannot be changed unless you later learn a different version of this spell.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Stamina](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Stamina.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, the target takes 3d8 [Tier 2](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%202.md) damage of the chosen type.

If you roll the same number on two or more of the d8s, the orb leaps to a different target of your choice within 30 feet of the target. Make an attack roll against the new target, and make a new damage roll. The orb only leaps once, unless [Upcast](../../Spell%20Attributes.md#Upcast). A creature can be targeted only once by each casting of this spell.

---
*Upcast: Deal an additional 1d8 damage and increase the max number of leaps by 1 per spell level.*
