# Mass Healing

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You reach out with restoring magic. Choose up to 6 creatures you can see within range.

Each affected creature regains 2d12 + [Magicka](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [HP](../../../../Player%20Characters/Point%20Pools/Health%20Points.md) and ceases to be [Wounded](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Wounded.md).

---
*Upcast: Heal 1d12 additional HP per spell level.*
