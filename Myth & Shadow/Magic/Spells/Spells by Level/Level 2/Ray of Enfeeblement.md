# Ray of Enfeeblement

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

A beam of enervating energy shoots from you toward a creature within range.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Health](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Health.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, the target takes 1d12 [Tier 3](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%203.md) [Necrotic](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Necrotic.md) damage and becomes [Wounded](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Wounded.md) for the duration.

---
*Upcast: Deal an additional 1d12 damage per spell level.*
