# Summon Minor Extraplanar

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | Self                                                                  |

You summon a minor [Extraplanar](../../../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Creature%20Types/Extraplanar.md) from one of the outer realms to your aid for the duration. See [Summon Rules](../../Summon%20Rules.md).

Choose [Cold](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Cold.md), [Fire](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Fire.md), [Lightning](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Lightning.md), [Necrotic](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Necrotic.md), or [Radiant](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Radiant.md). You must make this choice when you [Learn](../../../Spellcasting/Spell%20Learning/Known%20Spells.md#Spell%20Learning) this spell and it cannot be changed unless you later learn a different version of this spell. Alternatively, you can always choose [Blunt](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Blunt.md) or [Edged](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Edged.md) damage.

Until the spell ends, the minor extraplanar aids you in combat. It acts on its own [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) in [Initiative](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Initiative.md) and gets its own [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md), [Reaction](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Reaction.md), and [Movement](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Movement.md). You, the player, control it. It can only make a [Melee Attack](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Melee%20Attack.md) that deals [Tier 2](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%202.md) damage of the chosen type, and cannot [Power Attack](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Power%20Attack.md).
