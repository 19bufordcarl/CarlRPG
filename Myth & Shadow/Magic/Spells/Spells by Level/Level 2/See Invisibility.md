# See Invisibility

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled                                                       |
| Range    | Self                                                                  |

You gain magical sight letting you see creatures and objects that are [Invisible](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Invisible.md) as if they were visible. These creatures and objects appear ghostly.

The spell ends if you fall [Unconscious](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Unconscious.md) or cast this spell again.

---
*Upcast: None.*
