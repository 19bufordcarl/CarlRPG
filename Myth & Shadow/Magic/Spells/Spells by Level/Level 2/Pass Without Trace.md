# Pass Without Trace

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | Self                                                                  |

You radiate a concealing aura covering the [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) you are in for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to any [Stamina](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Stamina.md) ([Finesse](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Secondary%20Skills/Finesse.md)) [Check](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Check.md) related to stealth and you leave no tracks.

---
*Upcast: Increase the stealth bonus by 1 per spell level.*
