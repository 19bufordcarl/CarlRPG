# Summon Steed

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | Self                                                                  |

You summon a minor [Extraplanar](../../../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Creature%20Types/Extraplanar.md) from one of the outer realms that takes the form of a steed for the duration. See [Summon Rules](../../Summon%20Rules.md).

The steed resembles a Large, rideable animal of your choice, such as a horse, a camel, a dire wolf, or an elk. You must make this choice when you [Learn](../../../Spellcasting/Spell%20Learning/Known%20Spells.md#Spell%20Learning) this spell and it cannot be changed unless you later learn a different version of this spell.

**Combat:** The steed is an ally to you and your allies. In [Combat](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Combat.md) you can ride it like a [War Horse](../../../../Items%20and%20Gear/Gear/Trained%20Animals/Horse,%20War.md).
