# Lesser Restoration

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | None                                                                  |
| Range    | Touch                                                                 |

You touch a creature and end one of these [Conditions](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/{Conditions}.md) on it:

- [Blinded](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Blinded.md), [Deafened](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Deafened.md), [Paralyzed](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Paralyzed.md), or [Poisoned](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Poisoned.md).

---
*Upcast: Target up to 1 additional creature or cure 1 additional condition per spell level.*
