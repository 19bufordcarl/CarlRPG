# Warding Bond

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled                                                       |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

In order to cast this spell, you and exactly one other target must be wearing two silvered or nicer rings worth at least 500 [Coins](../../../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Economy/Coins.md) each that you have ritually bound together.

Until the spell ends, the other ring bearer gains a +1 bonus to their [Armor Class](../../../../Player%20Characters/Derived%20Statistics/Armor%20Class.md), [Checks](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Check.md), and becomes [Resistant](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Resistant.md) to all [Damage Tiers](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/{Damage%20Tiers}.md) and [Types](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/{Damage%20Types}.md). However, each time the other ring bearer takes damage, you take the same amount of damage.

The spell ends if you drop to 0 [HP](../../../../Player%20Characters/Point%20Pools/Health%20Points.md) or if you and the target become separated and become out of range of one another. It also ends if the spell is cast again on either of the connected creatures.

---
*Upcast: Increase the range by 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) per spell level.*
