# Magic Mouth

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled                                                       |
| Range    | Touch                                                                 |

You implant a message within an object you touch—a message that is uttered when a trigger condition is met. Choose an object that you can see and that isn't being worn or carried by another creature. Then speak the message, which must be 25 words or fewer, it is delivered in an amount of time you took to cast the spell, and you can cast it slower than 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) if desired. Finally, determine the circumstance that will trigger the spell to deliver your message.

When that trigger occurs, a magical mouth appears on the object and recites the message in your voice and at the same volume you spoke. If the object you chose has a mouth or something that looks like a mouth (for example, the mouth of a statue), the magical mouth appears there, so the words appear to come from the object's mouth. When you cast this spell, you can have the spell end after it delivers its message, or it can remain and repeat its message whenever the trigger occurs.

The trigger can be as general or as detailed as you like, though it must be based on visual or audible conditions that occur in the same [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) as the object. For example, you could instruct the mouth to speak when any creature moves into the [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) or when a silver bell rings in the [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md).

---
*Upcast: Target up to 1 additional object per spell level.*
