# Hold Person

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You magically restrict a [Humanoid](../../../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Creature%20Types/Humanoid.md) you can see within range for the duration.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Magicka](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, the target [Paralyzed](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Paralyzed.md). You must repeat this [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) at the start of each subsequent [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) if you wish to keep the spell going. On a failure, the spell ends.

---
*Upcast: Target 1 additional creature per spell level.*
