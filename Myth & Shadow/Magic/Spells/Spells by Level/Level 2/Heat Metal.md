# Heat Metal

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

Choose a manufactured metal object, like a metal [Weapon](../../../../Items%20and%20Gear/Weapons/Weapons.md) or a suit of metal [Armor](../../../../Items%20and%20Gear/Armor/Armor.md), that you can see within range. You cause the object to glow red-hot. Any creature in physical contact with the object takes 3d8 [Tier 1](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%201.md) [Fire](../../Spell%20Domains/Fire.md) damage when you cast the spell.

As a [Reaction](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Reaction.md) starting on your next [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md), you deal this damage again if the object is within range.

---
*Upcast: Deal an additional 1d8 damage per spell level.*
