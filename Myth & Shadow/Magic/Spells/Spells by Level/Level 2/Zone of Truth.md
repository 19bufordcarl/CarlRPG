# Zone of Truth

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You compel one creature you can see within range to tell the truth for the duration.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Magicka](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, the creature can't speak a deliberate lie while compelled in this way.

An affected creature is aware of the spell and can avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive yet must be truthful.

---
*Upcast: Target 1 additional creature per spell level.*
