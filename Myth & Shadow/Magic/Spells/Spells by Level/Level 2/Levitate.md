# Levitate

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | Touch                                                                 |

A target you touch becomes weightless for the duration. If the target is unwilling creature, or any object, you must make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Health](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Health.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). If the target is an object, this [DC](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/DC.md) is proportional to the object's weight and other relevant characteristics as determined by the GM.

You can control the vertical height of affected targets as a [Free Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md#Free%20Action) once on your [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) mentally. If you do so, you can move the target up to 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) up or down.

Affected targets can push off of other objects to move themselves in other directions.

---
*Upcast: Target up to 1 additional creature or object per spell level.*
