# Gust of Wind

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You create a strong wind blowing in any direction affecting any number of creatures in a given [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) that you can see within range. You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against each target's [Stamina](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Stamina.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, they are pushed out of this [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) into the next one if possible, or to the very edge of the [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) if not.

The [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) affected by the gusting wind requires [Difficult Movement](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Movement.md#Difficult%20Movement).

The gust disperses gas or vapor, and it extinguishes candles and similar unprotected flames in the area. It causes protected flames, such as those of lanterns, to dance wildly and has a 50 percent chance to extinguish them.

As a [Reaction](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Reaction.md) starting on your next [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md), you can change the direction of the wind gusts.

---
*Upcast: Increase the casting range by 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) per spell level.*
