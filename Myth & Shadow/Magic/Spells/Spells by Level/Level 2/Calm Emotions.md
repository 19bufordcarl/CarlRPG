# Calm Emotions

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

Choose up to 3 [Humanoids](../../../../Resources%20for%20GMs/Creature%20Types/Humanoid.md) you can see within range. If a target is unwilling, you must make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against the target's [Magicka](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success the target is affected. Choose one of the following:

- **Immunity:** The affected creatures are [Immune](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Immune.md) to any magical or supernatural effects that would alter their emotions, feelings, and general mental state against their will. If they are already under such an effect, the effect is suppressed until the spell ends.
- **Indifference:** The affected creatures become indifferent about creatures of your choice that they are hostile toward. This indifference ends if they take damage or witness their allies taking damage. When the spell ends, their attitude's returns to normal.

---
*Upcast: Target 1 additional creature per spell level.*
