# Enthrall

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You weave a distracting string of words, attempting to draw the attention of all creatures that can see or hear you within range.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against each creature's [Magicka](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Magicka.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). Any creature you or your allies are engaged in [Combat](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Combat.md) with resist this spell automatically. On a success, the creature is enthralled by your performance and does not look away or notice other things going on nearby.

---
*Upcast: Increase the range by 1 per spell level.*
