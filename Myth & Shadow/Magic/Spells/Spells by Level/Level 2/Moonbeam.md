# Moonbeam

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 2 [Zones](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)    |

A silvery beam of pale light shines down on a target, affecting it and everything [Engaged](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Engaged.md) with it. This beam extends into the heavens, or the roof if indoors and acts like magical [Light](../Cantrips/Light.md).

When the beam appears, you make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against each affected target's [Health](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Health.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, the target takes 2d12 [Tier 2](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%202.md) [Radiant](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Radiant.md) damage, and if the creature is shape-shifted (as a result [Polymorph](../Level%204/Polymorph.md), for example), it reverts to its true form and can't shape-shift until it leaves the beam. On a failure, a creature takes [Half](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Half.md) as much damage only. You may repeat these [Spellcasting Checks](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) at the start of each subsequent [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) for any target's in the beam.

---
*Upcast: Deal an additional 1d12 damage per spell level.*
