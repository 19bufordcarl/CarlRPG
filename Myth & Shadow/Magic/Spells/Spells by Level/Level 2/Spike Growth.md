# Spike Growth

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | 2 [Zones](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)    |

You conjure a growth of sprouting magically hard spiky thorns for the duration.

Choose up to 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) you can see within range. [Movement](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Movement.md) in this [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) becomes [Difficult Movement](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Movement.md#Difficult%20Movement) and whenever a creature enters this [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md) for the first time on a [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md), starts their [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) inside the [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md), or ends their [Turn](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Turn.md) inside this [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md), they take 2d6 [Tier 2](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Tiers/Tier%202.md) [Edged](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Edged.md) damage.

The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look entirely natural to the untrained eye. If a creature has a higher [Survival](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Secondary%20Skills/Survival.md) [Proficiency](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Skills.md#Proficiency) tier than the caster's [Arcana](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Primary%20Skills/Arcana.md) [Proficiency](../../../../Player%20Characters/Skills/Skills.md#Proficiency) tier.

---
*Upcast: Deal 1d6 additional damage per spell level.*
