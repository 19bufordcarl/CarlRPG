# Web

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled                                                       |
| Range    | 1 [Zone](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Zone.md)     |

You create a sticky web for the duration. Choose up to 4 creatures within range.

You make a [Spellcasting Check](../../../Spellcasting/Spellcasting%20Check.md) against each target's [Stamina](../../../../Player%20Characters/Attributes/Stamina.md) [Spell Resist DC](../../Spell%20Resist%20DC.md). On a success, that target is [Restrained](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Restrained.md). They may use their [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) to free themselves of the web and cease being [Restrained](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Restrained.md). Taking [Fire](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Combat/Damage/Damage%20Types/Fire.md) damage also destroys the webs immediately, but the affected starts [Burning](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Burning.md).

---
*Upcast: Target 1 additional creature per spell level.*
