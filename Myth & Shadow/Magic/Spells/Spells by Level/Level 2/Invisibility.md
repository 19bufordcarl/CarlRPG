# Invisibility

| Level    | [2nd Level Spell](2nd%20Level%20Spells.md)                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting  | 1 [Action](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Core%20Procedures/Action.md) |
| Duration | Until dispelled ([Concentration](../../Concentration.md))             |
| Range    | Touch                                                                 |

A target you touch becomes [Invisible](../../../../Game%20Procedures/Conditions/Invisible.md) for the duration.

This spell ends if the affected target casts a spell or causes damage to a creature or object. This applies even if they indirectly cause damage i.e. pushing someone off a cliff.

---
*Upcast: Target up to 1 additional creature per spell level.*
