# Character Creation

The standard 5.2 character creation process is modified in the following ways to make it easier for new players to follow, as well as allowed experienced players more dynamic choices.

## Modified Standard Ability Array

New PCs assign their ability scores with one of the following standard arrays (modified).

>*The Balanced Start*
>16 (+3), 16 (+3), 14 (+2), 10 (+0), 10 (+0), 8 (-1)

>*Looking to the Future*
>17 (+3), 15 (+2), 14 (+2), 10 (+0), 10 (+0), 8 (-1)

>*No Negatives*
>16 (+3), 14 (+2), 14 (+2), 12 (+1), 10 (+0), 10 (+0)

>*Min-Maxed*
>17 (+3), 16 (+3), 15 (+2), 9 (-1), 8 (-1), 7 (-2)

*The Balanced Start* will generally be better from levels 1-3, while *Looking to the Future* will generally be better at levels 4 and later. *No Negatives* is good for a more jack of all trades style character. *Min-Maxed* will only be better at levels 4 and later for certain specialized characters.

## Classes

See narrative notes for [Classes](Classes/Classes.md) in the land of Chardenfell. There are also new [Subclass Options](Classes/Subclasses/Subclass%20Options.md), but those are not important until 3rd level.

## Backgrounds

Your background can be anything you want. Think "former pirate", "former priest", or something along those lines. You can be as vague or specific as you desire! With it, you get:

- 2 skill proficiencies
- One of the standard [Languages](Species/Languages.md)
- A tool proficiency and the appropriate tools for it if your intelligence is 10 or more
- An Explorer's Pack or similar relevant pack
- 50 GP
