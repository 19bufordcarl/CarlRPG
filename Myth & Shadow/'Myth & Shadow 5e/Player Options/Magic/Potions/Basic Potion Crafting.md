# Basic Potion Crafting

All potion crafting requires the following ingredients (~10 GP base)

1. A glass vial (1 SP)
2. Purified alcohol (1 SP per dose)
3. An assortment of arcane salts and sodas (10+ GP)
4. An essence of a magical ingredient of rarity commensurate with the potion's strength

Brewing potions can only be done over a period of extended downtime, which usually occurs in between sessions.
