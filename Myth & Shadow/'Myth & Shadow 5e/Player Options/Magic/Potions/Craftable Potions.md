# Craftable Potions

## Healing Potions

Any PC trained in the *Medicine* skill and proficient with alchemist tools knows how to make healing potions.

| Potion           | Effect                    | Cost (GP) |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | --------- |
| Healing          | 10 HP                     | 50        |
| Greater Healing  | 20 HP                     | 100       |
| Superior Healing | 40 HP                     | 500       |
| Supreme          | 60 HP + Heal 1 death save | 1000      |
