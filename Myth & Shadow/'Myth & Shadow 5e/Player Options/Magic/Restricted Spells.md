# Restricted Spells

See reference spell list at <https://www.aidedd.org/spell/>

## 1st Level

1. Comprehend Languages[^1]
2. Goodberry[^3]

## 2nd Level

1. None!

## 3rd Level

1. Animate Dead[^2]
2. Create Food and Water[^3]
3. Sending[^1]
4. Speak with Dead[^2]
5. Tongues[^1]

## 4th Level

All spells 4th level or higher need to be found in world prior to being able to be learned.

[^1]: Lost to the ages.

[^2]: Dark Arts: Requires a dark ritual to learn. Illegal to cast in civilization.

[^3]: Material Cost: Converts something inedible but valuable into food rather than thin air.
