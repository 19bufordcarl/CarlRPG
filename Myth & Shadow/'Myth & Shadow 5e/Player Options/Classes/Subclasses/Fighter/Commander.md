# Commander

On the battlefield, victory is achieved through discipline, rigorous training and audacity. Commanders can often be seen leading the charge while bolstering their allies.

## Level 3: Commanding Presence

Gain proficiency in Intimidation and Insight.

## Level 3: Rousing Shout

You can use your bonus action to give all allies within 30 feet of you (including yourself) advantage on their next attack roll until the start of your next turn. Rousing Shout can be used a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier plus your Charisma modifier. All expended uses recharge on a long rest.

## Level 7: Defender of the People

When you take an attack action, you can forgo one of your attacks to grant an ally within 30 feet the benefits of the Dodge action until the start of your next turn.

## Level 10: Invigorating Shout

Rousing Shout's range increases from 30 feet to 60 feet. Allies affected by Rousing Shout now also gain temporary hit points equal to your level for 1 minute.
