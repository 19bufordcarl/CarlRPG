# Classes

The class options and abilities are the same as they are described in "5.2 SRD" or similar reference document.

The in-world reasoning provided for how that class gains abilities can be different. It is recommended you choose one of the following *Knowledge Infrastructures* that will determine the narrative for how your character acquired their skills. They may also provide certain benefits or restrictions according to the GM as appropriate. *Also see [Restricted Spells](../Magic/Restricted%20Spells.md).*

In return for these restrictions, a variety of new [Subclass Options](Subclasses/Subclass%20Options.md) are provided.

## Main Knowledge Infrastructures

### Arcane Guilds

Institutions of academics that collect arcane knowledge. Varying motivations and moral standards.

The most famous arcane guild in Chardenfell is House Telvanni. This is an ancient and a powerful association of primarily dark elves who practice all sorts of magical arts and have many very powerful mages. Their ranks are open to all who prove that they have sufficient magical talent, but they are rather callous when it comes to their practice of magic and many Telvanni master mages don't have the time nor the patience for truly teaching their apprentices. Thankfully elves live for a rather long time compared to humans, so this balances out a bit. The only magic that they generally don't practice is necromancy, specifically reanimation of the dead or their spirits in any way. The Temple of Vekh, the primary religion of the dark elves, views their ancestor spirits with extreme reverence and even worship. Any reanimation of the dead except in specific religiously sanctioned ceremonies is highly illegal and punishable by death.

### Knightly Orders

Most mortal monarchs or councils have their own orders of warriors to whom they entrust their protection and military matters.

The most famous and traditional knightly order would be House Redoran. They are the largest house and primarily dedicated to the ways of the traditional dark elf warrior. They value honor and bravery in battle and mastery over ones body and soul in the practice of martial arts and defense of their liege lord. They are a proud people and employ their services to those with just and honorable causes, rather than primarily for monetary gain.

### Religious Orders

There are many religious orders dedicated to the various different gods in the pantheon of the world of Mythril.

The dominant religious order in Chardenfell is the Tribunal Temple, whose heads are the three living gods. Vekh, Almalexia, and Sotha Sil. Their mythology claims that they were once a mortal dark elves who ascended to godhood through apotheosis. Chardenfell thus exists in a unique way compared to many of the other regions of the world. Their gods are much closer, more personable, and more directly powerful and influential over their people. Where the human gods are distant and fleeting, the gods of the dark elves are immediate and constant. To what extent they are truly "divine" is highly questioned outside of Chardenfell. Perhaps they are just incredibly powerful elven sorcerer kings. After all, who can tell the difference between especially powerful magic and godhood except for the gods themselves?

### Mercantile Organizations

There are many different mercantile organizations throughout the world of Mythril.

The most famous and influential mercantile organization in Chardenfell is House Hlaalu. They are another ancient house of primarily dark elves that have been the leaders of mercantile endeavors, shipwrights, architecture, and other economic and artisanal endeavors throughout the ages of Chardenfell. Their ranks are the most open to outsiders of any of the great houses of Chardenfell since they have the most experiences with the cultures and peoples outside of their realm due to trade and other activities.

### Secret Societies

Throughout the provinces, sometimes in cities and sometimes in villages, there may be secret societies dedicated to a uniting purpose. It might be a local thieves' guild dedicated towards pooling plunder for ease of fencing. It could be an archaeological society looking for long lost artifacts. It could be a witches coven.

The most famous secret society in Chardenfell are the Camonna Tong, also known as the Brotherhood. They are primarily an organized crime syndicate and have varying levels of influence throughout Chardenfell, but are most primarily located in the coastal towns of House Hlaalu.

### Hidden Groves

Throughout the wilderness of Mythril, there are rumored to be hidden groves of mortals who band together to exist outside the power structures of normal civilization.

*If your character is from one of these secret societies or hidden groves, think about why they would have left. Is it temporary -- something like a pilgrimage? Is it permanent -- were they exiled? What lengths would they go to protect the knowledge of their community from being discovered? Know that the discovery of your secret would put your life and your community in great peril.*
