# Species

The world of Mythril has a modified list of species for PCs to choose from. Chardenfell is the home of the Dark Elves. The species listed below are the only ones you would expect to run into during the course of a campaign. Choosing from this list would result in a character that fits in the world better, but is not strictly required.

## [Human](Species%20List/Human.md) (Unmodified)

The most numerous and varied humanoid species in the known world. One such famous culture is the people of Skrym, often referred to as the Nordfolk. They are known for their hardy nature, tolerance for cold climates, and prowess for fierce drinking.

## [Elf](Species%20List/Elf.md) (Modified)

The oldest and most storied species in the world of Mythril. There are many different cultures of elves with wildly varying physical and magical features. Ancient myths and tales of ages long past tell of many more types of elves, though the only ones with prominent civilizations today are the High Elves, Wood Elves, Snow Elves, and the Dark Elves. The Dark Elves are the natives of Chardenfell and its primary inhabitants.

## [Orc](Species%20List/Orc.md) (Unmodified)

The Orcs are a physically imposing folk, most identifiable by their great tusks. Orcish societies are often isolationist, but varied in structure. There are nomadic tribes who have seldom contact with the outside world, but there are also tales of great Orcish cities high in the mountains renowned for their prowess in metallurgy.

## [Catfolk](Species%20List/Catfolk.md) (New)

The Catfolk are found in a great many places throughout the known world taking many different shapes and sizes. They have generally always lived alongside humans and are often included in any human societies. They are, however, considerably rarer than humans. This is because their offspring do not always take the form of another catfolk. Oftentimes, they are simply born as another type of cat. How exactly their reproduction works is a mystery to Alchemists today, but a subject of great inquiry with many competing theories. According to their culture it is governed by the movements of the moon and other celestial bodies.

## [Lizardfolk](Species%20List/Lizardfolk.md) (New)

The Lizardfolk hail from the dark sprawling caverns and swamps of the Underdark. They do not often have contact with the other species, save for the dark elves who have encountered them in their extensive crystalline mines. The dark elves do not have a good relationship with the Lizardfolk, and they are often slavers to them. Orthodox dark elf philosophy tends towards a 'might makes right' stance.

Little is known about many parts of the Underdark, as there are many flora and fauna there that are incredibly toxic to outsiders. But, from the few Lizardfolk who emigrate from there to the surface, they seem to be a very adaptable folk and generally just as capable as the other known species.
