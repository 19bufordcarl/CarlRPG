# **Reference Sheet**

## **Measuring Time**

| Unit of Time | Duration   | Enough Time To                                           |
| ------------ | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Round        | 10 seconds | Perform an action in combat, take a round rest (page 52) |
| Stretch      | 15 minutes | Explore a room, take a stretch rest (page 52)            |
| Shift        | 6 hours    | Hike for 15 kilometers, take a shift rest (page 52)      |

---

## **Combat**

### Free Actions

- **Draw Weapon:** Draw, exchange, or put away a weapon kept at hand.
- **Change Position:** Throw yourself to the ground or get up.
- **Drop Item:** Drop an item on the ground.
- **Shout:** Say or shout a few words.

### Actions

- **Dash:** This action doubles your movement rate in the round.
- **Melee Attack:** Performed against an enemy within 2 meters (4 meters for long weapons).
- **Ranged Attack:** Attacks with a ranged weapon can be made against targets within the weapon's range.
- **Parry:** Both melee and ranged attacks can be parried, but the latter requires a shield. Parrying is a reaction and replaces your regular action.
- **Dodge:** Dodging melee or ranged attacks is also a reaction.
- **Pick Up Item:** Pick up an item within 2 meters or from your inventory.
- **Equip / Unequip Armor / Helmet:** Suits and helmets protect but restrict movement.
- **First Aid:** The HEALING skill is used to save the life of someone at zero HP.
- **Rally:** Use PERSUADE on a character at zero HP to rally them.
- **Break Down Door:** Doors require a certain amount of damage.
- **Pick Lock:** Requires SLEIGHT OF HAND; no lockpicks → bane.
- **Use Item:** Use a potion or other item within 2 meters.
- **Activate Ability:** Use innate or heroic abilities.
- **Cast Spell:** Usually an action; magic tricks included. Some spells are reactions or take longer.
- **Helping:** Gives a boon to a roll in the same round.
- **Round Rest:** Recover D6 WP. Once per shift.

# **Healing & Rest**

| Type of Rest | Effect                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Round Rest   | Recover D6 WP. Once per shift.                                                                                   |
| Stretch Rest | Heal D6 HP, or 2D6 HP if someone succeeds with HEALING roll. Recover D6 WP and heal 1 condition. Once per shift. |
| Shift Rest   | Recover all HP and WP, heal all conditions.                                                                      |

# **D20 Injury Table**

|D20|Injury|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|1–2|Broken nose|Bane on all AWARENESS rolls. Healing time: D6 days.|
|3–4|Scarred face|Bane on all PERFORMANCE and PERSUASION rolls. Healing time: 2D6 days.|
|5–6|Teeth knocked out|PERFORMANCE and PERSUASION permanently reduced by 2 (min 3).|
|7–8|Broken ribs|Bane on all STR and AGL skills. Healing time: D6 days.|
|9–10|Concussion|Bane on all INT-based skills. Healing time: D6 days.|
|11–12|Deep wounds|Bane on STR and AGL skills; each such roll deals D6 damage. Healing time: 2D6 days.|
|13|Broken leg|Movement halved. Healing time: 3D6 days.|
|14|Broken arm|Cannot use 2-handed or dual weapons; bane on actions using both arms. Healing: 3D6 days.|
|15|Severed toe|Movement permanently –2 (min 4).|
|16|Severed finger|Weapon skills –1 permanently (min 3).|
|17|Gouged eye|SPOT HIDDEN permanently –2 (min 3).|
|18|Nightmares|Roll to resist fear each shift; failed sleep doesn't count. Healing: 2D6 days.|
|19|Changed personality|Generate a new weakness (page 26).|
|20|Amnesia|Cannot remember identity; must roleplay. Healing: D6 days.|

# **Fear**

| D8  | Effect                                                     |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Enfeebled. Lose 2D6 WP (min 0) and become Disheartened.    |
| 2   | Shaken → Scared condition.                                 |
| 3   | Panting → Exhausted.                                       |
| 4   | Pale. You and all PCs within 10 meters become Scared.      |
| 5   | Scream. All who hear must resist the same fear attack.     |
| 6   | Rage. Must attack the fear source; become Angry.           |
| 7   | Paralyzed. Cannot act; roll WIL each turn to recover.      |
| 8   | Wild Panic. Must dash away; roll WIL each turn to recover. |
