# Falcon Heist

## General Overview

In the magical city of Astrea, there is a figure named Lord Delain. He is wealthy and powerful and holds many magical artifacts across several vaults in his sprawling estate. There is an upcoming masquerade he is hosting. Roughly 200 guests are invited, and he has an extensive private guard employed to boot.

A local thief, Marlowe, is down on his luck and in need of a score to pay back his loan shark, a local crime boss by of Vex Mortain is going to get his due from Marlowe one way or another. Marlowe meets the player at a local bar. The player is contracted to help Marlowe.

## Secrets and Clues

1. Some of the patrons of the masquerade sneak in drugs -- truly magical mushrooms that cause them to trip intensely. They go to hang out in one of the reading rooms inside the estate.
2. Marlowe is indebted to a crime lord Vex Mortain who wants the gilded falcon, and he will be in deep trouble if he fails to pay back his debt soon.
3. The vineyard has all sorts of magical enhancements to the vegetation to make it thrive better than it normally would
4. Lord and Lady Delain have immense wealth, but their true desire at this point is political power. They seek to aid the Mage House Dusk, of which they are high ranking members, to overtake Mage House Dawn.
5. Mage House Dusk is generally considered more on the darker side of magic, while Mage House Dawn is generally considered on the more altruistic side.
6. Mage House Dawn has their own share of internal power struggles, however. Their founder, an elf named Melvar, has been banished to a pocket dimension by Steward Cairos -- the leader of an upstart faction within house Dawn who seeks to work with house Dusk to lead Astrea on a military campaign to "spread their greatness" to the rest of the world.
7. Lord Delain has met with Cairos many times, and will meet with him at this Masquerade as well. He sees him as convenient for now, and will betray him when the time is right. Cairos has no love for house Dusk either, and will act similarly. They are only loyal to each other out of convenience, and they are both power hungry.
8. The Gilded Falcon lies in the Library's secret chamber. It is the oldest vault in the estate, and the least secure with the least valuables. Lord Delain simply has it linked to a book titled "On Advanced Falconry" which when moved triggers the mechanism opening a false door.
9. The Gilded Falcon vault is guarded by a sprite named Belair. Belair hates Lord Delain for trapping him in this chamber and bound to his service. It can only be broken if Lord Delain gives him a gift of some sort. Belair is willing to bargain rather than attack. The animated armor in the vault however is always hostile.
10. The taking of the Gilded Falcon sets off a magical alarm in one of the mage guards head. They will dispatch guards to the library as fast as possible, so it will be up to the distractions the group set up beforehand to slow them down.
