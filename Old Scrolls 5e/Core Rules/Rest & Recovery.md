# Rest & Recovery

During an adventure, you might have the chance to rest and recover your hit points and resources.

## Short Rest

A short rest is a 10-minute period of downtime. You can't use actions during this time. If you do so, you must start the rest over.

**Benefits of the Rest.** When you finish the rest, you regain all lost hit points and the use of features that are restored with a short rest.

## Long Rest

A long rest is a period of downtime that lasts at least 8 hours.

**Benefits of the Rest.** When you finish the rest, you regain all lost hit points and the use of features that are restored with a short or long rest.

**Interrupting the Rest.** A long rest is stopped if you take any damage or do anything more strenuous than read, tend to your camp, or eat.

If you rested at least 10 minutes before the interruption, you gain the benefits of a short rest.

You can resume a long rest immediately after an interruption. If you do so, the rest requires 1 additional hour per interruption to finish.

## Downtime

A period of extended downtime is at least a day, often it is a week or more. Some advancement requires a period of extended downtime. Leveling up or some other features require a period of extended downtime. The DM may override this rule for a faster paced campaign.
