# Treasure

The characters in this game have relatively bare advancement tables. You can compensate for that by using these treasure rules to give characters magic items.

## Gold Pieces

Use the table below to determine the gold piece value of coins, gems, and jewels found in the treasure. Half should be coins, half in gems and other trinkets.

| Level | GP Value      |
| ----- | ------------- |
| 1 – 4 | 1d20 x 5 GP   |
| 5 – 8 | 1d20 x 20 GP  |
| 9+    | 1d20 x 100 GP |

## Magic Armor

This section details the core magic armor properties.

**Flexible.** This armor is light and flexible enough that you gain your Dexterity modifier to AC while wearing it, despite heavy armor not normally allowing that.

**Fortified.** If you suffer a critical hit, roll a d20. On a 16 or higher, it instead becomes a normal hit.

**Hidden.** This armor is small and light enough that it can be worn under clothing without being noticed.

**Resistance.** You gain resistance to an energy type chosen or determined at random from the table below.

| d6 | Damage Type |
|----|-------------|
| 1 | Acid |
| 2 | Cold |
| 3 | Fire |
| 4 | Lightning |
| 5 | Poison |
| 6 | Thunder |

**Shadowy.** You can attempt to hide from any creature that is two or more areas away from you.

**Stout.** The armor's base AC increases by 1. A suit of armor can have this trait up to three times, increasing its AC by 3.

**Warded.** You gain an additional +2 AC against ranged attacks.

## Magic Shields

This section details the core magic shield properties.

**Alert.** If you are surprised, roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, you are not surprised.

**Blocking.** You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity saving throws.

**Guardian.** You gain a +2 bonus to AC against opportunity attacks. This benefit can stack up to three times, to a maximum of +6.

**Resistance.** You gain resistance to an energy type chosen or determined at random from the table below.

| d6 | Damage Type |
|----|-------------|
| 1 | Acid |
| 2 | Cold |
| 3 | Fire |
| 4 | Lightning |
| 5 | Poison |
| 6 | Thunder |

**Thundering.** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can also push them horizontally in a straight line up to 10 feet.

**Vigilant.** You gain advantage on initiative rolls.

**Warded.** You gain an additional +2 AC against ranged attacks.

## Magic Weapons

This section details the different magic weapon properties.

**Accurate.** You gain a +1 to +3 bonus to attacks made with this weapon.

**Brutal.** If you miss with an attack with this weapon, the target takes force damage equal to your Strength modifier.

**Defensive.** You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wielding this weapon.

**Returning.** When used to make a ranged attack, this weapon returns to its attacker.

## Creating Magic Weapons

Use the tables below to create new magic weapons.

Roll once for a common weapon, twice for an uncommon one, three times for a rare one, and four times for a very rare one.

This table grants a power upgrade, either a bonus to attacks or an increase in damage. You can stack the increased damage die and accurate property up to three times. A weapon can gain defensive only once. Reroll a benefit you can't apply to a weapon.

| d6 | Benefit |
|----|---------|
| 1 – 3 | Increased damage die |
| 4 – 5 | Accurate property |
| 6 | Defensive property |

This table summarizes how to increase a weapon's damage dice.

| Original Die | Increased Die |
|--------------|---------------|
| d4 | d6 |
| d6 | d8 |
| d8 | d10 |
| d10 | d12 |
| d12 | 2d8 |
| 2d4 | d12 |
| 2d6 | 2d8 |
| 2d8 | 2d10 |
| 2d10 | 2d12 |

| D6 | Result |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Reach |
| 2 | Thrown, Returning |
| 3 | Brutal |
