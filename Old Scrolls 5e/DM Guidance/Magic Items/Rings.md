# Rings

Magic rings are potent items. Typically, characters can only attune to 2 magic rings at a time.

## Uncommon Rings

|----|------|
| 1 | Beastkin |
| 2 | Brachiation |
| 3 | Splendor |
| 4 | Terror |

| 6 | Wave Walker |

## Ring of the Battering Ram

Rarity: Rare

As an action, you can touch this ring to an object that is the size of a door or larger. The object takes 5d6 force damage.

## Ring of Beastkin

Rarity: Uncommon

You gain the ability to speak with animals. You understand their speech and they understand you.

## Ring of Brachiation

Rarity: Uncommon

You gain a climb speed.

## Ring of Evasive Defense

Rarity: Rare

When you fail a Dexterity save, you can instead succeed it. Once you use this ring, it cannot be used again until you finish a long rest.

## Ring of Feathery Landings

Rarity: Rare

You take no damage from falling and always land on your feet.

## Ring of Invisibility

Rarity: Very Rare

While wearing this ring, you are invisible. If you attack a creature or cast a spell, you become visible at the end of your turn and the ring does not function for 1d4 hours.

## Ring of Life Force

Rarity: Very Rare

If you would gain your third failed death save, roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, you do not gain. Once you gain this ring's benefit it does not function again until you finish a long rest.

## Ring of Mighty Leaps

Rarity: Rare

In place of moving, you can jump up to 30 feet horizontally and 15 feet vertically.

## Ring of Night Eyes

Rarity: Rare

You gain darkvision with a range of 30 feet. If you already have dark vision, you extend its range by 30 feet.

## Ring of Piercing Sight

Rarity: Very Rare

As an action, you can press the hand on which you wear this ring against a surface. If it is 1 inch thick or less, you can view the area on the other side. You gain no special ability see in the dark when viewing an area in this way.

## Ring of Protection

Rarity: Rare

You gain +1 AC and +1 on all saving throws.

## Ring of Resistance

Rarity: Very Rare

You gain advantage on one type of saving throw, determined by rolling on the table below.

| d6 | Resistance |
|----|------------|
| 1 | Strength |
| 2 | Intelligence |
| 3 | Wisdom |
| 4 | Dexterity |
| 5 | Constitution |
| 6 | Charisma |

## Ring of Splendor

Rarity: Uncommon

As an action, you can cause your dress to change to match a general or specific set of clothes. Your clothes physically change, but if you remove them they return to their original form.

## Ring of Star Storms

Rarity: Very Rare

As an action, you can make a ranged attack with this ring using your Dexterity modifier and proficiency. The ring's range is 100 feet. The attack ignores cover. Its damage is 6d6 radiant. If you miss with the attack, you cannot use the ring for 1d10 minutes.

## Ring of Terror

Rarity: Uncommon

You have a +4 bonus to all Intimidation checks.

## Ring of the Wave Rider

Rarity: Uncommon

You gain a swim speed.

## Ring of the Wave Walker

Rarity: Uncommon

You can walk across liquids as of they were solid ground.

## Ring of Wishes

Rarity: Very Rare

When found, this ring has 1d3 wishes. As an action, you can concentrate and state what you wish for. The ring fulfills it in a literal manner. After the wishes are exhausted, the ring turns to lead.
