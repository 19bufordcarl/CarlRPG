## Amulet of Constitution

Rarity: Rare

You gain +1 Constitution while wearing this amulet.

## Bag of Rats

Rarity: Uncommon

As an action, you can reach your hand into his bag and pull out up to 1d4 giant rats. The rats obey your commands to the best of their abilities. You can communicate with them and understand their chittering. After an hour, or if they are slain, they disappear. When you use this bag, you cannot draw rats from it again for 4 hours.

## Bead of Containment

Rarity: Rare

As an action, you can hurl this bead up to 90 feet away from you. On impact, the bead expands into a gleaming wall of force 1 inch thick, 10 feet tall, and up to 50 feet wide. The wall expands 25 feet in opposite directions chosen by you. Each 10-foot segment of the wall has 100 hit points. The wall lasts for 10 minutes. The bead is consumed when it is used.

## Belt of Strength

Rarity: Very Rare

You gain +1 Strength while wearing this belt.

## Boots of Airy Steps

Rarity: Very Rare

While wearing these boots, you can walk vertically at an angle of up to 45 degrees. You cannot fall while wearing these boots unless you choose to.

## Boots of Elvenkind

Rarity: Uncommon

While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

## Boots of Leaping

Rarity: Rare

As an action, you can leap up to 30 feet.

## Boots of Swift Steps

Rarity: Rare

These boots grant you a +30 foot bonus to speed.

## Bracers of Protection

Rarity: Varies

While wearing these bracers, your armor class becomes the higher of your normal AC or the AC provided by the bracers. There are three varieties of these bracers.

Rarity AC Uncommon 14 Rare 16 Very Rare 18

## Cape of Unexpected Escapes

Rarity: Rare

In place of moving, you can teleport up to 30 feet to a spot you can see. When you use this cape, roll a d6. On a 1, the cape's magic is expended. You must wait 4 hours before you can use it again.

## Carpet of Flying

Rarity: Very Rare

This carpet can carry up to four Medium creatures or one Large one. It can fly at a speed of 90 feet as directed by a creature bound to it. To bind to the carpet, a creature must spend a long rest sleeping on it. The last creature to do so is the carpet's master.

## Cloak of the Scout

Rarity: Uncommon

This cloak's colors blend in with environment around you. While you are within 5 feet of a natural feature – rocks, a cliff, plants – that is as tall as you, you can attempt to hide.

## Cloak of Spiders

Rarity: Rare

While wearing this cloak, you gain a climb speed and can climb across horizontal surfaces.

## Crystal Ball of Visions

Rarity: Very Rare

If you spend one minute concentrating on this item, you can make a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check. If you succeed, you can name a location you have visited or state a direction and distance from you. A vision of the current state of that location in a radius up to 10 feet from a point of your choice, appears in the crystal ball for up to 1 minute.

## Gloves of Climbing

Rarity: Uncommon

These gloves grant you a climb speed and you take half damage from falls.

## Gloves of Swimming

Rarity: Uncommon

These gloves grant you a swim speed and the ability to breath water in addition to air.

## Hat of Intelligence

Rarity: Very rare

You gain +1 Intelligence while wearing this hat.

## Helm of Telepathy

Rarity: Rare

You can communicate via telepathy with any creature or creatures you can see. The communication is two way. If you are speaking with multiple creatures, you can allow them to converse with each other. When you can no longer see a creature, you cannot communicate with it in this manner.

## Lucky Charm

Rarity: Very rare

You can reroll an attack, check, or save. If the result is higher than your initial roll, you cannot use the charm for 1d4 hours.

## Rope of the Mountaineer

Rarity: Uncommon

As an action, you can command this 50foot coil of rope to unfurl, climb a surface, and secure itself. There must be some object, such a battlement, that the rope can tie itself around. A second action while touching the rope causes it to unknot and coil in your hand.
