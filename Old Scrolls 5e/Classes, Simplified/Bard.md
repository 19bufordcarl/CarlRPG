## Level 1: Bardic Influence

People and creatures take a natural liking to you. When you roll initiative, you can also make a DC 10 Charisma check. If you succeed, you call a truce and give both sides the opportunity to interact. If either side does something to offend the other, combat begins as normal. Otherwise, you have the chance to negotiate a truce.

## Level 1: Bardic Inspiration

During combat, your singing and oration inspire others. On your turn, you gain Bardic Inspiration dice based on your level. When you gain the dice, you can keep them or give some or all of them to friendly creatures that can hear you. A creature can roll a Bardic Inspiration die and add the result as a bonus to an attack roll, a damage roll, or a saving throw. Dice must be spent before rolling the d20 or damage.

All dice you gained on your last turn are lost at the start of your next turn when you regain your dice.

## Level 1: Bardic Magic

You pick up a strange mix of magic from your travels and studies. The table below summarizes when you learn spells. When you gain a spell, pick another class and roll on its table of your new spell's level. You learn that spell.

After you cast the spell, you cannot cast it again until you finish a long rest unless the spell is a cantrip. You cast a non-cantrip spell as if you used a spell slot equal to the spell's level.

The 'C' column is for cantrips.

| Level | C   | 1   | 2   |
| ----- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1     | 1   | –   | –   |
| 2     | 1   | 1   | –   |
| 4     | 2   | 1   | –   |
| 6     | 2   | 2   | –   |
| 8     | 2   | 2   | 1   |
| 10    | 2   | 2   | 2   |
