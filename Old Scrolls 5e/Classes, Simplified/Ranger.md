## Level 1: Rapid Strike

You can sacrifice power for rapid strikes.

**Swift Slash.** You can make two melee attacks as an action, but you do not add your ability modifier to your damage roll.

**Quick Shot.** You can make two ranged attacks as an action, but you do not add your ability modifier to your damage roll.

## Level 1: Natural Leader

You have unmatched experienced roaming the wilds and leading expeditions.

**Lead by Example.** When you make a check, you can provide guidance to your allies. Until your next turn, friendly creatures within 30 feet of you can use your result in place of rolling that same check.

**Druidic Novice.** You have picked up druidic magic allowing you to speak with animals. You must perform a ritual that takes 1 minute in order to activate this ability which then lasts for 1 hour.

## Level 3: Superior Strike

You can make a total of three attacks when you use rapid strike.

## Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 5: Whirling Strikes

You can make a total of four attacks when you use rapid strike.

## Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 9: Mighty Strikes

You can now add your ability modifier to damage rolls for attacks made with your Rapid Strike feature.
