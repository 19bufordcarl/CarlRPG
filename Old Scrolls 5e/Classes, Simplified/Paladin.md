## Level 1: Detect Soul

As an action, you can perform a sacred ritual to determine if a creature or object within 30 feet of you has a soul. If they do have a soul, you can sense if their soul is corrupted. Especially powerful creatures can conceal their true nature from this ability.

## Level 1: Sacred Aura

You exert an aura of a sacred, mystical power granted by your paladin oath. At the end of your turn, if a hostile creature is within 30 feet of you, creatures of your choice in that range regain hit points equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

## Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 3: Turn Undead

As an action, you present your sacred symbol. All undead within 30 feet of you must make Wisdom saves opposed by your Charisma check. If they fail, they are frightened of you for 1 minute or until they take damage. If they succeed, they are immune to your use of this feature for the next 24 hours.

## Level 5: Two Attacks

When you attack, you can make two weapon attacks.

## Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

## Level 7: Protective Aura

Your divine aura grows stronger. You and friendly creatures within 30 feet of you gain a bonus to all saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1).

## Level 9: Divine Smite

Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack, you can smite it to increase your damage by 3d10.
