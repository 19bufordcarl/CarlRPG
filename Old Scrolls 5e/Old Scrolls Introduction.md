# Old Scrolls Introduction

The Old Scrolls TTRPG is a compilation of streamlined rules for 5e inspired by the playtests of Mike Mearls, one of the lead designers on 5e for his take on what a streamlined spiritual successor to 5e would look like. It has an opinionated take on classes, moving away from some of the classics expected by many prior iterations of fantasy tabletop orthodoxy and instead is more inspired by the Elder Scrolls series view of a fantasy world.

This world is the Old Scrolls, very legally distinct. It is a world where magic is prevalent, but limited. The gods are but distant echoes of their legends. And the myths of the ancient past still haunt the present.

Most of the rules will be quite similar to 5e with a few places streamlined and a few places with additional options added. Overall, it is a lighter ruleset with less of a focus on precisely simulating reality with its rule text and more of a focus on plain English descriptions of abilities.
