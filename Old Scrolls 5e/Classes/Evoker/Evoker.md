# Evoker

As an evoker, you have learned how to harness one of the three core magical energies, fire, frost, and shock. Rather than take a generalist approach to magic where you learn specific spells, you focus instead on specializing and wielding your elemental energy to the fullest effect.

As an evoker, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Evoker Features table.

## Level 1: Evocation

You have learned to harness the elemental powers of magic.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is the spellcasting ability for evoker features. If you make an attack roll with an evoker spell or feature, your bonus is your spellcasting modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Spell Power.** When you use an evoker feature, its effects might be determined by your power. Many features have effects that scale, such as damage, based on your power.

**Mana Points.** Your magic is powered by mana. You gain mana points as indicated in the Evoker Features table. You can spend a number of points per turn up to your power.

You regain up to a number of mana points equal to your power when you finish a short rest, and all expended mana points when you finish a long rest.

**Arcane Bolt.** As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against a target within 120 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d10 + your spellcasting modifier damage. The damage increases to 2d10 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d10 if your power is 5 or greater. The damage type is either fire, cold, lightning, or thunder depending on your elemental specialty.

Before rolling your attack you can expend a mana point to increase the damage by 1d10 per point spent, and the target takes half damage on a miss.

# Elemental Specialties

Here are the elemental specialties to choose from. Each specialty gives you a set of core benefits, along with expanded benefits as your level increases.

## Pyromancy

You are trained in the power of fire, a destructive force that burns away your enemies. Fire is impossible to fully control. When you use your magic, you merely direct the power you tap into and are sometimes as surprised at the effects as others.

### Burning Flames

When rolling fire damage, if you roll the maximum possible result on a die you may roll an additional die of that type as additional fire damage. This benefit applies to the additional die you roll.

### Flickering Flame

As a magic action, you can cause fire to erupt from one of your hands. This fire sheds bright light for 20 feet and dim light for an additional 20 feet. As an action, you can touch a flammable object in melee range and cause it to ignite. You can choose to douse your hand on your turn without using an action.

### Gout of Flame

As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against up to three targets within 30 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 2d6 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d6 if your power is 5 or greater.

You can expend mana points to increase the attacks' damage by 2d6 per point spent, and a target takes half damage on a miss. Spend these points before rolling your attacks.

### Power 2: Cinder Cloud

If your power is 2 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to cause a great gout of smoke and cinders to erupt from your hands. Choose a point within range. The area within a 20-foot radius of that point fills with smoke that heavily obscures it for 1 minute per mana point spent or until you use this feature again. At the end of each of your turns while this effect lasts, creatures in the smoke-filled area take 1d6 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect.

### Unfettered Arcana – Raging Flame

When you gain the Unfettered Arcana class feature, you gain the following benefit.

On your turn, you can choose to channel a raging flame. For the next minute, when you roll fire damage you gain the benefits of your Burning Flames class feature when you roll the maximum value or one less than the maximum value on a die.

Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Power 3: Fireball

If your power is 3 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to hurl a burning ember at a point within 120 feet where it explodes. Each creature within a 20-foot radius of that point must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect or half that much on a successful save.

Until the end of your next turn, creatures that end their turn in the targeted area take 2 fire damage per mana point spent on this effect.

### Power 4: Conjure Flame Atronach

If your power is 4 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 4 mana points to conjure a flame atronach to serve you. It lasts for up to 10 minutes, disappearing when it is reduced to 0 hit points.

The flame atronach is size Medium and appears within 30 feet of you. It has AC 16 and hit points equal to your power times 5. If the flame atronach needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

The flame atronach uses your initiative and takes its turn after yours. It can use a melee attack with an attack bonus equal to 2 + your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting modifier that does 2d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage.

### Power 5: Fiery Form

If your power is 5 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 5 mana points to wreathe yourself in supercharged flames for 1 minute and gain the following benefits. You are immune to fire damage. You gain a fly speed of 60 feet. Once per turn when a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 5d6 fire damage. Your Burning Flames class feature now adds two additional dice when it triggers.

## Cryomancy

You are attuned to the power of cold, the stillness at the heart of creation. Ice is patient, implacable, and inevitable. Where fire consumes, cold preserves — and sometimes that preservation is a frozen death.

### Freezing Magic

Whenever you deal cold damage to a target, you can spend a mana point to force the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they cannot move on their next turn. They are paralyzed if your power is 3 or greater and they have less than 50 hit points remaining.

### Icy Touch

As a magic action, you can touch a body of water and freeze a 5-foot cube of it. You can also touch a non-magical object, such as a door, and freeze it solid. Opening the door requires a Strength (Athletics) check as an action. The DC equals your spell save DC. You can unfreeze the target at any time without using an action.

### Hail of Shards

As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against up to two targets within 30 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d8 + your spellcasting modifier cold damage. The damage increases to 2d8 if your power is 3 or greater and 3d8 if your power is 5 or greater.

You can expend mana points to increase the attacks' damage by 1d8 per point spent, and a target takes half damage on a miss. Spend these points before rolling your attacks.

### Power 2: Frozen Fog

If your power is 2 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to cause a freezing mist to erupt from your hands. Choose a point within range. The area within a 20-foot radius of that point fills with mist and is heavily obscured for one minute per mana point spent or until you use this feature again. The area is also difficult terrain. At the end of each of your turns while this effect lasts, creatures in the mist-filled area take 1d4 cold damage per mana point spent on this effect.

### Unfettered Arcana – Aegis of Frost

When you gain the Unfettered Arcana class feature, you gain the following benefit.

On your turn, you can choose to cloak yourself in frost armor. The armor appears as spectral frost that covers you and your gear. For the next hour, if your AC is lower than 18 it becomes 18. This frost armor has 5 hit points per power you have. When you would take damage, the frost armor takes the damage instead. If the damage source is within 10 feet, they take 5 cold damage per power you have. This effect ends when the frost armor reaches 0 hit points.

Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Power 3: Cone of Cold

If your power is 3 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to create a blast of extreme frost that erupts from your hands in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 1d8 cold damage per mana point spent on this effect or half that much on a successful save.

Until the end of your next turn, the targeted area becomes difficult terrain. Any water in the area is frozen solid.

### Power 4: Conjure Frost Atronach

If your power is 4 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 4 mana points to conjure a vicious frost atronach to serve you. It lasts for up to 10 minutes, disappearing when it is reduced to 0 hit points.

The frost atronach is size Medium and appears within 30 feet of you. It has AC 18 and hit points equal to your power times 5. If the frost atronach needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

The frost atronach uses your initiative and takes its turn after yours. It can use a melee attack with an attack bonus equal to 2 + your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting modifier that does 1d8 + your spellcasting modifier cold damage. The frost atronach can also interpose once per round, redirecting any attack at a creature within 5 feet of the frost atronach to target it instead.

### Power 5: Ice Sphere

If your power is 5 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 5 mana points to create a sphere of magical translucent ice for 1 minute that encloses a creature or object of Large size or smaller within 30 feet. An unwilling creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is enclosed for the duration.

Nothing – not physical objects, energy, or other spell effects – can pass through the ice sphere, in or out, though a creature in the sphere can breathe there. The sphere is immune to all damage, and a creature or object inside can't be damaged by attacks or effects originating from outside, nor can a creature inside the sphere damage anything outside it.

The ice sphere is weightless and just large enough to contain the creature or object inside. An enclosed creature can use its action to push against the sphere's walls and thus roll the sphere at up to half the creature's speed. Similarly, the sphere can be picked up and moved by other creatures.

When the spell ends, the ice sphere remains but ceases to be magical. It has AC 5 and 10 HP.

## Electromancy

You are attuned to the power of storm, the crackling energy that dances between earth and sky. Lightning is fast, precise, and unpredictable. When you call on it, you ride the razor's edge between control and chaos.

### Mage Bane

Whenever you deal lightning damage to a target, you can spend a mana point to force the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they have disadvantage on any spellcasting related checks they make until the end of their next turn.

### Static Charge

As a magic action, you can spend a mana point to suffuse one creature with static energy allowing it to defy gravity. One creature or loose object of your choice that you can see within 60 feet rises vertically, up to 20 feet, and remains suspended there for the duration. The spell can levitate a target that weighs up to 500 pounds. An unwilling creature that succeeds on a Constitution saving throw is unaffected.

The target can move only by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach (such as a wall or a ceiling), which allows it to move as if it were climbing. You can change the target's altitude by up to 20 feet in either direction on your turn. If you are the target, you can move up or down as part of your move. Otherwise, you can use your action to move the target, which must remain within 60 feet of you to continue floating.

### Chain Lightning

As a magic attack action, you can make a ranged attack against up to three targets within 60 feet. On a hit, this attack does 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier lightning damage. The damage increases to 1d12 if your power is 3 or greater and 2d12 if your power is 5 or greater.

You can expend mana points to increase the attacks' damage by 1d12 per point spent, and a target takes half damage on a miss. Spend these points before rolling your attacks.

### Power 2: Zephyr

If your power is 2 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to allow you to walk on air as if it were solid ground for 1 minute per mana point spent.

### Power 2: Thunder Blast

If your power is 2 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to cause the air at a point of your choice within 60 feet to erupt with a thunderous blast. Each creature within a 10-foot radius of that point must make a Constitution saving throw or take 1d8 thunder damage per mana point spent on this effect, or half that much on a successful save.

Creatures made of inorganic material such as stone, crystal, or metal have disadvantage on this saving throw. Non-magical objects automatically take maximum damage. If you touch the non-magical object, it takes double damage.

### Unfettered Arcana – Storm Cloak

When you gain the Unfettered Arcana class feature, you gain the following benefit.

On your turn, you can choose to cloak yourself in sparks of lightning. For the next minute, whenever a creature is within 10 feet of you, you can have a spark reach out and shock them, dealing 2d12 lightning damage. Once a creature has taken damage in this way, they cannot be dealt damage from this effect again until the start of your next turn.

Once you use this benefit, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Power 3: Lightning Bolt

If your power is 3 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 1 or more mana points to create a stroke of lightning forming a line of 100 feet long and 5 feet wide blasting out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d12 lightning damage per mana point spent on this effect or half that much on a successful save.

Until the end of your next turn, creatures that failed their saving throw are dazed.

### Power 4: Conjure Storm Atronach

If your power is 4 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 4 mana points to conjure a vicious storm atronach, to serve you. It lasts for up to 10 minutes, disappearing when it is reduced to 0 hit points.

The storm atronach is size Medium and appears within 30 feet of you. It has AC 16 and hit points equal to your power times 5. If the storm atronach needs to make a saving throw or check, it uses your bonus.

The storm atronach uses your initiative and takes its turn after yours. It can use a ranged attack with an attack bonus equal to 2 + your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting modifier that does 1d12 + your spellcasting modifier lightning damage. It can use a melee attack with the same attack bonus that does 2d8 + your spellcasting modifier thunder damage.

### Power 5: Storm Crowned

If your power is 5 or greater, you can use a magic action and expend 5 mana points to completely cloak yourself in lightning magic and gain the following benefits. You are immune to lightning damage. You have a fly speed of 60 feet. Once per turn when a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 2d12 lightning damage.

# Evocation Feats

Here are the core Evocation feats you can choose from.

## General Evocation Feats

**Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature**

These feats are available to any evoker, regardless of specialty.

### Arcane Sense

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature)**

Magical auras appear as shimmering lights to you, allowing you to detect magical objects or effects by sight. However, you cannot perceive the aura of an object that is hidden or invisible. If you take 10 minutes to study a magic object with this ability, you can discern its basic properties.

### Elemental Rune

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature)**

As a magic action, you can spend 1 or more mana points and store your raw elemental power in a magical glyph that you place on a surface within 10 feet. The rune is invisible to others but appears to you as a circle of arcane symbols in a circle occupying a 5 foot diameter. When a creature steps on the rune, it detonates, dealing 3d6 damage per mana point spent on this effect.

The damage type is determined by your specialty, either fire, cold, lightning, or thunder.

### Illusion Novice

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature)**

You learn the basics of illusion magic, allowing you to sculpt images as you wish. You gain the minor illusion and light cantrips. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for them.

### Restoration Novice

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature)**

You learn the basics of restoration magic. As a magic action, you can expend mana points and touch a creature to have it regain 2d8 hit points per point spent + your spellcasting modifier.

## Pyromancy Feats

**Prerequisite: Pyromancy Specialty**

### Cleansing Flame

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

Your fire can cauterize wounds and burn away affliction. As a magic action, you can expend mana points and touch a creature, which regains 2d8 hit points per mana point spent + your spellcasting modifier.

### Dancing Flames

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

You learn to sculpt fire into subtle illusions of light and heat. You gain the minor illusion and light cantrips from the Illusion spell list, manifesting them as dancing flames and heat shimmers. You use your evoker spellcasting ability for them.

### Deft Fire

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

You learn to deftly weave your fire around your allies. When you use magic that deals fire damage, you may reduce the damage it does to creatures of your choice to 0.

### Fire Jump

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

Once on your turn, you can spend 2 mana points and cause a burst of flames to erupt from under you, allowing you to move up to 30 feet in any direction without provoking opportunity attacks. This does not expend an action or any movement.

### Fireproof

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

You gain resistance to fire damage.

### Flame Whip

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

As a magic attack action, you can expend a mana point to make a melee attack against a creature within 30 feet using a conjured whip made of pure fire. On a hit, this attack does 2d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage and you can move the target 10 feet in any direction if they are size Medium or smaller. The damage increases to 4d6 if your power is 3 or greater and can move Large targets. The damage increases to 6d6 if your power is 5 or greater and can move Huge targets.

### Flaming Weapon

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

As a magic attack action, you can expend a mana point to cause a weapon you are wielding to become wreathed in flames. For the next 10 minutes, whenever you attack with the weapon it deals an additional 1d6 + your spellcasting modifier fire damage. The damage increases to 3d6 if your power is 3 or greater and 5d6 if your power is 5 or greater. On the turn that you take this action, you may also attack with the weapon once without using an action.

### Immolating Rebuke

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can expend 1 mana point and deal 2d6 fire damage to it.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a short rest.

### Infernal Stare

**Evocation Feat (Prerequisite: Evocation Technique Feature, Pyromancy)**

As a magic influence action, you can expend a mana point to let fire blaze in your eyes and voice. You can force up to 1 + your power creatures to make a Wisdom save. If they fail, they are frightened of you for the next minute.
