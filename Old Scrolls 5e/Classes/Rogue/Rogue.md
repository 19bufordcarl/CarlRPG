# Rogue

Rogues focus on skillful strikes, movement, stealth, and mastery over skills and knowledge rather than purely relying on raw combat ability like the fighter does.

## Core Rogue Traits

| **Primary Ability**            | Dexterity                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hit Points**                 | 10 at 1st level, 7 per level gained after 1st                                            |
| **Saving Throw Proficiencies** | Dexterity, Intelligence                                                                  |
| **Skill Proficiencies**        | Choose any 4 skills. If you choose a skill you already have, you gain expertise with it. |
| **Weapon Proficiencies**       | Choose 3: Blade, Blunt, Bow, Mechanical, Polearm, and Shield                             |
| **Starting Equipment**         | Padded Jacket, 2 Daggers, Sabre, Thieves' Tools                                          |

As a rogue, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified rogue levels.

## Level 1: Roguish Archetype

All rogues have a specialty, a focus for their activities that defines their career. Pick one of the following options. Each one gives you a special set of abilities.

- **Acrobat.** You gain a climb speed. You take no damage from falling. You can walk across surfaces as narrow as one inch, such as a rope.
- **Arcane Trickster.** You gain a wizard cantrip of your choice and can cast one wizard spell. Each time you gain a level, you can swap those choices for different options. You use the rules for casting spells as per the wizard class.
- **Assassin.** You are a hired killer capable of felling even the mightiest opponent with a single blow. You gain your Intelligence modifier as a bonus to damage rolls. As a search action, you can study a target within 30 feet. On your next turn, roll double the number of sneak attack dice you normally roll.
- **Bard.** You know how to play two musical instruments. You gain a mystic cantrip of your choice and can cast one mystic spell. Each time you gain a level, you can swap those choices for different options. You use the rules for casting spells as per the mystic class. When you cast that spell, you cannot cast it again until you finish a long rest. You can use Charisma as your spellcasting ability instead of Wisdom.
- **Brawler.** You gain an additional weapon proficiency and reduce the strength requirement for armor you wear by 1.
- **Con Artist.** You excel at adopting various disguises and personas to win trust. You have advantage on all checks made to convince others that you are a long lost friend, comrade, or member of their organization.
- **Pirate.** You gain an additional weapon proficiency and a swim speed. You know how to navigate by the constellations.
- **Scout.** You gain an additional weapon proficiency and proficiency with the Nature and Survival skills. If you are already proficient with a skill gained from this feature, you gain expertise with it. You also know how to navigate by the constellations.
- **Thief.** You gain a bonus to Stealth checks equal to your Intelligence modifier. If you are hidden and do anything on your turn to reveal yourself, you can try to hide again that turn. Normally when you reveal yourself you cannot try to hide again.

## Level 1: Sneak Attack

Once per turn as an attack action, you can make a special attack with a ranged or melee weapon called a sneak attack. You can also choose to use two-weapon fighting as part of this action.

If you have advantage on the attack roll, or if at least one friendly creature is in melee range of the target, you can deal additional damage based on your rogue level per the Sneak Attack column on the Rogue Features table. The extra damage's type is the same as the weapon's type.

If neither of those conditions apply, you gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to your level.

If the attack misses, you can apply the benefit of sneak attack to another attack you make this turn.

## Level 2: Cunning Action

Your quick thinking and agility allow you to move and act quickly. On your turn, you gain a second action called a cunning action. You can use your cunning action to take a dash or hide action.

## Level 3: Guile

In the back of your mind, you always have a plan. No matter the situation, you have just the right tool or stratagem for the job. You gain a pool of d20s called guile dice. You start with two dice and gain additional dice at levels 6 and 8.

After finishing a long rest, or at the start of an adventure, you roll each guile die. Record the results of each die. Whenever you make a test, instead of rolling you can use one of those d20 results. When a creature attacks you or attempts a Wisdom check to find you or discern a lie you told, you can replace its d20 roll with one of your guile dice.

You regain all expended dice and reroll ones that you did not expend, recording their new results, when you finish a long rest.

**Sneak Attack.** If you use guile to replace the roll of an attack made using Sneak Attack, you damage the target as if you had advantage on the attack, thus triggering the bonus damage.

## Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

## Level 4: Roguish Archetype

You select an additional Roguish Archetype.

## Level 5: Flashing Blade

When you use your sneak attack, you may make two attacks. Only one attack gains the additional damage from sneak attack.

## Level 6: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

## Level 6: Roguish Archetype

You select an additional Roguish Archetype.

## Level 7: Expanded Skills

Pick two skills you do not have expertise with. If you pick a skill you are not proficient with, you gain proficiency with it. If you pick a skill you are proficient with, you gain expertise with it.

## Level 8: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

## Level 9: Superior Reflexes

You gain expertise with Dexterity saves, allowing you to add your proficiency bonus to Dexterity saves twice. If you already have expertise with them, you gain expertise with a saving throw of your choice.

## Level 10: Rogue's Action

Once per turn, you can expend a guile die to take an extra action that is not a magic action or an attack action, using the die's result as the d20 roll for any checks made as part of the action. Alternatively, when you use Sneak Attack you can make one additional attack but must use the die as that attack's d20 roll.
