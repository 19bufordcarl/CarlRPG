# Wizard

As a wizard, you take a generalist approach to magic. Rather than focusing on one specific niche of magic, you dabble in harnessing various effects, learning spells as they suit your needs.

## Core Wizard Traits

| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hit Points**                 | 8 at 1st level, 6 per level gained after 1st                                             |
| **Saving Throw Proficiencies** | Intelligence, Wisdom                                                                     |
| **Skill Proficiencies**        | Choose any 2 skills. If you choose a skill you already have, you gain expertise with it. |
| **Weapon Proficiencies**       | Choose 1: Blade, Blunt, Bow, Mechanical, Polearm, and Shield                             |

As a wizard, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Wizard Features table.

### Level 1: Spellcasting

As a student of arcane magic, you have learned to cast spells.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Intelligence is the spellcasting ability for wizard spells. If you make an attack roll with a spell, your bonus is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The saving throw DC of your spells is 8 + your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus.

**Caster Level.** When you cast a spell, its effects might be determined by your level. A spell's level must be less than or equal to your level. Many spells have effects that scale, such as damage, based on your level.

**Spells Known.** You start with three spells known. Pick or randomly select three spells other than cantrips. When you gain a level, you learn an additional spell from the wizard spell list. Some class features grant you additional spells known.

**Spells Prepared.** You start with two spells prepared. You prepare spells from among your spells known. You gain additional prepared spells at levels 2, 5, and 8.

**Casting Spells.** When you cast a spell you have prepared, you can't cast it again until you recharge that spell. You can recharge one spell when you finish a short rest. You recharge all your spells when you finish a long rest.

**Swapping Prepared Spells.** When you recharge a spell, you can instead choose to prepare a different spell in its place from among those you gained from this class or from wizard spells in spellbooks or scrolls in your possession.

**Cantrips.** You know two wizard cantrips of your choice. Pick two from the table given later in this section or roll to pick at random, rerolling any duplicates. Unlike other spells, you retain the use of a cantrip after you cast it. You do not have to recharge it. Each time you gain a level, you can swap one or more of your cantrips for different ones. You gain a third cantrip at level 5.

### Level 2: School Specialization

As a wizard, you study and master the theories and mathematics behind the forces used to create spell effects. As part of your studies, you unlock deeper secrets. Choose a school specialization. You gain its level 2 benefit.

At levels 4 and 6 you gain additional benefits from your chosen specialization.

### Level 3: Sculpt Spells

When you cast a spell, you can choose to reduce the damage it does to creatures of your choice to 0.

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

### Level 4: School Specialization

You gain your school specialization's level 4 benefit.

### Level 5: School Mastery

Choose a school of magic. You gain a +1 bonus to your attacks with spells from that school and your save DC for those spells gains a +1 bonus.

### Level 6: School Specialization

You gain your school specialization's level 6 benefit.

### Level 7: Spell Research

Choose one of your spells that is not a cantrip, subject to restrictions noted below. Your research allows you to customize the spell, making it truly your own. Pick one of the following benefits. You can choose to apply it to that spell when you cast it.

- **Echo Spell.** You may choose a spell with a level less than or equal to half your level. When you cast this spell, you may cast the spell twice, but a creature cannot be targeted by the spell more than once. If a creature would suffer the spell's effects more than once, the effects of only one casting apply to it. You treat your caster level as half your level when casting the spell.
- **Elemental Infusion.** If the spell deals damage, you may change that damage's type to one of the following options: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. When you do so, you gain a bonus to damage rolls with that spell equal to your power during the turn on which you cast it.
- **Temporal Weave.** You may cast the spell without using your action. Your caster level is reduced to 1 when casting the spell in this way. You may apply this benefit only to 1st level spells. You cannot use this feature while you are prevented from using magic actions.

You choose a second spell to gain this benefit at level 9.

### Level 8: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to two abilities of your choice.

### Level 10: Spell Mastery

When you cast a 1st-level spell, you can choose to cast it as if your level was 1. If you do so, you can cast the spell again without needing to recharge it.

## School Specialization

You choose a technique of magic that reflects your focused area of study.

### Abjurer

You study magic that protects you and wards away threats.

**Level 1.** You gain the arcane shield cantrip. You gain it in addition to the cantrips you normally gain from the wizard class and always have it prepared.

**Level 4.** You gain arcane shield's level 4 benefit.

**Level 6.** You gain arcane shield's level 6 benefit.

### Arcane Archer

You study how to suffuse arrows with powerful magic.

**Level 1.** You gain proficiency with bows. As an attack action, you can make an attack with a bow that gains a bonus to its damage roll equal to your spellcasting modifier.

**Level 4.** When you attack with a bow, you can change its damage type to your choice of acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder.

**Level 6.** As a magic attack action, you can make a bow attack and cast a wizard cantrip, or you can make two bow attacks.

### Conjurer

You study magic that creates objects and summons creatures from the planes.

**Level 1.** You gain the conjure atronach cantrip. You gain it in addition to the cantrips you normally gain from the wizard class and always have it prepared.

**Level 4.** You gain conjure elemental's level 4 benefit.

**Level 6.** You gain conjure elemental's level 6 benefit.

### Evoker

You study magic that unleashes powerful, destructive forces.

**Level 1.** You gain the spell storm cantrip. You gain it in addition to the cantrips you normally gain from the wizard class and always have it prepared.

**Level 4.** You gain spell storm's level 4 benefit.

**Level 6.** You gain spell storm's level 6 benefit.

### Restorer

You study restoration magic, the powers that sustains and fortifies others.

**Level 1.** As a magic action, you can exhaust a prepared spell and cause a creature within 60 feet to regain 1d8 hit points per level + your spellcasting modifier.

**Level 4.** When you cast a wizard spell that is not a cantrip, a creature of your choice within melee range regains hit points equal to your level + your spellcasting modifier.

**Level 6.** Your level 4 benefit can now apply to up to three creatures within melee range.

### Transmuter

You study magic that allows you to alter living things and inert objects.

**Level 1.** You gain the modify self cantrip. You gain it in addition to the cantrips you normally gain from the wizard class and always have it prepared.

**Level 4.** You gain modify self's level 4 benefit.

**Level 6.** You gain modify self's level 6 benefit.
