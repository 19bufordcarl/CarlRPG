# Classes

## Character Class

Each character class has the following key elements.

### Hit Points

Your class determines the die you roll for hit points. You add your Constitution modifier to the roll. At level 1, you start with the maximum hit points you could roll. When you gain a level, you can roll or treat the die roll as half the maximum you could roll.

### Armor and Weapons

Your class lists the weapons you can use. If you attack with a weapon listed in your class, you gain a +2 bonus to the attack roll. This bonus increases to +3 at level 7.

### Armor

Your class lists the armor and shields you can use. While you wear armor or use a shield that is not on your class's list, you have disadvantage on all checks and saving throws.

### Starting Equipment

The class includes a starting gear bundle. You can swap items as you wish. You can exchange your armor for any cheaper set of armor. You can exchange your weapons for any other nonmagical weapons of the same category (martial, simple).

### Abilities

The class lists bonuses to your abilities and a list of default, starting ability modifiers. If you use the default ability array, it already includes the class's modifiers.

## Assassin

**Core Assassin Traits**

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit Points | 1d4 |
| Weapons | Simple, martial |
| Armor | Light |
| Equipment | Leather armor, longsword, light shield, poisoner's kit |
| Abilities | Dex +1 |

### Starting Abilities

Instead of rolling abilities, you can use this starting array:

- **Strength:** +2
- **Dexterity:** +2
- **Constitution:** +1
- **Intelligence:** +2
- **Wisdom:** –1
- **Charisma:** +0

### Level 1: Skills

At level 1 you gain 10 points you can allocate to your skills. You gain a +1 bonus in a skill per point spent on it. You can spend points on Climb Walls, Disguise, Find/Remove Traps, Hear Noise, Hide in Shadows, Move Silently, Open Locks, and Tracking.

Each time you gain a level, you gain 3 more points to spend on skills. You cannot increase a skill above +8.

### Level 1: Poison

If you have a poisoner's kit, as an action you can mix a poison and apply it to a weapon (or an arrow or similar ammunition) or a drink. The drink remains poisoned for 10 minutes. The weapon remains poisoned for 2 rounds.

If the poison is ingested or the weapon hits a creature, the target must make a Constitution save opposed by your Intelligence check. If the save fails, the target takes damage based on your level.

| Level | Poison Damage |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | 1d10 |
| 3–4 | 2d10 |
| 5–6 | 3d10 |
| 7–8 | 4d10 |
| 9–10 | 5d10 |

### Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

### Level 3: Ambusher

You have advantage on initiative rolls. If you surprise your enemies, you can take an extra action on your turn.

### Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

### Level 9: Deadly Venom

Your poisons are incredibly deadly. You can spend 1 minute to apply a poison rather than an action. If you do so, the poison gains one of the following benefits:

- Double its damage dice.
- If the target fails its saving throw, it is paralyzed. Repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the paralysis if it succeeds.

## Paladin

**Core Paladin Traits**

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit Points | 1d6 |
| Weapons | Simple, martial |
| Armor | Light, heavy |
| Equipment | Longsword, shield, ringmail, dagger |
| Abilities | Str +1 |

### Starting Abilities

Instead of rolling abilities, you can use this starting array:

- **Strength:** +2
- **Dexterity:** +1
- **Constitution:** +2
- **Intelligence:** –1
- **Wisdom:** +0
- **Charisma:** +2

### Level 1: Detect Alignment

As an action, you can focus your mind and determine if a creature or object within 30 feet of you is lawful or chaotic.

### Level 1: Divine Aura

You exert an aura of divine power. At the end of your turn, if a hostile creature is within 30 feet of you, creatures of your choice in that range regain hit points equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

### Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to Strength and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 3: Turn Undead

As an action, you present your holy symbol. All undead within 30 feet of you must make Wisdom saves opposed by your Charisma check. If they fail, they are frightened of you for 1 minute or until they take damage. If they succeed, they are immune to your use of this feature for the next 24 hours.

### Level 5: Two Attacks

When you attack, you can make two weapon attacks.

### Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to Strength and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 7: Protective Aura

Your divine aura grows stronger. You and friendly creatures within 30 feet of you gain a bonus to all saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1).

### Level 9: Divine Smite

Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack, you can smite it to increase your damage by 3d10.

## Ranger

**Core Ranger Traits**

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit Points | 1d6 |
| Weapons | Simple and martial weapons |
| Armor | Light |
| Equipment | Longsword, leather armor, dagger, short bow, 20 arrows |
| Abilities | Str +1 |

### Starting Abilities

Instead of rolling abilities, you can use this starting array:

- **Strength:** +2
- **Dexterity:** +2
- **Constitution:** +2
- **Intelligence:** +0
- **Wisdom:** +1
- **Charisma:** –1

### Level 1: Rapid Strike

You can sacrifice power for rapid strikes.

**Dual Wield.** You can attack twice as an action, once with two different weapons you wield, but you do not add your ability modifier to your damage roll.

**Quick Shot.** You can make two ranged attacks as an action, but you do not add your ability modifier to your damage roll.

### Level 1: Skills

At level 1 you gain 10 points you can allocate to your skills. You can allocate points to Climb Walls, Hear Noise, Hide in Shadows, Move Silently, and Tracking.

Each time you gain a level, you gain 3 more points to spend on skills. You cannot increase a skill above +8.

### Level 3: Superior Strike

You can make one extra attack when you use rapid strike.

### Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

### Level 5: Whirling Strikes

When you use rapid strike to make ranged attacks, you can make four attacks.

When you use dual wield, you can attack twice with each weapon.

### Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to two different abilities of your choice.

### Level 9: Mighty Strikes

You can now add your ability modifier to damage rolls for attacks made with your Rapid Strike feature.

## Thief

**Core Thief Traits**

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit Points | 1d4 |
| Weapons | Simple |
| Armor | Light |
| Equipment | Leather armor, dagger, thieves' tools |
| Abilities | Dex +1 |

### Starting Abilities

Instead of rolling abilities, you can use this starting array:

- **Strength:** +0
- **Dexterity:** +3
- **Constitution:** +1
- **Intelligence:** +1
- **Wisdom:** –1
- **Charisma:** +2

### Level 1: Skills

At level 1 you gain 20 points you can allocate to skills. You can allocate points to any skill.

Each time you gain a level, you gain 4 more points to spend on skills. You cannot increase a skill above +8.

### Level 1: Sneak Attack

You deal 1d6 extra damage with weapons if you have advantage on your attack roll or if the target is in melee range of a creature friendly to you.

This damage increases to 2d6 at level 3, 3d6 at level 5, 4d6 at level 7, and 5d6 at level 9.

### Level 3: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 3: Cunning Action

Once on your turn, you can dash, disengage, hide, or attack once with a weapon in your off hand without using your action.

### Level 7: Ability Bonus

You gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 9: Uncanny Dodge

You halve the damage you take from a single attack or effect. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until your next turn.
