# Magic

## Magic

The following rules apply to characters using magic.

### Spell Slots

To cast a spell other than a cantrip, you must expend a spell slot of a level greater than or equal to the spell's level. Cantrips do not require spell slots.

### Casting in Armor

You must have training with any armor you are wearing to cast spells while wearing it. You are otherwise too hampered by the armor for spellcasting.

### Spellcasting Abilities

The spellcasting ability for wizard spells is Intelligence, and the one for cleric spells is Wisdom.

To cast a spell other than a cantrip, you must succeed a

check with your spellcasting ability. If the check fails, you do not cast the spell but do not expend a spell slot.

#### Spell Attacks

Some spells require attacks. Use spellcasting ability to modify such a spell's attack and damage rolls. You also add a +2 bonus to attack rolls, or +3 at level 7 and higher, to reflect the training provided by your class.

### Concentration

Some spells and other effects require concentration to remain active, as specified in their descriptions. If you lose concentration, the effect ends. You can maintain concentration for up to 10 minutes.

Another Concentration Effect. You lose concentration on an effect the moment you start casting a

spell that requires concentration or activate another effect that requires concentration.

### Targets

A typical spell requires the caster to pick one or more targets to be affected by the spell's magic. A spell's description says whether the spell targets creatures, objects, a point of origin for an area of effect, or something else.

Valid Target. To target something with a spell, you must have a clear path to it and it cannot be hidden from you.

Targeting Yourself. If a spell targets a creature of your choice, you can choose yourself unless it must be specifically a creature other than you.

Targeting Areas. Some spells cover an area. As long as you can target any part of an area, you can use a spell that affects it.

### Combining Spell Effects

The effects of different spells add together while their durations overlap. The effects of the same spell cast multiple times don't combine. If the multiple castings each have a different effect, use the most powerful version of that effect. For instance, a spell might give a random bonus to AC. Use the highest bonus from among the castings of the spell.
