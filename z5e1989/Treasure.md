## Treasure

Ruins & Reptiles characters have relatively bare advancement tables. You can compensate for that by using these treasure rules to give characters magic items.

### Magic Armor

This section details the core magic armor properties.

Flexible. This armor is light and flexible enough that you gain your Dexterity modifier to AC while wearing it, despite heavy armor not normally allowing that.

Fortified. If you suffer a critical hit, roll a d20. On a 16 or higher, it instead becomes a normal hit.

Hidden. This armor is small and light enough that it can be worn under clothing without being noticed.

Shadowy. You can attempt to hide from any creature that is two or more areas away from you.

Stout. The armor’s base AC increases by 1. A suit of armor can have this trait up to three times, increasing its AC by 3.

Warded. You gain an additional +2 AC against ranged attacks.

### Magic Shields

This section details the core magic shield properties.

Alert. If you are surprised, roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, you are not surprised.

Blocking. You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity saving throws.

Guardian. You gain a +2 bonus to AC against opportunity attacks. This benefit can stack up to three times.

Thundering. Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can also push them horizontally in a straight line up to 10 feet.

Vigilant. You gain advantage on initiative rolls.

Warded. You gain an additional +2 AC against ranged attacks.

### Magic Weapons

This section details the different magic weapon properties.

Accurate. You gain a +1 to +3 bonus to attacks made with this weapon.

Brutal. If you miss with an attack with this weapon, the target takes force damage equal to your Strength modifier.

Defensive. You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wielding this weapon.

Returning. When used to make a ranged attack, this weapon returns to its attacker.

### Potions

Potions allow you to prepare for a variety of situations, if you have the money to stock up on them. They are available for purchase, with the variety on offer based on the talents of a town or city’s alchemists.


#### Basic Rules

Potions have a few special rules that make them distinct from other items.

Usage. Unless a potion specifies otherwise, using one is an action.

Single Use. Potions are single use magic items. You must drink a potion to gain its benefits.

Duration. A potion has a duration listed in rounds or minutes. If the potion has a duration of instantaneous, it applies its effects immediately but those

benefits remain. For example, a potion that restores hit points has an instantaneous duration. You regain the hit points, then the magic dissipates.

Limit. When you drink a potion, the duration of other potions currently affecting you comes to an end. Remember that instantaneous potions apply their effects, then their magic ends, so you retain any benefits from such potions.

#### Antitoxin

Rarity: Common

Duration: 1 hour

This potion looks like a light green jelly that oozes in its vial.

You gain resistance to poison damage.


#### Chromatic Dye

Rarity: Uncommon

Duration: 1 hour

This vial is filled with what looks like a swirling rainbow of liquids that represent every color.

This potion alters the color of your body and any gear you wear or carry, mirroring the colors around you. You can attempt to hide from any creature that is 30 or more feet away from you. Once hidden, you can move closer to them while staying out of sight.


#### Energy Draught

Rarity: Uncommon

Duration: Instantaneous

This potion bubbles and hisses in its container. When

you hold it, the hair on your arms stands on end.

If you can cast spells, you recharge one spell slot you have expended. The potion strains your mind and soul as the energy rushes into you, giving you a failed death save.


#### Envoy’s Elixir

Rarity: Common

Duration: 1 hour

This draught smells like sweet wine. When disturbed, such as by sloshing it around in its vial, a faint cacophony of voices in a variety of languages bubbles out of it.

This potion grants you the ability to understand all spoken languages for the duration of its effect. However, it gives you no special ability to speak a language you hear.


#### Feather Fall Draught

Rarity: Common

Duration: 10 minutes

This potion floats an inch off the ground. In your hand, it is as light as a feather. The liquid within it looks more like a swirling gas than a fluid.

While you gain this potion’s benefit, you take no damage from falling.


#### Giant’s Draught

Rarity: Common

Duration: 10 minutes

This potion is a light brown liquid, but when you open the vial the liquid flows out and expands in volume at an alarming rate.

This potion increases your size by one category, with a Medium creature becoming Large. You gain superior physical power at the cost of coordination. You have

advantage on all Strength- based tests but disadvantage on Dexterity-based ones. You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with weapon attacks. Attacks against you have advantage.


#### Goliath Draught

Rarity: Common

Duration: 1 hour

This potion smells like clean, fresh air and has an earthy taste.

You gain a climb speed.


#### Healing Potion

Rarity: Common

Duration: Instantaneous

This potion is transparent, with an orange tinge. It has a sickly, citrus taste that is unpleasant but tolerable.

When you drink this potion, you regain hit points equal to half your maximum. The strain of this rapid healing pushes your body beyond its limits, giving you a failed death save. You can avoid this drawback by spending 10 minutes sipping the potion. During this time you can do nothing else other than move and nurse the potion. If you are interrupted the potion is wasted and has no effect.


#### Hero’s Brew

Rarity: Common

Duration: 1 minute

This potion smells like exceptionally strong alcohol.

You gain a +2 bonus to all attacks, checks, and saves as this potion hones your body and mind into perfect harmony. However, the strain of this brew overwhelms you. When this potion’s duration ends, you gain a failed death save.


#### Iron Skin Flask

Rarity: Common

Duration: 1 hour

This potion feels heavy in your hand. Its liquid is a thick, viscous sludge.

When you drink this potion, you gain a new way to determine your AC. You can treat your AC as if you wore armor that gave your AC 15 and did not apply an ability modifier to AC.


#### Miner’s Draught

Rarity: Common

Duration: 12 hours

This potion looks like a vial of black ink.

You can see in the dark to a distance of up to 30 feet, but you are blinded while in areas of dim or bright light. Note that you are blinded only while in such an area. You suffer no effects, and can see into, lit areas at range.


#### Phantom Brew

Rarity: Uncommon

Duration: 10 minutes

This looks like an empty jar, but when you shake it you feel a liquid sloshing around inside it.

You turn invisible until you attack, cast a spell, or do anything that causes a creature to take damage.


#### Potion of Speed

Rarity: Uncommon

Duration: 1 minute

This potion bounces around in your hand, shuddering from side to side as if something inside is moving about.

You gain a +30 foot bonus to speed. However, this potion functions by pushing your body beyond its limit. When this potion’s duration ends, make a Constitution save or take a failed death save.


#### Potion of the Deep

Rarity: Common

Duration: 12 hours

This potion smells of the sea. It looks like a vial of ocean water that swirls in its container, forming miniature, foamy waves.

You gain the ability to breathe water in addition to your other modes of breathing.


#### Shapeshifter’s Brew

Rarity: Uncommon

Duration: 1 hour

The substance in this vial looks like a fleshy sludge that sometimes spawns a tiny eyeball that stares back at you.

You adopt the physical appearance of a creature of your size and same general body shape. For instance, if you are a humanoid you can adopt the appearance of a creature that stands upright and has two arms, two legs, and a head. You gear does not change. Your physical appearance is an exact duplicate.

You can attempt to duplicate a specific creature, but anyone familiar with that creature can detect your use with an Intelligence check.


#### Thief’s Passport

Rarity: Common

Duration: 10 minutes

This potion is a small bead of orange liquid that has settled at the bottom of its vial.

This potion reduces your size to tiny, allowing you to fit into passages or openings as narrow as 3 inches across. You can attempt to hide from creatures larger than you regardless of the environment.

### Scrolls

A scroll bears the words of a single spell, written in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list, you can read the scroll and cast its spell. Otherwise, the scroll is unintelligible. Casting the spell by reading the scroll requires the spell’s normal casting time. Once the spell is cast, the scroll crumbles to dust.

If the spell is on your spell list but of a higher level than you can normally cast, you make an ability check using your spellcasting ability to determine whether you cast the spell. On a failed check, the spell disappears from the scroll with no other effect.

Use your casting ability to determine the spell’s attack bonus.


#### Copying a Scroll into a Spellbook

With 8 hours of work, a wizard can add a wizard spell from a scroll to their spellbook. Doing so destroys the scroll.

#### Randomly Generating a Scroll

Determine the spell’s class at random based on classes in your campaign world. If the scroll is uncommon, roll a d6 again to determine its level. It is level 2 on a 1 – 3 and level 3 on a 4 – 6. Roll on the appropriate class and level table to determine the specific spell.


### Wands

Wands contain arcane power that a magic-user can tap into. Each wand specifies a spell. You must have the spell on your class’s list to use the wand.

When using a wand, use your spellcasting ability for the wand’s required spell determine its effects and attack bonus.

#### Very Rare Wands

#### Wand of Detection

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Detect Magic

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can point the wand in a space up to 300 feet away. You hear noises as if you stood at that point.

As an action, you can cast detect magic.


#### Wand of Flames

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Fireball

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can cause a flame equal to that of a torch to sprout from its end. It casts dim light in a 30-foot radius and can be used to ignite flammable material.

As an action, you can cast a reduced strength fireball. The spell’s damage is 4d6. If any of the damage dice roll a 1, the wand overheats and none of its features can be used for 10 minutes.


#### Wand of Glamours

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Disguise Self

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can cast disguise self.

As an action, you can create an illusion that fills an area up to 20 feet on each side. The illusion can include sounds and odors, but it fails to hold up to physical inspection. It lasts for up to 1 hour or until you use this feature of the wand again.


#### Wand of Lightning

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Lightning Bolt

This wand has two uses.

As an action, you can fire a single bolt of electricity from the wand. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within

300 feet. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 damage, or 4d6 damage if you are level 5 or higher.

As an action, you can cast a reduced strength lightning bolt. The spell’s damage is 4d6 and can target one creature. If any of the damage dice roll a 1, the wand generates a backlash of energy, leaving it unusable for 10 minutes.


#### Wand of Protection

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Mage Armor

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you gain the benefits of using a shield (+2 AC).

As an action, you can cast mage armor.


#### Wand of Terror

Rarity: Very rare

Required Spell: Fear

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you can alter your voice, granting you a ferocious growl that confers a +2 bonus to all Intimidation checks.

As an action, you can point the wand at a creature within 300 feet and force it to make a Wisdom save. If it fails, the creature is frightened of you. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.


#### Wand of Unerring Darts

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Magic Missile

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you gain a +1 bonus to all spell attack rolls.

The wand has a reservoir of 5 charges. As an action, you can cast magic missile.

Expend charges from the wand to create a spell slot used to cast the spell. The wand regains 1 charge per hour.


#### Wand of Vigilance

Rarity: Rare

Required Spell: Alarm

This wand has two uses.

While holding the wand, you add your Intelligence modifier as a bonus to initiative rolls.

As an action, you can cast alarm.
