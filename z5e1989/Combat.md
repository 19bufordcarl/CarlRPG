# Combat

## Combat

When a fight breaks out, the rules keep the action organized and easy to follow.


### Rounds

Each round is enough time for everyone involved in combat to take a turn. A round lasts about 6 seconds.


### Initiative

Initiative determines the order in which everyone acts during an encounter.


#### Rolling Initiative

At the start of combat, you make a Dexterity check and record the result as your initiative. During the action phase of each round, creatures act in order of initiative. The creature with the highest initiative goes first, then the one with the next highest, until everyone has taken a turn.


#### Surprise

You might be surprised at the start of combat. You roll initiative as normal. On the first round, the creatures that were not surprised act in initiative order. Then, the creatures that were surprised act in initiative order.


#### Tied Initiative

If initiative is tied, the players decide the order in which the tied creatures act. Once they decide, use that order for the rest of the battle.


### Your Turn

On your turn you can do each of the following once in any order:

- Move • Take an action


### Durations

At the start of your turn, reduce the duration of your ongoing effects you created

by 1 round. If this reduces an effect’s remaining duration to 0, it ends


### Size

Size determines how much space a creature takes up. If a creature somehow is in a space that it cannot fit into, it has disadvantage on all attacks, checks, and saves and moves at half speed.

### Movement

You can move once during your turn. You can use your action to move, allowing you to move twice.


#### Opportunity Attacks

If your movement causes you to leave the reach of a hostile creature, it can make a melee attack against you as an opportunity attack. A creature can make an opportunity attack once per turn.


#### Movement Modes

Every creature can walk with ease and can attempt to climb, swim, or jump.

Climb. If you have a climb speed, you can automatically climb areas. Otherwise, you must make a check with the Climb Walls skill. If you fail, you cannot climb this turn.

Jump. Make a Strength check. The result is the distance in feet you can jump.

Swim. If you have a swim speed, you can automatically climb areas. Otherwise, you must make a Strength check. If you fail, you cannot climb this turn.


#### Advanced Movement Modes

Only creatures with a special ability can use these forms of movement.

Burrow. You can burrow through loose soil, sand, and other material, but not solid objects.

Climb. This creature can climb without making Strength (Athletics) checks.

Fly. A flying creature can move vertically and horizontally.

Spider Climb. A creature with this movement mode can climb horizontally across surfaces such as ceilings.


#### Difficult Terrain

Some areas are marked as difficult terrain. You move at half speed in difficult terrain.

### Free Actions

A free action is a simple action, something you can do in a moment in addition to the main action you use during your turn.

A free action is any action that does not require a check or any other die roll.

You can use one free action on your turn.


### Attacks

Most attacks require an action.


#### Melee Attack

Melee attacks are made against targets in melee range, the distance you can physically reach.


#### Ranged Attack

Ranged attacks are made against targets within your attack’s range, usually 300 or 30 feet.


#### Target

You can target something with an attack if the following three points are true:

The target must be in range.

A clear, unblocked path large enough to accommodate the attack extends from you to the potential target.

The target is not hidden from you.

If you do not meet one of those requirements, you cannot target that creature or object.


#### If the Target Can’t See You

If the target can’t see you but you can see it, such as if you are hidden, your attack has advantage.


#### Rolling an Attack

An attack is a d20 roll + your Strength for melee attacks or Dexterity for ranged ones.


#### Armor Class

The target’s armor class (AC) is the target number for the attack roll. If more than one AC applies to a target, such as if it has cover or you cannot see it, use the highest one unless the target decides otherwise.

Determining Armor Class. Your AC is 10 + your Dexterity modifier unless you wear armor or have a feature that alters AC.

AC Modifiers. Some elements, such as shields, give a bonus to your AC.

Targets You Can’t See. If you can’t see the target but it is not hidden from you, the target uses the higher of AC 20 or its own AC against the attack.

Cover. If the target has cover it uses the higher of AC 20 or

its own AC. A creature has cover at the DM’s discretion.


#### Attack Result

If the result of your attack roll is greater than or equal to the target’s AC, the attack hits. Roll damage and subtract the result from the target’s hit points. Otherwise, the attack misses.

Critical Hits. If the die roll is a 20 without modifiers, the attack is a critical hit. On a critical hit, roll your damage twice (all dice and modifiers) and add the results together.


### Hide

You can try to evade detection by moving in the shadows and keeping quiet.


#### Requirements

You can hide if you meet at least one of the following requirements:

- Sight: A creature is unable to see you, such as if you are behind a wall, invisible, or in darkness. • Cover: If a creature could see you, you have cover against it. • Inattention: If a creature could see you, the DM rules it is not looking where you are hiding.

You must also meet this requirement for keeping quiet:

- Sound: You avoid making noise louder than a whisper.


#### Check

You make a Move Silently check. If you succeed, you are hidden.


#### Hidden

While you are hidden, you cannot be targeted and creatures do not react to your presence.


#### Becoming Revealed

If you attack, cast a spell, or otherwise target a hostile creature, you are no longer hidden. You are also no longer hidden if you fail to meet the requirements for hiding. You might also be revealed if a creature finds you.

On Another Creature’s Turn. If you are revealed during another creature’s turn, you are immediately no longer hidden from it and anyone it alerts to your presence.

On Your Turn. If you are revealed during your own turn, you can’t use hide for the rest of the turn and no longer gain the benefits of being hidden at the end of your turn. You still gain the benefits during your turn, as your foes struggle to react.


#### Hide in Shadows

The Hide in Shadows skill is a special case. If you are

already hidden, you can use Hide in Shadows to attempt to move into an area of dim light while remaining hidden.

The area must be dimly lit, but you do not need cover and a creature might otherwise be able to see you in that area.

If you succeed, you remain hidden in dim light. If you fail, you are spotted.


### Influence

You urge a creature to do something. Describe or roleplay how you’re communicating with the monster. Are you trying to deceive, intimidate, amuse, or gently persuade? The DM then determines what happens next.


#### Charisma

Most influence attempts use Charisma, but the DM can determine that a different ability applies based on your describe the attempt.


#### Traits

Traits determine how a creature reacts to your influence attempt. They are a creature’s core beliefs and desires. Traits have a modifier, like abilities, that ranges from +2 up to +8. Your check must be greater than or equal 10 + the relevant trait to succeed.

Willing. If your urging aligns with the monster’s traits, no check is necessary. The monster fulfills your request as it sees fit.

Unwilling. If your urging is counter to its traits or goals, it doesn’t comply.

Hesitant. If you urge the monster to do something that its traits make it hesitant to do, you make a check opposed by the trait’s bonus.

On a successful check, the creature fulfills your request within the bounds of its personality and goals as determined by the DM.

Scope of Influence. During combat, you can get a creature to change its actions for the next round. It might move closer to or away from you, call a truce, or become distracted by food you offer it.

Influencing Multiple Creatures. You can always influence one creature but can attempt to influence up to a number equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Note that if you influence an enemy leader, their actions or decisions might sway all your enemies into taking a course of action.


### Search

You can try to evade detection by moving in the shadows and keeping quiet.

Roll a Hear Noise check. If you succeed, all hidden creatures within 30 feet of you must immediately make Move Silently or Hide in Shadows checks. Their result must be greater than or equal to your Hear Noise roll. If they fail, they are no longer hidden. Of they succeed, they are hidden but you know someone is hidden nearby.


### Other Actions

As an action, you can complete any activity that requires about six seconds of work.

Describe to the DM what you intend to do. They may ask for a check, might rule the action impossible, or may add some limitation to what you want to do.

The list below provides examples of what you can do. You can always describe what you want to do and the DM can pick a skill and ability to model it.


#### Use a Potion

You can draw a potion and consume it or administer it to a creature in melee range that is friendly or one unable to prevent you from pouring the brew into its mouth.

You make a Wisdom check to determine if you notice anything.


#### Study a Creature

You can study a creature to learn about its attitude or internal state with a Wisdom check. You can learn about its nature or capabilities with an Intelligence check.


#### Inspect an Object

You can inspect a single object or wall near you. You make an Intelligence check. If you succeed, you learn some additional details about the object or notice anything hidden within it, such as a secret door.


#### Stabilize a Creature

You provide medical assistance to a dying creature in melee range. Make an Intelligence check. If you succeed, that creature regains 1 hit point.


#### Scan an Area

You can scan a space that is up to 30 feet on each side.
