# Bestiary

This section includes monster stats.

## Monster Framework

### Level

Level reflects a monster's power.

### Initiative

Monsters do not roll initiative. A monster has a list of actions with an initiative value for each. When one of its initiatives comes up, it takes a turn and can use one of the actions listed for that initiative.

A creature takes a full turn each time its initiative comes up. It moves and acts.

Most creatures that can take two turns are elite or solo, but some standard creatures have this ability.

If a creature lists multiple actions on the same initiative, it can use one of those actions on its turn.

### Armor Class

A monster's AC is a set number, rather than a derived value based on ability modifiers, armor, and class features.

### Hit Points

A monster's hit points are defined by its level, overall toughness, and the nature of its actions and tactics. These monsters do not have hit dice.

### Tests

The monster lists a tests bonus it uses for checks and saves, along with attacks other than the ones that have an attack bonus in the stat block. Some monsters list ability-specific overrides (e.g., **Str +6**) that apply only to checks and saves using that ability.

### Speed

A monster's speed is 30 feet unless otherwise noted.

### Senses

A monster has darkvision with a range of 60 feet unless otherwise noted.

#### Attacks and Damage

As with hit points and AC, the attack bonus and damage for a creature are based on its level, skill, and relative power. Damage is based on the creature's lethality rather than a strict formula using the rules for weapons.

**Melee Attacks.** If an attack is listed as a melee attack, its range is its reach.

**Ranged Attacks.** If an attack is listed as a ranged attack, it has a range of 300 feet.

### Elites and Solos

Some monsters are labeled as **elite** or **solo**. Elite monsters are meant to take on two characters at once, while solo ones are meant to fight an entire party.

Their XP values assume they are outnumbered. Thus, an elite has the same XP as a normal monster but has more hit points and does more damage.

### Troopers and Minions

These creatures are weaker than normal for their level. Two troopers is a match for one PC, as are four minions.

### Random Monsters

Use these tables to generate random encounters. The type determines the number appearing:

- **Minions.** 1d8 per character.
- **Trooper.** 1d4 per character.
- **Standard.** 1d2 per character.
- **Elite.** 1 per 2 characters.
- **Solo.** 1 per 4 characters.
