# Character Creation

## Character Creation

Use the following steps to create a character.


### The Six Abilities

Strength measures physical might and power.

Dexterity measures agility, coordination, reflexes, and balance.

Constitution gauges health and durability.

Intelligence reflects logical reasoning, memory, and knowledge.

Wisdom measures intuition, insight, and willpower.

Charisma reflects personal magnetism and charm.


### Determine Abilities and Class

Roll to determine your abilities then pick a class.


#### Random Method

Roll six d6s and allocate each die to a different ability, choosing how to allocate them or doing it at random. Use the table below to determine their abilities. Then, pick a class and apply its listed changes to your ability modifiers.

d6 Ability Score 1 –1 2 +0 3 – 4 +1 5 – 6 +2


### Array Method

Assign +2 to two abilities, +2 to two abilities, +0 to one ability, and –1 to one ability. Then apply any modifiers for ancestry and class. Each class also has a predetermined array you can use. That array includes class modifiers to abilities.


### Ancestry

You can pick one of the ancestries listed below or roll on the provided table. Your size is Medium and your creature type is humanoid. You know the Common language.

d10 Result 1 Dragonborn 2 Dwarf 3 Elf 4 Halfling 5 Half-Elf 6 Half-Orc 7 – 9 Human 10 Tiefling


#### Dwarf

You have darkvision with a range of 30 feet. You know the Dwarvish language.

Indomitable. During your turn, you can tap into a well of energy and toughness. You regain 1d8 + your Constitution modifier hit points. This amount increases by 1d8 when you reach level 5 (2d8)

and 9 (3d8). Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.


#### Elf

You know the Elvish language.

Learned Expertise. Your long years of study and lived experience before becoming an adventurer serve you in good stead. Pick two skills. You gain a +2 bonus to them. You gain an additional +1 bonus with them at levels 5 and 9.


#### Gnome

You know the Gnomish language. You are size Small. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls against creatures larger than you, and a +2 bonus to AC against their attacks.


#### Halfling

Your size is Small. You know the Halfling language.

Halfling Luck. You have advantage on all saving throws.


#### Half-Elf

You know the Elvish language, You gain a +1 bonus to Intelligence or Dexterity.


#### Half-Orc

You know the Orc language. You gain a +1 bonus to Strength or Constitution.


#### Human

Increase one of your abilities by +1.

### Personality

Roll on the following table to determine your alignment, then flip to the Alignment and Convictions section to determine random convictions based on your alignment.

d6 Ethical Alignment 1 Evil 2 – 4 Good 5 – 6 Neutral


### Convictions

Your convictions reflect how your alignment shapes your beliefs. You have two. Each conviction has a rating of +2. After learning if you succeeded or failed at an attack, save, or check, you can choose to add a conviction’s rating to the roll, possibly turning failure into success. You regain one expended conviction when you finish a long rest.
