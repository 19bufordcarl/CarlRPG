# Rest & Conditions

## Rest and Recovery

During an adventure, you might have the chance to rest and recover your hit points and resources.


### Short Rest

A short rest is a 10-minute period of downtime. You can’t use actions during this time. If you do so, you must start the rest over.

Benefits of the Rest. When you finish the rest, you regain all lost hit points and the use of features that are restored with a short rest.


### Long Rest

A long rest is a period of downtime that lasts at least 8 hours.

Benefits of the Rest. When you finish the rest, you regain all lost hit points and the use of features that are restored with a short or long rest.

Death Saves. The conditions for your long rest determine how many failed death saves you remove. Make a Constitution check for each failed one. Each successful check removes a failed death save. The check might have advantage or disadvantage

- Disadvantage. Resting in areas infested with biting insects, strange noises, and other factors that make rest difficult. You also suffer poor conditions if you rest without the proper gear, such as bedrolls. • A normal check represents the typical conditions on an adventure or while camping outdoors. • Advantage applies in a comfortable inn, home, or other safe bastion of civilization.

Interrupting the Rest. A long rest is stopped if you take any damage or do anything more strenuous than read, tend to your camp, or eat.

If you rested at least 10 minutes before the interruption, you gain the benefits of a short rest.

You can resume a long rest immediately after an interruption. If you do so, the rest requires 1 additional hour per interruption to finish.

## Optional Rule: Wounds

The default rules err on the side of making heroes vulnerable via death saves but still allowing them to operate without restriction. The game emulates heroes who can take a beating, but you might want something grittier. The wounds system adds more bite to failed death saves.


### Taking a Wound

Each failed death save represents a serious injury, a wound that limits you in some significant way.

When you gain a failed death save, roll 1d6 and consult the table below. The injury applies a -2 penalty to your ability modifier until you remove that failed death save. That penalty increases to -4 if a second wound applies to the same ability.

## Conditions

Various spells and monster attacks imbue conditions on a character.


### Asleep

While asleep you can’t do anything on your turn. Your class features provide no benefit and your spells that require concentration end. You wake up if you take damage or if the DM judges that a sound or other event is enough to rouse you. Your AC is 5 while you are asleep and attacks that hit you are critical hits.


### Blinded

A blinded creature cannot see.


### Charmed

A charmed creature is friendly toward the creature that charmed it. It does not necessarily obey its orders

but cannot target it with attacks, offensive abilities, or anything else that harms or hinders it.


### Dazed

A dazed creature can either move or use its action on its turn, not both.


### Dying

A creature with 0 hit points is dying and must make death saves.


### Frightened

A frightened creature cannot willingly move closer to the creature or effect that frightened it.


### Goaded

A creature that is goaded has disadvantage on attacks against creatures that are not goading it.


### Grappled

A grappled creature cannot move further than 5 feet away from the creature or effect that is grappling it. In place of moving, a creature can make an opposed check using Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) to break free.


### Invisible

An invisible creature cannot be seen unless an effect specifically grants the ability to see invisible things.


### Paralyzed

While paralyzed you can’t do anything on your turn. Your AC is 5 while you are paralyzed and attacks that hit you are critical hits.


### Prone

While prone instead of moving you can stand up. You can choose to fall prone in place

of moving on your turn. Melee attacks against you have advantage.


### Unconscious

While unconscious you can’t do anything on your turn. Your class features cannot benefit other creatures and effects that require concentration end. Your AC is 5 while you are unconscious and attacks that hit you are critical hits.
