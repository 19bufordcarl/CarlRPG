# Advancement

## Leveling Up

You can track XP or use the alternate triumphs system for leveling.


### Experience Points

You gain experience points for defeating monsters and completing important goals. As you gain XP, your level increases.

Level Experience Points 1 0 2 150 3 300 4 600 5 900 6 1,800 7 2,700 8 4,600 9 6,500 10 10,000


#### Earning Experience Points

You earn experience points for overcoming challenges. If you defeat monsters in combat or overcome them through

interaction or clever play, you gain XP equal to their total XP value. Add up the monsters’ XP and divide it among the characters in the party.


#### Quests

You can also earn XP for completing a quest. The DM assigns a level to a quest to reflect its importance. If you complete it, each character gains the XP listed below. Do not divide it among the characters.

Level Quest Experience 1 75 2 100 3 150 4 200 5 300 6 400 7 450 8 500 9 800 10 1,000


### Triumphs

You level up by earning triumphs. You earn triumphs by defeating deadly encounters or by completing deeds.


#### Deadly Encounters

A deadly encounter is a battle built with the high XP budget.

If you defeat a deadly encounter, you earn a triumph. You defeat the creatures involved in a deadly encounter by foiling their aims, overcoming them in battle, or succeeding at your goals despite their interference. You might talk the monsters that guard a bridge into helping you, sneak past a dragon to steal its treasure, or kill the goblin king and his retinue.


#### Deeds

Deeds are notable, memorable, and heroic

actions that are the stuff of legend. If the folk at the local tavern talk about your exploits in healing the king’s wounds, banishing the demon lurking at the bottom of the local dungeon, or defeating the petulant dwarf queen in a drinking contest, then you have completed a deed.

Deeds are key events that shape the campaign world, making your characters more than just another gang of heavily armed, homeless wanderers. Your actions alter the course of history, gain the attention of kings and priests, and even draw the eyes of the gods.

The DM can declare the opportunity to complete a deed at any point in the game. This transparent declaration is a call to arms, a challenge for the players to lean into the action and seek out the chance to gain a triumph.

Deeds are usually the core goal of an adventure, such as a quest to defeat a necromancer or the chance to steal a massive emerald from the vaults of a shadowy cult.


#### Combining Deeds and Deadly Encounters

An especially important deadly encounter might also award a deed. If the PCs defeat the God of Swords in front of the entire city, they earn two triumphs, one for completing a deed and another for defeating a deadly encounter.


### Gaining Levels

For every five triumphs you earn, you gain a level. You gain the benefits of that level the next time you complete a long rest.
