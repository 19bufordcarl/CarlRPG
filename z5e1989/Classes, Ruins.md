# Classes

## Character Class

Each character class has the following key elements.

### Primary Ability

Each class has a core ability that defines its features. When in doubt, make this your highest ability.

### Hit Points

Your class determines your hit points. At level 1, you start with the maximum hit points you could roll. When you gain a level, you can roll or treat the die roll as half the maximum you could roll + 1.

### Proficiencies

Your class grants you saving throw, weapon, and armor proficiencies.

### Expertise

If you choose proficiency in a skill that you are already proficient with, you gain expertise with it instead.

### Starting Equipment

The class includes a starting gear bundle. You can swap items as you wish. You can exchange your armor for any cheaper set of armor. You can exchange your weapons for any other nonmagical weapons of the same category (martial, simple).

### Quick Start Abilities

Instead of rolling abilities, you can use the array provided by the character class.

### Level 1: Turn Undead

As an action, you present your holy symbol. All undead within 30 feet of you must make Wisdom saves (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier). You gain a +1 bonus to Wisdom and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

## Fighter

**Core Fighter Traits**

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Ability | Strength |
| Hit Points | 1d6 + Constitution modifier per level |
| Saving Throw Proficiencies | Strength, Constitution |
| Skill Proficiencies | Choose 2: Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion, Perception, Survival. |
| Weapon Proficiencies | Simple and martial weapons |
| Armor Proficiencies | Light, medium, and heavy armor, and shields |
| Starting Equipment | Longsword, shield, ringmail, dagger |
| Quick Start Abilities | Str +4, Int -1, Wis +0, Dex +2, Con +2, Cha +1 |

### Fighter Class Features

As a fighter, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Fighter Features table.

#### Fighter Features

| Level | Proficiency | Class Features |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +2 | Weapon Specialization |
| 2 | +2 | — |
| 3 | +2 | Specialist Attack |
| 4 | +2 | — |
| 5 | +2 | Two Attacks |
| 6 | +2 | — |
| 7 | +2 | Ability Score Improvement |
| 8 | +2 | — |
| 9 | +3 | Three Attacks |
| 10 | +3 | — |

### Level 1: Weapon Specialization

You gain a bonus to damage rolls with weapons equal to your proficiency bonus.

### Level 3: Specialist Attack

The first time you miss an attack on your turn, reroll the attack. If you have disadvantage or advantage, reroll one or both dice.

### Level 5: Two Attacks

When you attack, you can make two weapon attacks.

### Level 7: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to Strength and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 9: Three Attacks

When you attack, you can make three weapon attacks.

| Trait | Value |

| Hit Points | 1d4 + Constitution modifier per level |
| Saving Throw Proficiencies | Dexterity, Intelligence |
| Skill Proficiencies | Choose 4: Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth. |

| Armor Proficiencies | Light armor, small shield |
| Starting Equipment | Leather armor, dagger, thieves' tools |
| Quick Start Abilities | Str -1, Int +2, Wis +0, Dex +4, Con +2, Cha +1 |

### Thief Class Features

As a thief, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified levels. These features are listed on the Thief Features table.

#### Thief Features

| Level | Proficiency | Class Features |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +2 | Sneak Attack |
| 2 | +2 | — |
| 3 | +2 | Cunning Action |
| 4 | +2 | — |
| 5 | +2 | — |
| 6 | +2 | — |
| 7 | +2 | Ability Score Improvement |
| 8 | +2 | — |
| 9 | +3 | Uncanny Dodge |
| 10 | +3 | — |

### Level 1: Sneak Attack

You deal 1d6 extra damage with weapons if you have advantage on your attack roll or if the target is in melee range of a creature friendly to you.

This damage increases to 2d6 at level 3, 3d6 at level 5, and 4d6 at level 7. At level 9 the thief is a deadly assassin, their sneak attack jumping to 5d10.

### Level 3: Cunning Action

Once on your turn, you can dash, disengage, hide, or attack once with a light weapon in your off hand without using your action.

### Level 7: Ability Score Improvement

You gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity and a +1 bonus to a different ability of your choice.

### Level 9: Uncanny Dodge

You halve the damage you take from a single attack or effect. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until your next turn.
